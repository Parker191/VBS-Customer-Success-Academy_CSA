# 📈 Upsell Customer Playbook

**Document Type:** Playbook
**Category:** Growth & Expansion
**Owner:** Customer Success Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## When to Use This Playbook

Use this playbook when a customer's existing scope could genuinely be expanded to deliver more value — more seats, more services, or a higher tier of the same offering. This is distinct from Cross-sell (a different product/service line) and Referral (a new customer entirely).

**Only proceed if:**
- Health status is 🟢 Green (never upsell into a Yellow or Red account)
- The opportunity is driven by a genuine gap in the customer's current scope, not a quota target

---

## Step 1 — Identify the Gap

- Review the Customer Success Plan for goals the current scope doesn't fully address.
- Look for usage signals suggesting the customer has outgrown current scope (e.g. hitting limits, requesting features outside contract).

## Step 2 — Validate Internally

- Confirm with your manager that this is a genuine expansion opportunity, not a stretch.
- Check pricing/packaging options available for the upsell.

## Step 3 — Introduce Naturally

- Raise it in context — during a Business Review, tied directly to a goal or pain point the customer has already voiced.
- Avoid leading with price; lead with the outcome the expanded scope would unlock.

## Step 4 — Hand Off to Sales

- Loop in Sales with full context: what was discussed, the customer's stated need, and any urgency.
- Log the opportunity in CRM as a tracked upsell opportunity.

## Step 5 — Track Outcome

- Follow up on the result (won/lost/deferred).
- If lost, record why — this feeds future packaging and pricing decisions.

---

## Do

- Confirm 🟢 Green health before raising anything commercial.
- Tie the pitch to a need the customer already expressed, not a target VBS needs to hit.

## Don't

- Don't upsell into a Yellow or Red account — fix the relationship first.
- Don't negotiate pricing yourself without Sales/manager involvement.

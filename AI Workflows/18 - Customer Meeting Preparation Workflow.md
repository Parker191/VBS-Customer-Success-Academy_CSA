# 📅🧠 Customer Meeting Preparation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing for customer meetings.

The objective is to help the CSM enter every important customer meeting with sufficient context, clear objectives, relevant questions, known risks, and desired outcomes.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Meeting
      ↓
1. Confirm Meeting Details
      ↓
2. Review Customer Context
      ↓
3. Review Previous Meeting
      ↓
4. Review Open Actions
      ↓
5. Review Customer Health
      ↓
6. Review Current Projects
      ↓
7. Identify Meeting Objectives
      ↓
8. Prepare Questions
      ↓
9. Prepare Talking Points
      ↓
10. Prepare Risks and Opportunities
      ↓
11. Define Desired Outcomes
      ↓
12. Create Meeting Agenda
      ↓
13. Conduct Meeting
      ↓
14. Capture Actions
      ↓
15. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Confirm Meeting Details

Verify:

- Customer
- Meeting date
- Meeting time
- Meeting type
- Participants
- Customer participants
- Internal participants
- Meeting objective
- Previous meeting date

Possible meeting types:

- Kickoff
- WBR
- MBR
- QBR
- Check-in
- Project review
- Escalation meeting
- Renewal discussion
- Executive meeting
- Feedback meeting

Do not assume the meeting objective if it has not been confirmed.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Customer Context

Before the meeting, review:

- Customer services
- Current projects
- Customer goals
- Customer health
- Stakeholders
- Recent interactions
- Recent feedback
- Open issues
- Escalations
- Renewal status
- Expansion opportunities

Focus on information relevant to the meeting.

---

# 3️⃣ Review Previous Meeting

Review the previous meeting notes.

Identify:

- Decisions made
- Customer concerns
- Commitments
- Open questions
- Promised follow-ups
- Deadlines
- Customer requests

Create:

| Previous Action | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Status] |

Never claim an action was completed without evidence.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Open Actions

Check actions owned by:

### CSM

- Customer follow-ups
- Internal coordination
- Reports
- Meetings
- Updates

### Customer

- Requirements
- Approvals
- Feedback
- Documents
- Decisions

### Internal Teams

- Delivery
- Finance
- Technical
- Management
- KAM / Sales

Identify anything that could affect the upcoming meeting.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Customer Health

Check:

- Current health
- Previous health
- Health trend
- Engagement
- Sentiment
- Delivery
- Open risks

Identify:

🟢 Healthy

🟡 Needs Attention

🟠 At Risk

🔴 Critical

⚪ Unknown

If health has changed, identify the evidence behind the change.

---

- Customer feedback

# 7️⃣ Identify Meeting Objectives

Define what the CSM wants to accomplish.

Use:

[Objective]

### Objective 2

[Objective]

### Objective 3

[Objective]

Keep objectives focused.

Avoid creating too many objectives.

---

# 8️⃣ Prepare Questions

Prepare questions based on the customer's situation.

### Customer Experience

> How has the experience been since our last discussion?

### Delivery

> Are the current deliverables meeting your expectations?

### Future Needs

> What upcoming projects or priorities should we be preparing for?

### Improvement

> Is there anything you would like us to improve?

### Relationship

> Is there anything we can do to make working with VBS easier?

Questions should be relevant to the meeting.

Do not ask questions simply to fill time.

---

# 9️⃣ Prepare Talking Points

Prepare the most important points.

Possible talking points:

- Project progress
- Completed milestones
- Customer wins
- Open issues
- Improvements
- Upcoming priorities
- Risks
- Customer requests
- Renewal
- Expansion opportunities

Separate:

**Confirmed Information**

from

**Questions / Topics to Explore**

Do not present assumptions as facts.

---

# 🔟 Prepare Risks and Opportunities

Identify:

### Risks

- Delivery concerns
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Reduced engagement
- Escalations
- Renewal risk
- Payment concerns
- Stakeholder changes

### Opportunities

- New projects
- Increased capacity
- Additional services
- Cross-sell
- Upsell
- Strategic relationship
- Future requirements

Potential opportunities should not be presented as confirmed business.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Define Desired Outcomes

Before the meeting, define what success looks like.

Examples:

- Customer confirms satisfaction.
- Open issue receives a clear action plan.
- Future requirements are identified.
- Renewal conversation progresses.
- Customer agrees to next meeting.
- Stakeholder introduction is secured.
- Delivery concern is resolved.

Create:

| Desired Outcome | How We Will Know It Happened |
|---|---|
| [Outcome] | [Evidence] |

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Create Meeting Agenda

Example:

## Meeting Agenda

### 1. Opening

Quick introduction and meeting objective.

### 2. Customer Update

Ask about current priorities and experience.

### 3. Project / Delivery Review

Review relevant work.

### 4. Open Actions

Review outstanding items.

### 5. Risks / Concerns

Discuss important issues.

### 6. Future Priorities

Discuss upcoming needs.

### 7. Next Steps

Confirm actions, owners, and deadlines.

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Conduct Meeting

During the meeting:

- Listen carefully.
- Capture customer feedback.
- Record decisions.
- Record commitments.
- Clarify unclear points.
- Avoid interrupting unnecessarily.
- Do not promise unconfirmed actions.
- Confirm important next steps before closing.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Enter a meeting without reviewing previous commitments.
- Invent customer feedback.
- Invent project status.
- Assume customer satisfaction.
- Present potential opportunities as confirmed business.
- Promise unapproved actions.
- Ignore customer concerns.
- Overload the meeting with unnecessary information.

The objective is **preparedness—not over-preparation**.

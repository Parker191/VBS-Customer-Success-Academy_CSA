# 📅📊 Customer Success Weekly Review Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for reviewing weekly Customer Success activity, customer health, risks, opportunities, completed actions, and upcoming priorities.

The objective is to help the CSM understand what happened during the week and create a focused plan for the following week.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Weekly Customer Data
      ↓
1. Review Customer Activity
      ↓
2. Review Customer Health Changes
      ↓
3. Review Customer Meetings
      ↓
4. Review Customer Feedback
      ↓
5. Review Issues and Escalations
      ↓
6. Review Risks
      ↓
7. Review Revenue / Expansion
      ↓
8. Review Completed Actions
      ↓
9. Identify Weekly Wins
      ↓
10. Identify Missed / Delayed Actions
      ↓
11. Identify New Priorities
      ↓
12. Create Next Week Action Plan
      ↓
13. Update CRM / Dashboard
```

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Measure CSM performance only by activity volume.
- Invent customer outcomes.
- Invent health changes.
- Hide missed actions.
- Treat potential revenue as confirmed.
- Ignore risks because the week was busy.
- Mark incomplete work as completed.
- Change customer health without evidence.
- Create priorities without understanding customer impact.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The weekly review should answer:

> **What happened?**

> **What changed?**

> **What matters?**

> **What needs action?**

> **What should I focus on next week?**

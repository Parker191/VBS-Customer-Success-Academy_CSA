# ❤️ At-Risk Customer Recovery Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying why a customer is at risk and creating a practical recovery plan.

The goal is to understand the customer's situation, address the underlying problems, rebuild confidence, and validate recovery with the customer.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
At-Risk Customer
       ↓
1. Collect Account Information
       ↓
2. Analyze Customer Health
       ↓
3. Analyze Customer Sentiment
       ↓
4. Identify Risk Drivers
       ↓
5. Identify Root Causes
       ↓
6. Create Recovery Plan
       ↓
7. Prepare Customer Communication
       ↓
8. Create Success Plan
       ↓
9. Follow Up
       ↓
10. Validate Recovery
       ↓
11. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Account Information

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Current customer health
- Customer goals
- Services being used
- Recent projects
- Delivery performance
- Customer engagement
- Recent customer feedback
- Open issues
- Escalations
- Stakeholder relationships
- Recent wins
- Recent losses or problems
- Renewal date
- Known future requirements

Do not assume missing information.

---

# 2️⃣ Analyze Customer Health

Use:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Review:

- Overall health
- Risk level
- Engagement
- Delivery
- Relationship
- Commercial risk
- Retention risk

Determine which areas are contributing most to the customer's risk.

---

# 3️⃣ Analyze Customer Sentiment

Use:

**Customer Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer**

Identify:

- Positive signals
- Negative signals
- Customer concerns
- Customer expectations
- Relationship sentiment
- Confidence level

Do not label the customer based on sentiment alone.

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Risk Drivers

Create a structured assessment:

| Risk Area | Evidence | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |
| Communication | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |
| Relationship | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |
| Value | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |
| Engagement | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |
| Commercial | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Priority] |

Only include risks supported by evidence.

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Root Causes

Use:

**Escalation & RCA Assistant**

Where appropriate, identify:

- Immediate problem
- Root cause
- Contributing factors
- Customer impact
- Internal impact

Do not confuse symptoms with root causes.

Example:

```text
Symptom:
Customer is unhappy with delivery.

Possible Root Cause:
Unclear project capacity planning.

Contributing Factor:
Limited visibility into upcoming workload.
```

---

# 6️⃣ Create Recovery Plan

Use:

**Customer Feedback Action Plan Generator**

Create:

| Risk / Issue | Recovery Action | Owner | Deadline | Success Measure | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prioritize actions that directly affect:

1. Customer trust
2. Customer outcomes
3. Delivery performance
4. Communication
5. Relationship strength

---

# 7️⃣ Prepare Customer Communication

Use:

**Customer Communication Quality Reviewer**

The communication should:

- Acknowledge the customer's concerns
- Demonstrate understanding
- Take appropriate ownership
- Explain actions
- Set realistic expectations
- Avoid unsupported promises
- Provide a clear next step

Do not attempt to hide problems.

---

# 8️⃣ Create / Update Customer Success Plan

Use:

**Customer Success Plan Generator**

Review:

- Customer goals
- Definition of success
- Success metrics
- Current state
- Desired future state
- Milestones
- Responsibilities
- Risks
- Review points

The recovery plan should connect to the customer's desired business outcomes.

---

# 9️⃣ Follow-Up

Create a follow-up schedule based on the seriousness of the risk.

Possible cadence:

```text
Critical Risk
↓
Frequent follow-up

High Risk
↓
Weekly review

Medium Risk
↓
Regular health review

Low Risk
↓
Normal customer cadence
```

The actual cadence should be determined by the CSM and account context.

---

# 🔟 Validate Recovery

Do not consider the account recovered merely because internal actions were completed.

Validate:

- Customer feedback
- Customer engagement
- Delivery performance
- Customer confidence
- Success metrics
- Relationship health

Ask:

> Has the customer's underlying concern actually improved?

# 🚦 Recovery Status

🟢 **Recovered**

Customer concerns have been addressed and health is stable.

🟡 **Improving**

Actions are underway and customer indicators are improving.

🟠 **Active Recovery**

Significant risks remain and active intervention is required.

🔴 **Critical Recovery**

Serious relationship, financial, contractual, or customer-impact risk requires immediate internal attention.

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer information.
- Assume the reason for dissatisfaction.
- Automatically label every unhealthy account as a churn risk.
- Promise compensation or discounts.
- Assign blame.
- Change customer health without evidence.
- Declare recovery without customer validation.
- Send AI-generated communication without CSM review.

For serious risks, follow the official VBS escalation process.

# 🎯 Desired Outcome

The workflow should help the CSM move from:

**Risk → Understanding → Root Cause → Recovery → Customer Validation → Stable Relationship**

The ultimate goal is not simply to make the health score Green.

The goal is to restore genuine customer value and confidence.

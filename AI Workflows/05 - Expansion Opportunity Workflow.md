# 💰 Expansion Opportunity Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying, evaluating, and validating genuine customer expansion opportunities.

The objective is to identify additional customer needs that VBS can appropriately support.

Expansion should be based on customer value, business need, and evidence—not pressure or assumptions.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Existing Customer
       ↓
1. Review Customer Account
       ↓
2. Assess Customer Health
       ↓
3. Review Current Services
       ↓
4. Understand Customer Goals
       ↓
5. Identify Demand Signals
       ↓
6. Identify Customer Gaps
       ↓
7. Evaluate Expansion Opportunity
       ↓
8. Validate With Customer
       ↓
9. Create Expansion Action Plan
       ↓
10. Coordinate With Sales / KAM
       ↓
11. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Review Customer Account

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Current services
- Current engagement model
- Current project volume
- Historical project volume
- Current stakeholders
- Decision makers
- Customer goals
- Success criteria
- Recent feedback
- Customer health
- Open issues
- Recent wins
- Future requirements
- Known upcoming projects

Do not assume missing information.

---

# 2️⃣ Assess Customer Health

Use:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Review:

- Overall health
- Delivery performance
- Customer sentiment
- Relationship strength
- Engagement
- Retention risk

Expansion should generally be pursued when the customer relationship is healthy enough to support the conversation.

If the account is at serious risk, prioritize recovery before expansion.

---

- Increased delivery requirements

---

# 4️⃣ Understand Customer Goals

Identify:

- Current business priorities
- Upcoming projects
- Growth plans
- Delivery challenges
- Capacity requirements
- Resource requirements
- New capabilities needed
- Timeline changes

Focus on the customer's business needs.

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Demand Signals

Possible expansion signals include:

- Customer mentions increased workload
- Customer asks about additional capacity
- Customer discusses upcoming projects
- Customer requests additional services
- Existing resource capacity is insufficient
- Customer asks about new capabilities
- Customer expresses satisfaction and interest in doing more
- Customer is entering a new project phase
- Customer has requirements outside the current engagement

Classify each signal:

**Confirmed / Strong Signal / Possible / Unknown**

---

# 6️⃣ Identify Customer Gaps

Determine whether there is a genuine gap between:

**Customer Need**

and

**Current VBS Support**

Create:

| Customer Need | Current Support | Gap | Potential Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Need] | [Current] | [Gap] | [Potential solution] |

Do not create a gap simply to generate a sales opportunity.

---

# 7️⃣ Evaluate Expansion Opportunity

Use:

**Revenue Expansion Opportunity Analyzer**

Assess:

### 🟢 High Potential

Clear customer need, strong relationship, evidence of demand, and appropriate VBS capability.

### 🟡 Medium Potential

Potential need exists but customer requirements or timing are not yet confirmed.

### 🟠 Low Potential

Weak evidence or unclear customer need.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information.

Evaluate:

- Customer need
- Business impact
- Timing
- Relationship health
- Customer interest
- VBS capability
- Delivery capacity
- Commercial feasibility

---

# 8️⃣ Validate With Customer

Do not assume the customer wants additional services.

Use customer-focused questions such as:

- What projects are coming up over the next few months?
- Do you anticipate needing additional delivery capacity?
- Are there areas where your current resources are stretched?
- Are there services you currently handle internally that you may want support with?
- What challenges are you expecting as project volume changes?
- Would additional support in this area be useful?

The objective is discovery, not pressure.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer requirements.
- Assume customer interest.
- Push unnecessary services.
- Pressure the customer.
- Promise pricing or discounts.
- Promise capacity without confirmation.
- Promise delivery timelines without validation.
- Ignore customer health issues.
- Treat every customer comment as an expansion opportunity.
- Send AI-generated customer communication without CSM review.

Customer value comes before revenue opportunity.

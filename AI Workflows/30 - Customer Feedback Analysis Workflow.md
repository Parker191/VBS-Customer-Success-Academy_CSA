# 💬🔍 Customer Feedback Analysis Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for collecting, analyzing, categorizing, prioritizing, and acting on customer feedback.

The objective is to turn customer feedback into meaningful Customer Success insights and measurable improvement actions.

---

```text
Customer Feedback
      ↓
1. Capture Feedback

2. Identify Feedback Type

      ↓
5. Identify Root Cause / Driver
      ↓
6. Determine Customer Impact

      ↓
```

# 1️⃣ Capture Feedback

Collect feedback from relevant sources.

Possible sources:

- Customer emails
- Customer meetings
- Calls
- Surveys
- CSAT
- NPS
- QBRs
- MBRs
- Support discussions
- Escalations
- Project reviews
- Direct customer comments

Capture the customer's actual message whenever possible.

---

# 2️⃣ Identify Feedback Type

Classify feedback into one or more categories.

### 😊 Positive Feedback

Customer recognizes something that is working well.

Examples:

- Good communication
- Excellent quality
- Fast response
- Strong technical support
- Helpful team

### ⚠️ Improvement Feedback

Customer identifies something that could be better.

Examples:

- Better communication required
- Faster delivery needed
- More frequent updates
- Better documentation

### 🚨 Complaint

Customer reports dissatisfaction or failure.

Examples:

- Missed deadline
- Quality problem
- Poor communication
- Unresolved issue

### 💡 Suggestion

Customer recommends a new idea or approach.

Examples:

- New reporting format
- New workflow
- Additional service

### ⭐ Appreciation

Customer specifically recognizes an individual, team, or service.

### ❓ Request

Customer asks for something specific.

---

# 3️⃣ Separate Facts From Interpretation

Separate what the customer actually said from what the CSM believes it means.

Example:

### Customer Statement

> "We don't always know the current project status."

### Fact

Customer wants better visibility.

### Interpretation

Customer may feel communication is insufficient.

The interpretation should remain an interpretation unless the customer confirms it.

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Customer Need

Ask:

> What does the customer actually need?

Possible needs:

- Visibility
- Faster communication
- Better quality
- Faster delivery
- More capacity
- Better technical support
- Clearer expectations
- Better reporting
- More proactive engagement

Create:

**Customer Need:**

[Need]

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Root Cause / Driver

Determine what is driving the feedback.

Possible drivers:

- Process
- Communication
- Delivery
- Quality
- Resource
- Technology
- Expectations
- Pricing
- Scope
- Customer-side dependency
- Internal coordination

Do not claim a root cause without evidence.

If the cause is unclear:

**Root Cause: Not Confirmed**

---

# 6️⃣ Determine Customer Impact

Assess the effect on the customer.

Possible impact:

🟢 Low

Minor inconvenience with limited effect.

🟡 Medium

Creates noticeable operational difficulty.

🟠 High

Affects project performance, relationship, or customer confidence.

🔴 Critical

May create serious business or relationship risk.

⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information.

Create:

| Feedback | Impact | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Feedback] | [Impact] | [Evidence] |

---

# 7️⃣ Determine Priority

Prioritize feedback based on:

**Customer Impact + Frequency + Urgency + Business Importance**

### 🔴 P1 — Critical

Immediate attention required.

### 🟠 P2 — High

Important issue requiring prompt action.

### 🟡 P3 — Medium

Should be addressed through normal improvement.

### 🟢 P4 — Low

Monitor or consider for future improvement.

Do not prioritize based only on how strongly the feedback is worded.

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Required Action

Convert feedback into a specific action.

Weak:

> Improve communication.

Better:

> Provide a weekly project status update covering completed work, current work, blockers, and next milestones.

Actions should be:

- Specific
- Practical
- Relevant
- Trackable

---

# 9️⃣ Assign Owner

Possible owners:

- CSM
- KAM
- Delivery
- Project Manager
- BDE / Sales
- Finance
- Technical Team
- Management

If ownership is unknown:

**Owner: To Be Confirmed**

Never guess.

---

# 🔟 Track Resolution

Track the feedback action.

Possible statuses:

⚪ Not Started

🔵 In Progress

🟢 Resolved

🟡 Delayed

🔴 Blocked

Do not mark feedback as resolved simply because an internal action was completed.

Resolution should consider the customer outcome.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Confirm Customer Outcome

After action is taken, determine whether the customer believes the issue has been addressed.

Possible outcomes:

🟢 Resolved

🟡 Partially Resolved

🟠 Still a Concern

🔴 Not Resolved

⚪ Customer Confirmation Pending

Customer confirmation is preferred where appropriate.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Identify Improvement Opportunity

Ask:

> Is this feedback specific to one customer, or could it indicate a broader process improvement?

Examples:

### Customer-Specific

One customer requires a particular reporting format.

### Broader Improvement

Multiple customers request better project visibility.

Potential improvements:

- SOP update
- Process change
- Reporting improvement
- Training
- CRM field
- Communication cadence
- Quality control
- Delivery process

# 🧠 Feedback Example

### Customer Feedback

> "The team is doing a good job, but we need more visibility into project progress."

### Analysis

**Type:** 🟡 Improvement Feedback

**Positive Signal:**  
Customer recognizes that the team is doing a good job.

**Customer Need:**  
Better project visibility.

**Driver:**  
Communication / reporting.

**Impact:**  
Medium — customer lacks sufficient visibility.

**Priority:**  
P2 — High.

**Recommended Action:**  
Introduce a consistent project status update containing completed work, current work, blockers, and upcoming milestones.

**Owner:**  
CSM / Delivery — confirm ownership.

**Customer Outcome:**  
To be confirmed after implementation.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Change the meaning of customer feedback.
- Invent customer statements.
- Treat AI interpretation as customer confirmation.
- Assign blame without evidence.
- Mark feedback resolved without considering customer outcome.
- Ignore repeated feedback.
- Treat every complaint as a critical escalation.
- Treat every suggestion as a required change.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Listen → Understand → Act → Confirm → Improve**

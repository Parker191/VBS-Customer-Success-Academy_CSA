# 🎯 Customer Action Tracker Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for capturing, organizing, tracking, and closing customer-related action items.

The objective is to make sure commitments made by customers, CSMs, and internal teams are clearly documented, assigned, followed up, and completed.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Meeting / Email / Customer Interaction
              ↓
1. Capture Action Items
              ↓
2. Separate Customer and Internal Actions
              ↓
3. Define the Required Action
              ↓
4. Assign Owner
              ↓
5. Assign Deadline
              ↓
6. Define Expected Outcome
              ↓
7. Track Status
              ↓
8. Monitor Dependencies
              ↓
9. Identify Overdue Actions
              ↓
10. Follow Up
              ↓
11. Confirm Completion
              ↓
12. Update Customer
              ↓
13. Update CRM
              ↓
14. Close Action
```

---

# 1️⃣ Capture Action Items

Capture actions from:

- Customer meetings
- Kickoff meetings
- WBRs
- MBRs
- QBRs
- Emails
- Calls
- Escalations
- Internal meetings
- Customer requests

Do not rely only on memory.

Every important commitment should be documented.

---

# 2️⃣ Separate Customer and Internal Actions

Separate actions into:

### 👤 Customer Action

Something the customer needs to do.

Examples:

- Share drawings.
- Confirm project requirements.
- Approve a deliverable.
- Provide feedback.
- Confirm a meeting time.

### 🏢 Internal Action

Something VBS needs to do.

Examples:

- Share project update.
- Review timesheet.
- Resolve delivery issue.
- Prepare report.
- Coordinate with Finance.
- Follow up with Delivery.

### 🤝 Shared Action

Both customer and VBS have responsibilities.

Example:

- Customer provides project information.
- VBS prepares the required model.

---

# 3️⃣ Define the Required Action

Make the action specific.

Weak:

> Follow up with customer.

Better:

> Ask the customer to confirm the expected project start date.

Weak:

> Check project.

Better:

> Confirm with Delivery whether the revised model will be delivered by Friday.

The action should describe exactly what needs to happen.

---

# 4️⃣ Assign Owner

Every action should have an owner.

Possible owners:

- CSM
- Customer
- KAM
- BDE / Sales
- Delivery
- Project Manager
- Finance
- Operations
- Management

If the owner is unknown:

**Owner: To Be Confirmed**

Never guess ownership.

---

# 5️⃣ Assign Deadline

Every important action should have a deadline where possible.

Record:

**Due Date:**
[Date]

If no deadline was agreed:

**Due Date: Not Confirmed**

Do not invent deadlines.

If the action is time-sensitive but no date exists, the CSM should clarify the expected timeline.

---

# 6️⃣ Define Expected Outcome

Ask:

> What should be true when this action is completed?

Examples:

**Action:**

Customer to share drawings.

**Expected Outcome:**

VBS receives the required drawings and can begin project preparation.

Another example:

**Action:**

CSM to confirm delivery timeline.

**Expected Outcome:**

Customer receives a confirmed and realistic delivery date.

---

# 7️⃣ Track Status

Use simple statuses.

### ⚪ Not Started

Action has not begun.

### 🔵 In Progress

Action is currently being worked on.

### 🟡 Waiting

Action is waiting for another person or dependency.

### 🟠 At Risk

Action may miss the deadline.

### 🔴 Overdue

Deadline has passed and action is not complete.

### 🟢 Completed

Action has been completed.

### ⚫ Cancelled

Action is no longer required.

---

# 8️⃣ Monitor Dependencies

Some actions cannot move forward without another action.

Examples:

```text
Customer sends drawings
        ↓
Delivery reviews drawings
        ↓
CSM confirms requirements
        ↓
Project begins
```

Record dependencies.

Create:

| Action | Dependency | Dependency Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Dependency] | [Owner] | [Status] |

Do not repeatedly chase an action without understanding what is blocking it.

---

# 9️⃣ Identify Overdue Actions

Review action items for missed deadlines.

Create:

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Days Overdue | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Days] | [Impact] |

Prioritize overdue actions based on:

- Customer impact
- Urgency
- Business impact
- Dependency
- Escalation risk

Not every overdue action is equally important.

---

# 🔟 Follow Up

Use appropriate follow-up based on ownership.

### Customer Follow-up

Keep it polite and specific.

Example:

> Hi [Name], just following up on the information discussed during our meeting. Once we receive it, we can proceed with the next step.

### Internal Follow-up

Be concise.

Example:

> Hi [Name], checking on the status of the action item from our discussion. Could you please confirm the expected completion date?

### Escalated Follow-up

If the action is critical and repeatedly delayed, follow the appropriate escalation process.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Confirm Completion

Do not mark an action completed simply because someone says:

> "Done."

Confirm that the expected outcome was achieved.

Example:

**Action:**

Send revised project model.

**Completion:**

Model sent.

**Outcome:**

Customer confirmed receipt.

Where appropriate, customer confirmation provides stronger evidence of completion.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Update Customer

If an internal action affects the customer, communicate the outcome.

Examples:

- Issue resolved.
- Timeline confirmed.
- Updated model delivered.
- Report shared.
- Requirement confirmed.

The customer should not have to repeatedly ask for status.

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Update CRM

Create a CRM-ready action tracker.

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Status | Dependency | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Status] | [Dependency] | [Outcome] |

Record important customer commitments and actions in the appropriate CRM location.

---

# 1️⃣4️⃣ Close Action

An action can be closed when:

- Required work is completed.
- Expected outcome is achieved.
- Relevant stakeholders are informed.
- CRM / tracker is updated.

Use:

**Status: Completed**

Do not leave completed actions open unnecessarily.

# 🧠 Example

### Meeting Discussion

Customer says:

> "We'll send the updated drawings by Wednesday."

VBS says:

> "We'll review them and confirm the project timeline."

### Action Tracker

| Action | Type | Owner | Due Date | Status | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Send updated drawings | Customer | Customer | Wednesday | Not Started | Drawings received |
| Review drawings | Internal | Delivery | After receipt | Waiting | Drawings reviewed |
| Confirm timeline | Internal | CSM | After review | Waiting | Customer receives confirmed timeline |

### Important Observation

The CSM should not chase Delivery for the timeline before the drawings are received.

The drawing submission is a dependency.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent action items.
- Invent owners.
- Invent deadlines.
- Mark actions complete without evidence.
- Ignore customer-owned actions.
- Ignore internal dependencies.
- Chase people unnecessarily.
- Treat every overdue action as an escalation.
- Leave important customer commitments undocumented.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Every important action needs an owner, a timeline, and a clear outcome.**

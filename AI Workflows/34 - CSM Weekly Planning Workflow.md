# 📅⚡ CSM Weekly Planning Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for helping Customer Success Managers plan their week, prioritize customer activities, manage follow-ups, identify risks, and focus on high-impact work.

The objective is to create a realistic weekly plan based on customer needs, business priorities, deadlines, and existing commitments.

---

```text
CSM Weekly Inputs

1. Collect Open Tasks

```

# 9️⃣ Identify Urgent Work

Identify tasks that cannot reasonably wait.

Examples:

🔴 Customer escalation

🔴 Critical delivery issue

🔴 Immediate customer deadline

🔴 Important renewal action

🔴 Time-sensitive invoice issue

Urgency should be based on actual impact and deadline.

Do not make everything urgent.

# 🔟 Prioritize Work

Use four planning levels.

## 🔴 P1 — Must Do

Critical or time-sensitive work.

## 🟠 P2 — Important

Important work that should be completed this week.

## 🟡 P3 — Planned

Useful work that should be completed if capacity allows.

## 🟢 P4 — Optional

Lower-impact work that can be deferred if necessary.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Treat every task as urgent.
- Overload the weekly plan.
- Ignore customer emergencies.
- Ignore important deadlines.
- Assume dependencies are complete.
- Invent meetings.
- Invent deadlines.
- Invent customer priorities.
- Prioritize only revenue-generating work.
- Ignore operational responsibilities.
- Ignore strategic customer work.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Plan the week around customer impact, urgency, deadlines, and business priorities — while keeping enough flexibility for unexpected customer needs.**

# 🚨 Customer Escalation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured end-to-end workflow for handling customer escalations using AI-assisted analysis.

The workflow helps the CSM move from the initial escalation through analysis, action planning, customer communication, follow-up, and CRM documentation.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Escalation
       ↓
1. Collect Information
       ↓
2. Analyze Customer Sentiment
       ↓
3. Assess Customer Health Impact
       ↓
4. Perform Root Cause Analysis
       ↓
5. Create Corrective Action Plan
       ↓
6. Prepare Customer Response
       ↓
7. Create Follow-Up Plan
       ↓
8. Create CRM Documentation
       ↓
9. CSM Review
       ↓
10. Take Action
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Information

Before starting the analysis, collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Escalation date
- Escalation source
- Customer concern
- Project / service involved
- Customer impact
- Current project status
- Previous related issues
- Stakeholders involved
- Current customer health
- Recent customer communication
- Relevant delivery information

Do not proceed with assumptions when critical information is missing.

---

# 2️⃣ Analyze Customer Sentiment

Use:

**Customer Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer**

Determine:

- Overall sentiment
- Positive signals
- Negative signals
- Customer concerns
- Customer expectations
- Relationship risk

The CSM must review the AI interpretation.

---

# 3️⃣ Assess Customer Health Impact

Use:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Determine whether the escalation should affect:

- Customer health
- Relationship risk
- Engagement risk
- Delivery risk
- Retention risk

Do not automatically mark a customer Red because an escalation occurred.

Consider the broader customer relationship.

---

# 4️⃣ Root Cause Analysis

Use:

**Escalation & RCA Assistant**

Identify:

- Problem statement
- Immediate cause
- Root cause
- Contributing factors
- Customer impact
- Internal impact
- Corrective actions
- Preventive actions

Avoid assigning blame.

Focus on understanding and fixing the problem.

---

# 5️⃣ Corrective Action Plan

Create:

| Issue | Action | Owner | Deadline | Priority | Success Measure | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Only use confirmed owners and deadlines.

If information is missing:

**To be confirmed**

---

# 6️⃣ Customer Response

Use:

**Customer Communication Quality Reviewer**

Before sending the response, verify:

- Customer concern is acknowledged
- Appropriate ownership is shown
- Facts are accurate
- Actions are clearly explained
- Timeline is accurate
- No unsupported commitment is made
- Tone is professional and empathetic
- Next step is clear

---

# 7️⃣ Follow-Up Plan

Define:

- Next customer communication
- Internal follow-up
- Action-item review
- Customer validation
- Escalation closure criteria

The escalation should not be considered successfully resolved simply because an internal task was completed.

The customer outcome should be validated.

---

# 8️⃣ CRM Documentation

Create a concise CRM note containing:

**Escalation:**
[Summary]

**Customer Impact:**
[Impact]

**Root Cause:**
[Confirmed root cause]

**Actions:**
[Actions]

**Owner:**
[Owner]

**Deadline:**
[Deadline]

**Customer Communication:**
[Communication provided]

**Next Follow-Up:**
[Date / action]

**Current Status:**
[Open / Monitoring / Resolved / Closed]

---

# 9️⃣ CSM Review

Before taking action, verify:

- [ ] Facts are correct
- [ ] Customer impact is understood
- [ ] Sentiment assessment is reasonable
- [ ] Customer health impact is appropriate
- [ ] Root cause is evidence-based
- [ ] Owners are confirmed
- [ ] Deadlines are confirmed
- [ ] Customer response is reviewed
- [ ] Internal stakeholders are aligned
- [ ] CRM is updated

---

# 🚦 Escalation Status

Use:

🟢 **Monitoring**  
Issue is being monitored and customer impact is limited.

🟡 **Active**  
Issue requires active CSM coordination.

🟠 **High Priority**  
Issue has significant customer or delivery impact.

🔴 **Critical**  
Issue presents serious customer, financial, contractual, or relationship risk and requires immediate internal escalation.

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent information
- Assign blame
- Create unauthorized commitments
- Promise unapproved compensation
- Change customer health automatically
- Close an escalation without validating the outcome
- Send AI-generated customer communication without review

For serious escalations, follow the official VBS escalation process and involve the appropriate internal stakeholders.

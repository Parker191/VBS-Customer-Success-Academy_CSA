# 🚨 Customer Risk Radar Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying early warning signals that may indicate customer risk.

The objective is to help the CSM detect potential customer problems before they become serious escalations, churn risks, or relationship failures.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
1. Review Health Signals
      ↓
2. Review Engagement Signals
      ↓
3. Review Delivery Signals
      ↓
4. Review Relationship Signals
      ↓
5. Review Financial Signals
      ↓
6. Review Renewal Signals
      ↓
7. Review Sentiment Signals
      ↓
8. Identify Risk Patterns
      ↓
9. Determine Risk Level
      ↓
10. Identify Evidence
      ↓
11. Create Mitigation Plan
      ↓
12. Assign Owner
      ↓
13. Monitor Risk
      ↓
14. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Review Health Signals

Look for:

- Health score decline
- Green → Yellow movement
- Yellow → Orange movement
- Orange → Red movement
- Repeated health concerns
- Missing health information

A health change should have supporting evidence.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Engagement Signals

Look for:

- Customer stops responding
- Fewer meetings
- Cancelled meetings
- Delayed responses
- Reduced communication
- Reduced usage
- Reduced project activity
- Stakeholder becomes inactive

Important:

Low communication does not automatically mean the customer is unhappy.

Consider the customer's normal communication pattern.

---

# 3️⃣ Review Delivery Signals

Look for:

- Missed deadlines
- Repeated delays
- Quality issues
- Rework
- Customer complaints
- Unresolved blockers
- Resource problems
- Scope confusion
- Repeated delivery issues

Identify whether the issue is:

**One-Time**

or

**Recurring**

Recurring problems generally require greater attention.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Relationship Signals

Look for:

- Champion becomes inactive
- Decision maker is not engaged
- Key stakeholder leaves
- No executive relationship
- Single-threaded relationship
- Customer stops providing feedback
- Customer relationship becomes weaker

Relationship gaps can create future risk even when delivery is currently healthy.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Financial Signals

Where relevant, review:

- Overdue invoices
- Payment delays
- Revenue decline
- Reduced project volume
- Reduced resource usage
- Budget concerns
- Revenue at risk

Separate:

**Financial Risk**

from

**Normal Payment Processing**

Do not label a customer financially at risk without evidence.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Renewal Signals

Look for:

- Renewal approaching
- Renewal discussion delayed
- No renewal conversation
- Customer value unclear
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Open critical issues
- Reduced usage
- Reduced engagement
- Competitive concerns

High renewal risk should receive appropriate attention before the renewal deadline.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Sentiment Signals

Review customer communication for:

### 🟢 Positive

- Appreciation
- Satisfaction
- Positive feedback
- Confidence

### 🟡 Neutral

- Routine communication
- No obvious concerns

### 🟠 Negative

- Frustration
- Repeated complaints
- Concern
- Disappointment

### 🔴 Serious

- Escalation
- Threat to leave
- Strong dissatisfaction
- Major trust concern

Do not infer emotion without evidence.

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Risk Patterns

Look for combinations of signals.

Example:

```text
Low Engagement
      +
Negative Feedback
      +
Upcoming Renewal
      =
🚨 Higher Risk
```

Another example:

```text
Delivery Delays
      +
Repeated Quality Issues
      +
Customer Complaints
      =
🚨 Delivery Risk
```

Multiple related signals may indicate greater risk than one isolated signal.

---

# 9️⃣ Determine Risk Level

## 🟢 Low Risk

No meaningful warning signals.

Normal customer-success cadence.

---

## 🟡 Emerging Risk

Early warning signals exist.

Requires monitoring and proactive action.

---

## 🟠 High Risk

Multiple meaningful risk signals exist.

Requires active CSM intervention.

---

## 🔴 Critical Risk

Severe customer impact or immediate retention/relationship concern.

Requires immediate escalation and action.

---

## ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information to determine risk.

Do not assume low risk simply because information is missing.

---

# 🔟 Identify Evidence

Every risk should have evidence.

Create:

| Risk | Evidence | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Risk] | [Evidence] | [Date] | [Meeting / Email / CRM] |

Avoid vague statements such as:

> "Customer seems unhappy."

Instead identify what happened.

Example:

> "Customer raised two delivery concerns during the last meeting."

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Treat one quiet email as proof of churn.
- Assume low engagement means dissatisfaction.
- Invent customer sentiment.
- Create risks without evidence.
- Ignore repeated warning signals.
- Treat potential risks as confirmed problems.
- Automatically escalate every warning.
- Ignore context.
- Share unnecessary confidential information.

The purpose of the Risk Radar is:

**Early Detection → Evidence → Action**

Not:

**Guessing → Panic → Escalation**

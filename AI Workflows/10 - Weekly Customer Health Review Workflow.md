# ❤️📊 Weekly Customer Health Review Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for reviewing the health of the CSM's customer portfolio each week.

The objective is to identify customers that are healthy, customers that need attention, emerging risks, and the actions required to maintain or improve customer relationships.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Portfolio
       ↓
1. Collect Customer Data
       ↓
2. Review Customer Health
       ↓
3. Review Engagement
       ↓
4. Review Delivery
       ↓
5. Review Customer Feedback
       ↓
6. Review Revenue / Renewal
       ↓
7. Identify Risk Signals
       ↓
8. Classify Customer Health
       ↓
9. Prioritize Customers
       ↓
10. Create Weekly Action Plan
       ↓
11. Update CRM
       ↓
12. Review Changes Next Week
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Customer Data

Collect available information for each customer:

- Customer identifier
- Current services
- Current projects
- Last customer interaction
- Next planned interaction
- Customer health
- Recent feedback
- Open issues
- Escalations
- Delivery status
- Engagement level
- Renewal date
- Revenue information where appropriate
- Expansion signals
- Outstanding customer actions
- Outstanding VBS actions

Do not assume missing information.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Customer Health

Review the current health status.

Possible status:

🟢 Green — Healthy

🟡 Yellow — Needs Attention

🟠 Orange — At Risk

🔴 Red — Critical Risk

⚪ Gray — Insufficient Information

Health status should be based on evidence.

---

# 3️⃣ Review Engagement

Review:

- Recent meetings
- Customer responses
- Email engagement
- Proactive check-ins
- Stakeholder engagement
- Meeting attendance
- Customer responsiveness
- Changes in communication patterns

Possible signals:

🟢 Strong engagement

🟡 Moderate engagement

🟠 Declining engagement

🔴 Very low / no engagement

⚪ Unknown

Do not assume silence always means dissatisfaction.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Delivery

Review:

- Current projects
- Delivery progress
- Quality
- Timelines
- Open issues
- Blockers
- Customer concerns
- Completed milestones

Identify:

**On Track**

**Needs Attention**

**At Risk**

**Blocked**

---

# 5️⃣ Review Customer Feedback

Review recent:

- Emails
- Meeting notes
- Survey responses
- Customer comments
- Complaints
- Positive feedback
- Requests
- Suggestions

Use:

**Customer Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer**

Identify sentiment:

🟢 Positive

🟡 Neutral / Mixed

🟠 Negative

🔴 Serious Concern

⚪ Unknown

Look for changes in sentiment over time.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Revenue / Renewal

Where relevant, review:

- Current revenue
- Renewal date
- Renewal status
- Outstanding invoices
- Payment concerns
- Expansion opportunities
- Contract changes
- Usage changes

Revenue information should be handled according to company policy.

Do not expose sensitive financial information unnecessarily.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Risk Signals

Potential risk signals include:

- Declining engagement
- Negative feedback
- Repeated delivery issues
- Missed deadlines
- Open escalations
- Unresolved customer concerns
- Renewal approaching without engagement
- Reduction in project volume
- Stakeholder departure
- Payment concerns
- Customer inactivity
- Unclear future requirements

Classify each signal:

**Confirmed / Strong Signal / Possible / Unknown**

Do not treat assumptions as facts.

---

# 8️⃣ Classify Customer Health

Create:

| Customer | Health | Engagement | Delivery | Sentiment | Key Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer] | [Status] | [Status] | [Status] | [Status] | [Risk] |

Do not automatically classify a customer based on a single metric.

---

# 9️⃣ Prioritize Customers

Prioritize customers using:

### 🔴 P1 — Immediate Attention

Critical customer risk or serious escalation.

### 🟠 P2 — High Attention

Meaningful risk that requires action soon.

### 🟡 P3 — Monitor

Potential concern that should be monitored.

### 🟢 P4 — Healthy

No significant intervention currently required.

Create:

| Priority | Customer | Reason | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Action] |
| P2 | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Action] |
| P3 | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Action] |
| P4 | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Action] |

# 🔟 Create Weekly Action Plan

For each priority customer, define:

- What needs to happen?
- Who owns it?
- When should it happen?
- What outcome is expected?

Create:

| Customer | Action | Owner | Deadline | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer] | [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Outcome] |

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Review Changes Next Week

Compare this week's review with the previous review.

Look for:

- Health improvement
- Health decline
- New risks
- Closed risks
- Increased engagement
- Reduced engagement
- Delivery improvements
- New customer requirements
- Renewal changes
- Expansion signals

The goal is to identify trends—not just snapshots.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Automatically classify customers based on one metric.
- Assume silence means dissatisfaction.
- Assume low usage means churn.
- Invent customer sentiment.
- Invent customer feedback.
- Ignore qualitative information.
- Ignore serious delivery problems because the health score is Green.
- Treat every customer as requiring the same level of attention.
- Share sensitive customer information unnecessarily.

Health scoring should support CSM judgment—not replace it.

# 📊 QBR Preparation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing an effective Quarterly Business Review (QBR).

The workflow helps the CSM move beyond reporting activities and create a customer-focused story around goals, outcomes, value, challenges, and future priorities.

The objective is to make the QBR a strategic customer conversation—not simply a presentation of completed tasks.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
QBR Due
   ↓
1. Collect Customer Information
   ↓
2. Review Customer Goals
   ↓
3. Review Work Delivered
   ↓
4. Analyze Performance
   ↓
5. Review Customer Feedback
   ↓
6. Identify Value Delivered
   ↓
7. Identify Risks and Gaps
   ↓
8. Identify Future Priorities
   ↓
9. Build QBR Story
   ↓
10. Prepare Discussion Questions
   ↓
11. Create Action Plan
   ↓
12. Conduct QBR
   ↓
13. Document Outcomes
```

# 1️⃣ Collect Customer Information

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Review period
- Current services
- Customer goals
- Success criteria
- Projects completed
- Projects in progress
- Delivery performance
- Quality performance
- Customer feedback
- Customer health
- Open issues
- Escalations
- Previous action items
- Stakeholders
- Decision makers
- Future requirements

Do not assume missing information.

---

Use:

- Customer objectives
- Business priorities
- Success criteria
- Expected outcomes
- Current progress
- Changes in priorities

Create:

| Customer Goal | Success Measure | Current Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Goal] | [Measure] | [Status] | [Evidence] |

Confirm whether the original goals are still relevant.

---

# 3️⃣ Review Work Delivered

Summarize:

- Projects completed
- Tasks completed
- Deliverables provided
- Hours / resources used where relevant
- Major milestones
- Important achievements
- Issues encountered
- Issues resolved

Separate **activity** from **outcome**.

Example:

```text
Activity:
Completed BIM modeling for 5 projects.

Outcome:
Customer received modeling support for five projects within the agreed delivery period.
```

---

# 4️⃣ Analyze Performance

Review available performance indicators.

Possible areas:

- Delivery timeliness
- Quality
- Productivity
- Responsiveness
- Resource utilization
- Project completion
- Customer satisfaction
- Open issues
- SLA / agreed expectations

Create:

| Metric | Target | Actual | Status | Comment |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| [Metric] | [Target] | [Actual] | [Status] | [Comment] |

Only use metrics that are actually available.

Do not invent numbers.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Customer Feedback

Use:

**Customer Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer**

Review:

- Positive feedback
- Negative feedback
- Customer concerns
- Expectations
- Sentiment trends
- Relationship signals
- Requests for improvement

Identify whether sentiment has:

🟢 Improved

🟡 Remained Stable

🟠 Declined

⚪ Cannot Be Determined

---

# 6️⃣ Identify Value Delivered

Use:

**QBR Builder & Customer Value Story Generator**

Create a value summary.

Focus on:

- Customer goals achieved
- Business outcomes
- Delivery improvements
- Capacity provided
- Quality improvements
- Time saved
- Problems solved
- Successful projects
- Positive customer outcomes

Use evidence wherever possible.

Do not exaggerate value.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Risks and Gaps

Review:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Identify:

- Delivery risks
- Relationship risks
- Engagement risks
- Value gaps
- Open issues
- Stakeholder gaps
- Future risks
- Renewal risks

Create:

| Risk / Gap | Evidence | Impact | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Risk] | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Action] |

Not every QBR needs to present every internal risk.

Use CSM judgment when deciding what should be customer-facing.

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Future Priorities

Understand:

- Upcoming projects
- Expected workload
- New customer initiatives
- Resource requirements
- Potential challenges
- Customer priorities
- Areas where VBS may provide additional support

Do not automatically turn every future requirement into a sales opportunity.

First understand the customer's need.

---

# 9️⃣ Build QBR Story

Use:

**QBR Builder & Customer Value Story Generator**

Recommended structure:

```text
1. Customer Objectives
       ↓
2. What We Delivered
       ↓
3. Performance
       ↓
4. Value / Outcomes
       ↓
5. Customer Feedback
       ↓
6. Challenges / Improvements
       ↓
7. Future Priorities
       ↓
8. Recommended Next Steps
```

The story should answer:

> **"What has VBS helped the customer accomplish, and what should we do next?"**

---

# 🔟 Prepare Discussion Questions

Prepare customer-focused questions such as:

### Value

- What has been most valuable to your team?
- Where have we helped you most?

### Improvement

- What should we improve?
- Where have we not met expectations?

### Future

- What are your biggest priorities for the next quarter?
- What projects or changes should we prepare for?

### Partnership

- How can VBS become more useful to your team?
- Is there anything you need from us that you are not currently receiving?

Avoid turning the QBR into an interrogation.

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Conduct QBR

During the QBR:

- Start with customer priorities.
- Review agreed objectives.
- Discuss outcomes.
- Share evidence.
- Discuss feedback.
- Address important concerns.
- Discuss future priorities.
- Confirm actions.
- Confirm owners.
- Confirm next steps.

The CSM should actively listen rather than simply present.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent performance metrics.
- Invent customer outcomes.
- Exaggerate value.
- Hide important customer concerns.
- Present internal assumptions as customer facts.
- Turn every QBR into a sales presentation.
- Promise future delivery without validation.
- Send AI-generated customer material without CSM review.

A successful QBR should create shared understanding and clear next steps.

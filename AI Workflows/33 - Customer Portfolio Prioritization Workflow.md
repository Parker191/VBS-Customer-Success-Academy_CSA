# 📊🎯 Customer Portfolio Prioritization Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for prioritizing customers within a Customer Success portfolio.

The objective is to help CSMs determine where attention and time should be focused based on customer health, risk, business importance, engagement, renewal timing, and opportunities.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Portfolio
      ↓
1. Collect Customer Information
      ↓
2. Review Customer Health
      ↓
3. Review Customer Risk
      ↓
4. Review Revenue Importance
      ↓
5. Review Renewal Timing
      ↓
6. Review Customer Engagement
      ↓
7. Review Customer Value
      ↓
8. Review Expansion Opportunities
      ↓
9. Identify Strategic Importance
      ↓
10. Calculate Priority
      ↓
11. Group Customers
      ↓
12. Create CSM Focus Plan
      ↓
13. Schedule Actions
      ↓
14. Review Priority Regularly
```

# 4️⃣ Review Revenue Importance

Review:

- Current revenue
- Revenue trend
- Revenue at risk
- Confirmed revenue
- Potential expansion

Separate:

**Confirmed Revenue**

from

**Potential Revenue**

Never prioritize solely because a customer has high revenue.

Customer health and risk must also be considered.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Renewal Timing

Identify customers approaching renewal.

Example planning bands:

### 🔴 Immediate

Renewal is very close.

### 🟠 Near-Term

Renewal is approaching and preparation should begin.

### 🟡 Future

Renewal is not yet close but preparation can begin.

### 🟢 Long-Term

No immediate renewal focus required.

Use the company's actual renewal process and timelines where available.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Customer Engagement

Review:

- Meeting frequency
- Email responsiveness
- Stakeholder participation
- Project activity
- Feedback
- Communication quality

Possible engagement levels:

🟢 High

🟡 Moderate

🟠 Low

🔴 Very Low

⚪ Unknown

Low engagement does not automatically mean the customer is at risk.

Consider the full context.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Customer Value

Determine:

- Value delivered
- Customer outcomes
- Project success
- Business impact
- Customer-stated benefits

Ask:

> How important is the relationship to the customer's goals?

Do not invent ROI or business impact.

---

# 8️⃣ Review Expansion Opportunities

Review:

- New projects
- Increased workload
- Additional resources
- New services
- Capacity requirements
- Customer growth

Classify:

🟢 Confirmed

🟡 Developing

🔵 Early Signal

⚪ None

Expansion opportunity should not automatically make a customer high priority.

---

# 9️⃣ Identify Strategic Importance

Consider whether the customer has strategic importance.

Possible factors:

- Large relationship
- Long-term partnership
- Important reference customer
- Strong stakeholder relationship
- Significant future opportunity
- Strategic market importance
- High customer influence

Do not assume strategic importance based only on revenue.

---

# 🔟 Calculate Priority

Evaluate the customer across:

### Customer Health

### Customer Risk

### Revenue Importance

### Renewal Timing

### Engagement

### Customer Value

### Expansion Opportunity

### Strategic Importance

Use these factors to create an overall planning priority.

Do not treat the result as an objective mathematical truth.

It is a CSM decision-support tool.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Group Customers

Use three main groups.

## 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

Customers requiring significant CSM attention.

Typical reasons:

- High risk
- Critical issue
- Near renewal with concerns
- Significant customer impact
- Major stakeholder concern
- Important recovery action

---

## 🟠 MEDIUM PRIORITY

Customers requiring regular proactive attention.

Typical reasons:

- Moderate risk
- Developing opportunity
- Important upcoming milestone
- Moderate engagement concern
- Renewal preparation

---

## 🟢 LOW PRIORITY

Customers currently requiring normal monitoring and cadence.

Typical reasons:

- Healthy relationship
- Stable delivery
- Low risk
- No immediate renewal concern
- No major unresolved issues

Low priority does not mean unimportant.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Prioritize customers based only on revenue.
- Ignore healthy customers completely.
- Assume low engagement means churn risk.
- Assume high revenue means strategic importance.
- Treat AI priority as a final decision.
- Ignore customer-specific context.
- Invent renewal dates.
- Invent revenue.
- Invent customer health.
- Ignore customer risks.
- Neglect low-priority customers.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Prioritize based on customer need, risk, impact, and business importance — not simply revenue.**

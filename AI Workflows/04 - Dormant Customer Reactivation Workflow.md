# 😴➡️🟢 Dormant Customer Reactivation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for understanding why a customer has become inactive and creating a thoughtful plan to re-engage the customer.

The objective is not simply to send another follow-up email.

The objective is to understand the customer's current situation, identify whether there is a legitimate reason to reconnect, provide relevant value, and rebuild engagement.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Dormant Customer
       ↓
1. Review Customer History
       ↓
2. Analyze Previous Relationship
       ↓
3. Identify Possible Dormancy Drivers
       ↓
4. Assess Reactivation Potential
       ↓
5. Identify Relevant Customer Value
       ↓
6. Create Reactivation Strategy
       ↓
7. Prepare Personalized Outreach
       ↓
8. Follow Up
       ↓
9. Measure Response
       ↓
10. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Review Customer History

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Last interaction date
- Last project date
- Services previously used
- Previous project volume
- Previous customer goals
- Historical customer feedback
- Previous issues
- Previous escalations
- Previous wins
- Previous stakeholders
- Known decision makers
- Previous commercial relationship
- Last known customer requirement

Do not assume why the customer became inactive.

---

# 2️⃣ Analyze Previous Relationship

Review:

- What services did the customer use?
- What type of projects did they use VBS for?
- How frequently did they engage?
- What value did VBS provide?
- What did the customer like?
- Were there previous concerns?
- Was the relationship healthy before becoming dormant?

# 3️⃣ Identify Possible Dormancy Drivers

Possible reasons may include:

- No current projects
- Project completion
- Seasonal workload
- Budget constraints
- Internal customer changes
- Leadership changes
- Staffing changes
- Customer priorities changed
- Previous delivery concerns
- Communication gaps
- Relationship deterioration
- Competitor involvement
- Unknown reason

Do not present assumptions as facts.

Classify each reason as:

**Confirmed / Possible / Unknown**

---

# 4️⃣ Assess Reactivation Potential

Evaluate:

### 🟢 High Potential

Strong historical relationship, positive value, and likely future requirements.

### 🟡 Medium Potential

Some positive history but future demand is uncertain.

### 🟠 Low Potential

Limited historical engagement or significant unresolved concerns.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information to determine potential.

Assess:

- Historical value
- Relationship strength
- Previous engagement
- Customer feedback
- Current market/project signals
- Known future requirements
- Previous issues

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Relevant Customer Value

Use:

**QBR Builder & Customer Value Story Generator**

Identify the most relevant historical value.

Examples:

- Projects completed
- Delivery capacity provided
- Quality achieved
- Time saved
- Successful outcomes
- Previous positive feedback
- Flexible resource support

Do not exaggerate historical value.

The purpose is to remind the customer of relevant value—not to create artificial urgency.

---

# 7️⃣ Prepare Personalized Outreach

Use:

**Customer Communication Quality Reviewer**

The outreach should:

- Be personalized
- Reference the previous relationship where appropriate
- Show genuine interest
- Avoid sounding like mass marketing
- Ask about current priorities
- Provide a relevant reason to reconnect
- Use a simple call to action

Avoid:

- Excessive sales language
- Pressure
- Generic "just checking in" messages
- Unsupported assumptions
- Artificial urgency

---

# 8️⃣ Follow Up

Create a reasonable follow-up sequence.

Example:

```text
Initial Outreach
      ↓
Wait
      ↓
Follow-Up #1
      ↓
Wait
      ↓
Follow-Up #2
      ↓
Final Value-Based Check-In
```

The exact cadence should depend on:

- Customer history
- Relationship strength
- Previous communication preferences
- Business context

Do not spam the customer.

---

# 9️⃣ Measure Response

Track:

- Outreach sent
- Response received
- Meeting scheduled
- Customer requirement identified
- Opportunity identified
- Customer reactivated
- No response
- Customer requested future follow-up

Possible status:

🟡 Outreach Started

🔵 Engaged

🟢 Reactivated

⚪ No Current Need

🔴 Relationship Risk

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Assume why a customer became dormant.
- Blame the customer.
- Create artificial urgency.
- Spam the customer.
- Invent previous customer feedback.
- Exaggerate historical value.
- Promise discounts or special pricing without approval.
- Treat every dormant customer as an immediate sales opportunity.
- Send AI-generated communication without CSM review.

The purpose of reactivation is to rebuild a valuable customer relationship.

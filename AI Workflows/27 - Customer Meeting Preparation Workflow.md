# 🧠📅 Customer Meeting Preparation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing for customer meetings.

The objective is to help the CSM enter every important customer conversation prepared with context, objectives, questions, risks, opportunities, and desired outcomes.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Information
      ↓
1. Review Customer Background
      ↓
2. Review Previous Meeting
      ↓
3. Review Customer Health
      ↓
4. Review Open Actions
      ↓
5. Review Risks
      ↓
6. Review Opportunities
      ↓
7. Review Customer Goals
      ↓
8. Define Meeting Objective
      ↓
9. Prepare Questions
      ↓
10. Create Agenda
      ↓
11. Define Desired Outcomes
      ↓

      ↓
13. Prepare Backup Topics

      ↓
16. Update CRM
```

# 1️⃣ Review Customer Background

Before the meeting, review:

- Customer name
- Company
- Industry
- Services used
- Current projects
- Relationship history
- Key stakeholders
- Customer goals
- Current account status

Only use verified information.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Previous Meeting

Review the most recent meeting.

Capture:

- Main discussion
- Customer concerns
- Decisions
- Commitments
- Open actions
- Customer feedback
- Next steps

Create:

| Previous Topic | Outcome | Open Action |
|---|---|---|
| [Topic] | [Outcome] | [Action] |

---

# 3️⃣ Review Customer Health

Check:

- Current health
- Previous health
- Health trend
- Engagement
- Delivery
- Relationship
- Financial status
- Customer sentiment

Create:

**Current Health:** [Health]

**Trend:** [Improving / Stable / Declining]

**Reason:** [Evidence]

Do not assume health status without evidence.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Open Actions

Identify unfinished actions.

Create:

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Status] |

Highlight:

- Overdue actions
- Blocked actions
- Customer-owned actions
- Internal actions

---

# 5️⃣ Review Risks

Look for:

- Delivery risks
- Relationship risks
- Renewal risks
- Engagement risks
- Payment risks
- Quality risks
- Stakeholder risks

Create:

| Risk | Evidence | Impact | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Risk] | [Evidence] | [Impact] | [Action] |

---

# 6️⃣ Review Opportunities

Look for:

- New projects
- Additional resource requirements
- New services
- Expansion
- Upsell
- Cross-sell
- Process improvements
- Customer advocacy

Do not force a sales conversation.

Only discuss opportunities that are relevant to the customer's needs.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Customer Goals

Identify what the customer is trying to achieve.

Examples:

- Complete a project
- Increase capacity
- Reduce workload
- Improve delivery speed
- Improve quality
- Support new projects
- Reduce operational pressure

Ask:

> What matters most to the customer right now?

---

# 8️⃣ Define Meeting Objective

Every important meeting should have a clear objective.

Examples:

### Relationship Meeting

Strengthen stakeholder relationship and understand customer priorities.

### Delivery Meeting

Review project performance and resolve blockers.

### QBR

Review value, performance, goals, risks, and future priorities.

### Escalation Meeting

Understand the problem, agree corrective actions, and restore confidence.

### Renewal Meeting

Review value, future needs, and renewal requirements.

Create:

**Meeting Objective:**

[Objective]

---

# 9️⃣ Prepare Questions

Prepare questions based on the meeting objective.

### Customer Priorities

- What are your biggest priorities right now?
- Has anything changed since our last conversation?
- What challenges are you currently facing?

### Delivery

- How are things progressing from your perspective?
- Are there any current delivery concerns?
- Is the current support meeting expectations?

### Relationship

- How has your experience with our team been?
- Is there anything we could improve?

### Future

- What projects or requirements are coming up?
- Where might additional support be useful?

Do not ask questions simply to fill time.

---

# 🔟 Create Agenda

Create a simple agenda.

Example:

```text
1. Welcome and objectives
2. Review previous actions
3. Current project / service update
4. Customer feedback
5. Risks and concerns
6. Upcoming priorities
7. Opportunities / additional support
8. Agree actions
9. Confirm next steps
```

Adjust the agenda to the meeting purpose.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Define Desired Outcomes

Before the meeting ask:

> What should be different after this meeting?

Possible outcomes:

- Issue resolved
- Decision made
- Action agreed
- Customer requirement understood
- Timeline confirmed
- Stakeholder identified
- Renewal next step agreed
- Customer concern addressed

Create:

| Desired Outcome | Success Measure |
|---|---|
| [Outcome] | [Measure] |

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Prepare Talking Points

Prepare concise talking points.

Structure:

### What happened?

[Context]

### What does it mean?

[Insight]

### What should we do?

[Recommendation]

### What do we need from the customer?

[Customer Input]

Avoid overloading the meeting with unnecessary information.

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Prepare Backup Topics

Prepare additional topics if time allows.

Examples:

- Customer feedback
- Upcoming projects
- Stakeholder engagement
- Success metrics
- Value delivered
- Process improvements
- Future requirements
- Customer advocacy

Do not force every topic into the meeting.

---

# 1️⃣4️⃣ Conduct Meeting

During the meeting:

- Listen actively.
- Ask open questions.
- Confirm understanding.
- Capture commitments.
- Avoid interrupting.
- Clarify unclear information.
- Identify risks.
- Identify opportunities.
- Confirm next steps.

Customer conversations should be two-way.

---

# 1️⃣5️⃣ Capture Actions

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Enter meetings without a clear purpose.
- Assume customer priorities.
- Invent customer concerns.
- Force sales conversations.
- Overload the customer with information.
- Promise unapproved solutions.
- Record unconfirmed commitments as facts.
- Ignore customer feedback.
- Share unnecessary confidential information.

The goal is:

**Prepare → Listen → Understand → Act → Follow Through**

# 💬➡️🎯 Customer Feedback Action Plan Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for converting customer feedback into clear, prioritized, and trackable actions.

The objective is to ensure customer feedback does not simply get recorded—it leads to appropriate action and visible follow-through.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Feedback
       ↓
1. Collect Feedback
       ↓
2. Understand the Feedback
       ↓
3. Separate Fact, Opinion, and Request
       ↓
4. Identify Theme
       ↓
5. Assess Customer Impact
       ↓
6. Determine Priority
       ↓
7. Create Action Plan
       ↓
8. Assign Owner
       ↓
9. Set Deadline
       ↓
10. Communicate Response
       ↓
11. Track Progress
       ↓
12. Close the Loop
       ↓
13. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Feedback

Feedback may come from:

- Customer emails
- Meetings
- Calls
- Surveys
- CSAT
- NPS
- QBRs
- WBRs
- MBRs
- Support conversations
- Project reviews
- Informal customer comments

Capture the original meaning accurately.

Do not change the customer's message to make it sound more positive or negative.

---

# 2️⃣ Understand the Feedback

Ask:

- What is the customer saying?
- What problem are they describing?
- What are they happy about?
- What are they unhappy about?
- What do they want changed?
- Is the customer asking for action?
- Is the feedback simply an observation?

If the meaning is unclear, mark it:

**Needs Clarification**

Do not guess.

---

# 3️⃣ Separate Fact, Opinion, and Request

Classify feedback into:

### Fact

Something that can be verified.

Example:

> A delivery was received two days later than expected.

### Opinion

The customer's perception or feeling.

Example:

> The communication felt slow.

### Request

Something the customer wants changed or done.

Example:

> Please provide a weekly progress update.

One piece of feedback may contain more than one category.

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Theme

Group feedback into themes such as:

- Delivery
- Quality
- Communication
- Responsiveness
- Technical capability
- Process
- Pricing
- Billing
- Relationship
- Resource availability
- Project management
- Customer experience

Create:

| Feedback | Theme | Category |
|---|---|---|
| [Feedback] | [Theme] | [Fact / Opinion / Request] |

---

# 5️⃣ Assess Customer Impact

Determine how important the feedback is.

### 🟢 Low Impact

Minor improvement or suggestion.

### 🟡 Moderate Impact

Meaningful issue but no immediate major consequence.

### 🟠 High Impact

Significant effect on customer satisfaction, delivery, or relationship.

### 🔴 Critical Impact

Serious customer, contractual, financial, or relationship risk.

Do not classify impact based only on emotional language.

Consider the actual consequence.

---

# 6️⃣ Determine Priority

Use:

### P1 — Critical

Immediate intervention required.

### P2 — High

Action required soon.

### P3 — Normal

Should be addressed through normal improvement processes.

### P4 — Low

Can be monitored or considered later.

Create:

| Feedback | Impact | Priority | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Feedback] | [Impact] | [Priority] | [Reason] |

---

# 7️⃣ Create Action Plan

Turn meaningful feedback into an action.

Create:

| Feedback | Action | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [Feedback] | [Action] | [Outcome] |

The action should directly address the feedback.

Avoid creating actions that do not solve the underlying issue.

---

# 8️⃣ Assign Owner

Possible owners:

- CSM
- Delivery Team
- KAM
- BDE / Sales
- Finance
- Technical Team
- Management
- Customer

If ownership is unclear:

**Owner: To Be Confirmed**

Never guess.

---

# 9️⃣ Set Deadline

Use:

- Customer-agreed deadline
- Internal target
- Recommended deadline

Clearly distinguish between confirmed and recommended dates.

Example:

**Confirmed Deadline:** July 20

or

**Recommended Deadline:** Within 3 business days

Do not present recommendations as customer commitments.

---

# 🔟 Communicate Response

Where appropriate, tell the customer:

1. We heard the feedback.
2. We understand the concern.
3. We are taking action.
4. Who is responsible.
5. What happens next.
6. When they can expect an update.

Example:

> Thank you for sharing this feedback. We understand the concern around communication and have aligned internally to provide more consistent progress updates. We will share the next update by [date].

Do not promise something that has not been confirmed internally.

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Close the Loop

Feedback should not be considered closed simply because an internal task was completed.

Confirm:

- Action was completed.
- Customer was informed where appropriate.
- Customer concern was addressed.
- Outcome is acceptable.
- CRM was updated.

Ask where appropriate:

> "Has this addressed the concern?"

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Change the meaning of customer feedback.
- Invent customer concerns.
- Assume the customer is unhappy without evidence.
- Ignore negative feedback.
- Promise fixes without internal confirmation.
- Treat every suggestion as a critical issue.
- Close feedback without appropriate follow-through.
- Share sensitive customer information unnecessarily.

Customer feedback should lead to learning and improvement.

# 🎯 Customer Success Action Plan Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for converting customer information, risks, opportunities, and goals into clear and actionable Customer Success plans.

The objective is to ensure that important customer findings result in specific actions, owners, deadlines, and measurable outcomes.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Information
      ↓
1. Identify Customer Priorities
      ↓
2. Identify Problems
      ↓
3. Identify Risks
      ↓
4. Identify Opportunities
      ↓
5. Define Desired Outcomes
      ↓
6. Create Actions
      ↓
7. Prioritize Actions
      ↓
8. Assign Owners
      ↓
9. Set Deadlines
      ↓
10. Define Success Measures
      ↓
11. Execute Actions
      ↓
12. Monitor Progress
      ↓
13. Review Outcomes
      ↓
14. Update Customer Plan
      ↓
15. Update CRM
```

# 1️⃣ Identify Customer Priorities

Understand what matters most to the customer.

Possible priorities:

- Current project delivery
- Quality
- Timeline
- Capacity
- Cost efficiency
- New projects
- Resource requirements
- Process improvement
- Communication
- Strategic goals
- Renewal
- Expansion

Priorities should come from verified customer information whenever possible.

---

# 2️⃣ Identify Problems

Identify current problems affecting customer success.

Examples:

- Delivery delays
- Quality concerns
- Communication gaps
- Resource constraints
- Unclear requirements
- Open issues
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Process problems

For each problem identify:

[Problem]

**Impact:**

**Evidence:**

# 3️⃣ Identify Risks

Review potential risks.

Examples:

- Customer health decline
- Renewal risk
- Revenue risk
- Relationship risk
- Delivery risk
- Payment risk
- Stakeholder risk
- Engagement risk

Create:

| Risk | Impact | Evidence | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Risk] | [Impact] | [Evidence] | [Priority] |

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Opportunities

Identify potential opportunities to improve customer outcomes.

Examples:

- Additional resources
- New projects
- New services
- Process improvements
- Better stakeholder engagement
- Expansion
- Cross-sell
- Upsell
- Customer advocacy
- Referral opportunity

Opportunities must be based on evidence or a genuine customer need.

---

# 5️⃣ Define Desired Outcomes

For each priority, define the desired outcome.

Examples:

> Resolve the current delivery issue.

> Improve communication with the customer.

> Support the customer's upcoming project requirements.

> Prepare the customer for renewal.

> Improve stakeholder engagement.

Create:

| Priority | Desired Outcome |
|---|---|
| [Priority] | [Outcome] |

---

# 6️⃣ Create Actions

Convert each outcome into specific actions.

Weak action:

> Improve communication.

Better action:

> Schedule a weekly 15-minute project status call with the customer.

Actions should be:

- Specific
- Practical
- Clear
- Relevant
- Trackable

---

# 7️⃣ Prioritize Actions

Use:

## 🔴 P1 — Immediate

Critical customer or business impact.

## 🟠 P2 — High

Important action requiring prompt attention.

## 🟡 P3 — Planned

Useful action that can be scheduled.

## 🟢 P4 — Monitor

No immediate action required.

Prioritize based on:

**Customer Impact + Risk + Urgency + Business Importance**

---

# 8️⃣ Assign Owners

Possible owners:

- CSM
- KAM
- Delivery
- Project Manager
- BDE / Sales
- Finance
- Technical Team
- Management
- Customer

Every important action should have an owner.

If ownership is not confirmed:

**Owner: To Be Confirmed**

Never guess.

---

# 9️⃣ Set Deadlines

Every important action should have a clear deadline where appropriate.

Create:

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [P1/P2/P3/P4] |

Use only confirmed dates.

Do not invent deadlines.

---

# 🔟 Define Success Measures

Every important action should have a way to determine whether it worked.

Examples:

### Action

Resolve delivery issue.

### Success Measure

Customer confirms delivery is back on track.

---

### Action

Improve communication.

### Success Measure

Customer receives agreed weekly updates.

---

### Action

Prepare renewal.

### Success Measure

Renewal discussion is completed and next steps are agreed.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Execute Actions

Track action status.

Possible statuses:

⚪ Not Started

🔵 In Progress

🟢 Completed

🟡 Delayed

🔴 Blocked

Update the status as work progresses.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Monitor Progress

Review:

- Completed actions
- Delayed actions
- Blocked actions
- Customer response
- New risks
- New opportunities
- Changes in customer priorities

Ask:

> Is the action still relevant?

Customer priorities can change.

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Review Outcomes

After actions are completed, determine:

- Was the problem solved?
- Was the customer outcome achieved?
- Did customer health improve?
- Did risk decrease?
- Did the opportunity progress?
- Is additional action required?

Create:

| Action | Result | Customer Impact | Next Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Result] | [Impact] | [Next Step] |

---

# 1️⃣4️⃣ Update Customer Plan

Update the plan when:

- Customer priorities change.
- Risks change.
- Opportunities change.
- Actions are completed.
- New actions are identified.
- Customer goals change.

The action plan should remain a living document.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Create actions without a clear reason.
- Assign owners without confirmation.
- Invent deadlines.
- Promise outcomes that are not approved.
- Treat potential opportunities as guaranteed.
- Ignore customer priorities.
- Keep outdated actions in the plan.
- Mark actions complete without evidence.
- Focus on activity instead of outcomes.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The goal is:

**Clear Action → Clear Owner → Clear Deadline → Clear Outcome**

# 💰🚀 Revenue Expansion Opportunity Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying, validating, prioritizing, and acting on genuine customer expansion opportunities.

The objective is to identify opportunities based on customer needs and business value rather than pushing unnecessary sales.

# 6️⃣ Identify Service Gaps

Look for services the customer may need but does not currently use.

Potential VBS services may include:

- BIM Modeling
- 3D Modeling
- Scan to BIM
- CAD to BIM
- Dedicated BIM Resources
- Additional project support
- Other approved services

Do not recommend a service simply because it exists.

The recommendation must connect to a customer need.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Expansion Signals

Possible signals:

### 🟢 Strong Signal

Customer directly asks for additional capacity, resources, services, or projects.

### 🟡 Moderate Signal

Customer discusses increasing workload, future projects, or capacity concerns.

### 🔵 Early Signal

Customer mentions a potential future need without a confirmed requirement.

### ⚪ No Signal

No evidence of an expansion opportunity.

Create:

| Signal | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| [Signal] | [Evidence] | [Strong / Moderate / Early / None] |

---

# 8️⃣ Validate Customer Need

Before treating an opportunity as real, validate the need.

Useful questions:

- What additional capacity will you need?
- What projects are coming up?
- When do you expect the requirement?
- What type of support would be most useful?
- How much additional capacity may be required?
- What problem would additional support solve?
- What timeline are you working toward?

Do not treat assumptions as confirmed requirements.

---

# 9️⃣ Assess Opportunity Fit

Assess whether the potential opportunity is a good fit.

Consider:

- Customer need
- VBS capability
- Timing
- Customer relationship
- Delivery capacity
- Strategic importance
- Commercial fit
- Customer value

# 🔟 Assess Expansion Readiness

Use:

### 🟢 Ready

Customer need is confirmed and timing is clear.

### 🟡 Developing

Customer need appears real but requires further validation.

### 🔵 Early Signal

Potential opportunity exists but is not yet confirmed.

### ⚪ No Opportunity

No meaningful expansion signal identified.

Do not classify an opportunity as "Ready" without evidence.

# 🧠 Example

### Customer Situation

A customer tells the CSM:

> "We have three large projects starting next quarter and our internal BIM team may not have enough capacity."

### Analysis

**Customer Need:**

Additional BIM capacity.

**Expansion Signal:**

🟢 Strong.

**Evidence:**

Customer identified three upcoming projects and an internal capacity concern.

**Potential Solution:**

Additional BIM support or dedicated resources, subject to customer requirements.

**Timing:**

Next quarter.

**Readiness:**

🟡 Developing — requirement still needs to be validated.

**Next Action:**

Schedule a discovery conversation to understand project volume, timeline, required skills, and expected capacity.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Force an upsell.
- Invent customer needs.
- Invent project requirements.
- Invent revenue.
- Promise pricing without approval.
- Treat early signals as confirmed opportunities.
- Recommend services without customer need.
- Ignore delivery capacity.
- Prioritize revenue over customer value.
- Pressure customers into expansion.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Expansion should solve a real customer problem.**

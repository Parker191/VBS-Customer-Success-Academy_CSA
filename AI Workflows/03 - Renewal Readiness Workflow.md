# 🔄 Renewal Readiness Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing a customer for renewal by evaluating customer value, health, relationship strength, risks, stakeholder engagement, and future requirements.

The goal is not to assume renewal.

The goal is to identify what must be true for a healthy and well-prepared renewal conversation.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Renewal Date
      ↓
1. Collect Account Information
      ↓
2. Assess Customer Health
      ↓
3. Review Customer Value
      ↓
4. Assess Relationship Strength
      ↓
5. Identify Renewal Risks
      ↓
6. Identify Value Gaps
      ↓
7. Assess Renewal Readiness
      ↓
8. Create Renewal Action Plan
      ↓
9. Prepare Customer Conversation
      ↓
10. Validate Customer Intent
      ↓
11. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Account Information

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Renewal date
- Current services
- Current commercial arrangement
- Customer goals
- Success criteria
- Customer health
- Recent projects
- Delivery performance
- Customer feedback
- Stakeholders
- Decision makers
- Open issues
- Escalations
- Recent wins
- Value delivered
- Future requirements
- Known competitive risks

Do not assume missing information.

---

# 2️⃣ Assess Customer Health

Use:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Review:

- Overall health
- Delivery health
- Relationship health
- Engagement
- Customer sentiment
- Commercial risk
- Retention risk

Identify whether the account is:

🟢 Healthy  
🟡 Needs Attention  
🟠 At Risk  
🔴 Critical

Customer health should be based on evidence.

---

# 3️⃣ Review Customer Value

Use:

**QBR Builder & Customer Value Story Generator**

Document:

- Customer goals
- Work completed
- Outcomes achieved
- Performance improvements
- Customer wins
- Positive feedback
- Business value

Create:

| Customer Goal | Value Delivered | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

Do not claim value without evidence.

---

# 4️⃣ Assess Relationship Strength

Review:

- Executive relationship
- Day-to-day stakeholder relationship
- Decision-maker access
- Customer responsiveness
- Meeting participation
- Feedback quality
- Trust level
- Communication quality

Identify relationship gaps.

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Renewal Risks

Use:

**Renewal Readiness & Risk Analyzer**

Review:

- Customer dissatisfaction
- Delivery issues
- Unresolved escalations
- Weak stakeholder relationships
- Low engagement
- Value gaps
- Budget concerns
- Changing customer priorities
- Competitive alternatives
- Uncertain future demand

Create:

| Risk | Evidence | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not assume a risk without evidence.

---

# 6️⃣ Identify Value Gaps

Ask:

- Has the customer clearly experienced value?
- Are customer goals being achieved?
- Are success metrics being tracked?
- Are there unresolved problems?
- Is VBS demonstrating outcomes or only activities?
- Does the customer understand the value received?

Identify areas where value needs to be better demonstrated.

---

# 7️⃣ Assess Renewal Readiness

Classify the account:

### 🟢 Ready

Strong value, healthy relationship, manageable risks, and clear future requirements.

### 🟡 Partially Ready

Positive signals exist but important information or actions are still missing.

### 🟠 At Risk

Significant concerns must be addressed before renewal.

### 🔴 Critical

Serious customer, relationship, commercial, contractual, or delivery risks require immediate attention.

Do not classify based on a single metric.

---

# 8️⃣ Create Renewal Action Plan

Create:

| Priority | Action | Owner | Deadline | Success Measure | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prioritize:

1. Customer-impacting issues
2. Value gaps
3. Relationship gaps
4. Renewal blockers
5. Future planning

Only use confirmed owners and deadlines.

If unknown:

**To be confirmed**

---

# 9️⃣ Prepare Customer Conversation

Use:

**Customer Communication Quality Reviewer**

Prepare a customer-focused conversation covering:

### Opening

Confirm the customer's experience and priorities.

### Value Review

Discuss outcomes and value delivered.

### Feedback

Ask what is working and what should improve.

### Future

Understand upcoming priorities and requirements.

### Renewal

Discuss continued needs and expectations.

### Next Steps

Agree on actions and timelines.

---

# 🔟 Validate Customer Intent

Do not assume renewal intent.

Ask appropriate questions such as:

- How are you evaluating the current engagement?
- What is working well?
- What would you like us to improve?
- What are your priorities for the next period?
- Do you anticipate changes in project volume?
- Are there any concerns we should address before renewal?
- What would make continuing the partnership valuable for you?

Document confirmed customer feedback.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Assume renewal.
- Invent customer intent.
- Invent customer value.
- Create unauthorized commercial commitments.
- Promise discounts.
- Ignore unresolved issues.
- Treat health as the only renewal indicator.
- Pressure the customer into renewal.
- Send AI-generated communication without CSM review.

The objective is to create a healthy, value-based renewal—not simply to close a renewal.

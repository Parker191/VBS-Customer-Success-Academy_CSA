# 🔍📊 Customer Account Review Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for conducting a complete review of an individual customer account.

The objective is to give the CSM a clear view of customer health, relationships, delivery, feedback, risks, revenue, renewal readiness, opportunities, and required actions.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Account
      ↓
1. Review Customer Profile
      ↓
2. Review Stakeholders
      ↓
3. Review Customer Health
      ↓
4. Review Projects and Delivery
      ↓
5. Review Customer Feedback
      ↓
6. Review Issues and Escalations
      ↓
7. Review Revenue and Billing
      ↓
8. Review Renewal Status
      ↓
9. Review Growth Opportunities
      ↓
10. Identify Risks
      ↓
11. Create Action Plan
      ↓
12. Assign Overall Account Health
      ↓
13. Create Account Summary
      ↓
14. Update CRM
```

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Assign Overall Account Health

Consider all available evidence.

### 🟢 Green — Healthy

Customer relationship and delivery are performing well.

### 🟡 Yellow — Needs Attention

Some issues exist but are manageable.

### 🟠 Orange — At Risk

Meaningful concerns could affect retention or customer success.

### 🔴 Red — Critical

Immediate intervention is required.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information to make a reliable assessment.

Do not assign health based on one issue alone.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer information.
- Invent revenue.
- Invent health scores.
- Treat potential revenue as confirmed.
- Ignore negative feedback.
- Hide important risks.
- Assume renewal is guaranteed.
- Assign account health without evidence.
- Share unnecessary confidential information.
- Replace CSM judgment with AI output.

A complete account review should be:

**Evidence-Based + Balanced + Customer-Focused + Action-Oriented**

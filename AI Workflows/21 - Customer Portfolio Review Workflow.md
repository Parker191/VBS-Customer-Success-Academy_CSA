# 📈 Customer Portfolio Review Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for reviewing an entire customer portfolio and prioritizing CSM attention.

The objective is to help the CSM identify which customers require immediate attention, which customers are healthy, where risks exist, and where growth or retention opportunities may exist.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
All Customers
      ↓
1. Collect Portfolio Data
      ↓
2. Review Customer Health
      ↓
3. Review Customer Engagement
      ↓
4. Review Delivery
      ↓
5. Review Revenue
      ↓
6. Review Renewal Status
      ↓
7. Review Customer Feedback
      ↓
8. Identify Risks
      ↓
9. Identify Opportunities
      ↓
10. Rank Customer Priorities
      ↓
11. Create Portfolio Action Plan
      ↓
12. Assign CSM Actions
      ↓
13. Review Progress
      ↓
14. Update CRM / Dashboard
```

# 5️⃣ Review Revenue

Where relevant, review:

- Current revenue
- Revenue trend
- Revenue at risk
- Expansion opportunities
- Upsell opportunities
- Cross-sell opportunities
- Payment concerns

**Potential Revenue**

- Days until renewal
- Renewal status
- Customer health
- Customer value
- Open issues
- Customer satisfaction
- Future requirements
- Renewal risk

Prioritize customers where:

**High renewal importance + High risk**

# 🔟 Rank Customer Priorities

Assign a priority level.

## 🔴 Priority 1 — Immediate Attention

Examples:

- Critical health
- Major escalation
- Serious customer dissatisfaction
- High renewal risk
- Significant revenue risk
- Major unresolved issue

These customers require immediate CSM attention.

---

## 🟠 Priority 2 — High Attention

Examples:

- Declining health
- Low engagement
- Upcoming renewal
- Important unresolved issue
- Significant growth opportunity
- Delivery concern

These customers should be actively managed.

---

## 🟡 Priority 3 — Planned Attention

Examples:

- Minor concerns
- Moderate engagement
- Upcoming customer review
- Early-stage opportunity
- Routine follow-up required

These customers need planned action.

---

## 🟢 Priority 4 — Monitor

Examples:

- Healthy customer
- Strong engagement
- Stable delivery
- No major risks
- No urgent actions

Continue normal customer-success cadence.

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Assign CSM Actions

Examples:

- Schedule customer meeting.
- Contact inactive customer.
- Resolve delivery concern.
- Coordinate internal escalation.
- Prepare renewal conversation.

- Engage additional stakeholder.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Automatically label every quiet customer as at risk.
- Rank customers using revenue alone.
- Ignore low-revenue customers with serious health risks.
- Treat potential revenue as confirmed revenue.
- Invent customer health information.
- Ignore renewal risks.
- Ignore customer feedback.
- Prioritize activity over customer impact.
- Hide important risks.
- Change customer health without evidence.

Portfolio prioritization should consider:

**Customer Health + Risk + Engagement + Revenue + Renewal + Customer Impact**

No single metric should determine the priority by itself.

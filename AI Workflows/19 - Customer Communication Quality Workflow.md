# 💬✨ Customer Communication Quality Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for reviewing and improving customer-facing communication.

The objective is to ensure customer emails and messages are clear, professional, accurate, respectful, customer-focused, and aligned with the intended outcome.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Draft Message
     ↓
1. Identify Communication Purpose
     ↓
2. Review Customer Context
     ↓
3. Check Accuracy
     ↓
4. Check Tone
     ↓
5. Check Clarity
     ↓
6. Check Customer Impact
     ↓
7. Check Call-to-Action
     ↓
8. Check Professionalism
     ↓
9. Improve Message
     ↓
10. CSM Final Review
     ↓
11. Send
     ↓
12. Record Important Communication
```

---

# 1️⃣ Identify Communication Purpose

Determine why the message is being sent.

Possible purposes:

- Follow-up
- Customer update
- Meeting invitation
- Feedback request
- Issue resolution
- Escalation response
- Invoice follow-up
- Renewal communication
- Project update
- Thank-you message
- Introduction
- Check-in
- Request for information

The message should have a clear purpose.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Customer Context

Before reviewing the message, consider:

- Customer relationship
- Recent conversations
- Customer health
- Current projects
- Open issues
- Previous commitments
- Customer preferences
- Communication history

A message should make sense in the context of the existing relationship.

---

# 3️⃣ Check Accuracy

Verify:

- Names
- Dates
- Invoice information
- Project information
- Commitments
- Deadlines
- Numbers
- Service information
- Meeting details

Do not allow AI to invent missing information.

If information is unknown:

**Mark it for confirmation.**

---

# 4️⃣ Check Tone

The tone should match the situation.

Possible tones:

### Friendly

For normal check-ins and relationship building.

### Professional

For regular business communication.

### Empathetic

For customer concerns or difficult situations.

### Clear and Firm

For important deadlines or repeated issues.

### Executive

For senior stakeholders and leadership communication.

Avoid:

- Aggressive language
- Blame
- Sarcasm
- Unnecessary pressure
- Defensive language
- Overly casual language
- Excessive jargon

---

# 5️⃣ Check Clarity

Ask:

- Is the purpose obvious?
- Is the message easy to understand?
- Are important details easy to find?
- Are sentences too long?
- Is unnecessary information included?
- Does the customer know what is being requested?

Use simple language where possible.

---

# 6️⃣ Check Customer Impact

Ask:

> How might the customer feel after reading this?

Check for:

- Respect
- Empathy
- Clarity
- Confidence
- Professionalism
- Appropriate urgency

Avoid making the customer feel blamed or ignored.

---

# 7️⃣ Check Call-to-Action

Every action-oriented message should make the next step clear.

Examples:

> Could you please confirm a convenient time?

> Please let us know if the invoice has been processed.

> Could you share the updated requirements?

> Please confirm whether the proposed timeline works for you.

Avoid vague requests.

---

# 8️⃣ Check Professionalism

Review:

- Grammar
- Spelling
- Formatting
- Greeting
- Closing
- Sentence structure
- Professional language

The message should be concise without becoming cold.

# 🔟 CSM Final Review

Before sending, the CSM should confirm:

- Is the information accurate?
- Is the message appropriate?
- Is the tone correct?
- Is the customer request clear?
- Are commitments approved?
- Are dates correct?
- Are sensitive details handled appropriately?
- Does the message sound like a human CSM?
- Would I be comfortable sending this to the customer?

AI should never automatically send customer communication without appropriate human review.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Send

After the CSM approves the message:

- Send through the approved communication channel.
- Use the appropriate recipients.
- Check attachments.
- Check CC / BCC.
- Confirm the correct customer contact.
- Confirm the subject line.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent facts.
- Change customer commitments.
- Make unauthorized promises.
- Use aggressive language.
- Blame the customer.
- Share unnecessary confidential information.
- Send AI-generated communication without review.
- Change the customer's intended meaning.
- Make a customer concern sound less serious than it is.

The goal is not to make every message sound perfect.

The goal is to make every message **clear, professional, accurate, and human**.

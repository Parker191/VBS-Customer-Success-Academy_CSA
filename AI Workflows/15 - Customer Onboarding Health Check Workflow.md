# 🚀❤️ Customer Onboarding Health Check Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for checking whether a newly onboarded customer is progressing successfully.

The objective is to identify onboarding progress, customer engagement, communication gaps, delivery concerns, risks, and corrective actions before small problems become larger customer-success issues.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
New Customer
     ↓
1. Confirm Onboarding Status
     ↓
2. Review Onboarding Milestones
     ↓
3. Review Customer Engagement
     ↓
4. Review Communication
     ↓
5. Review Initial Delivery
     ↓
6. Review Customer Understanding
     ↓
7. Identify Risks
     ↓
8. Assign Health Status
     ↓
9. Create Corrective Actions
     ↓
10. Confirm Next Steps
     ↓
11. Update CRM
     ↓
12. Continue Monitoring
```

---

# 1️⃣ Confirm Onboarding Status

Verify:

- Customer
- Onboarding start date
- Services
- Assigned CSM
- Assigned internal team
- Key stakeholders
- Kickoff date
- Current onboarding stage
- Expected onboarding completion date

Do not assume missing information.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Onboarding Milestones

Check whether important onboarding activities are complete.

Examples:

- Contract / commercial confirmation
- Customer introduction
- Kickoff meeting
- Requirements collection
- Scope confirmation
- Communication channels established
- Delivery process explained
- Team introductions
- First deliverable
- Feedback collection
- Success criteria confirmed

Create:

| Milestone | Expected Date | Actual Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Milestone] | [Date] | [Date] | [Status] |

Possible statuses:

🟢 Complete

🔵 In Progress

🟡 Delayed

🔴 Blocked

⚪ Not Started

---

# 3️⃣ Review Customer Engagement

Review:

- Customer responsiveness
- Meeting attendance
- Stakeholder participation
- Requirement sharing
- Feedback provided
- Questions raised
- Responsiveness to requests

Classify:

🟢 Strong

🟡 Moderate

🟠 Declining

🔴 Very Low

⚪ Unknown

Do not assume low engagement automatically means dissatisfaction.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Communication

Check:

- Communication frequency
- Response time
- Clarity of communication
- Customer understanding
- Internal coordination
- Follow-up completion

Identify:

### Positive

Communication is clear and consistent.

### Needs Attention

Some communication gaps exist.

### At Risk

Important communication problems are affecting onboarding.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Initial Delivery

Review the customer's first experience with delivery.

Check:

- First deliverable
- Quality
- Timeliness
- Accuracy
- Customer feedback
- Rework
- Open issues
- Delivery expectations

Ask:

> Has the customer's first experience matched what was promised?

Classify:

🟢 On Track

🟡 Needs Attention

🟠 At Risk

🔴 Critical

---

# 6️⃣ Review Customer Understanding

Confirm that the customer understands:

- What VBS provides
- Who their main contacts are
- How communication works
- How work is submitted
- How feedback is provided
- Expected timelines
- Escalation process
- Reporting / meeting cadence
- Success criteria

If important information is unclear, create a corrective action.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Risks

Potential onboarding risks include:

- Missing requirements
- Delayed kickoff
- Poor customer engagement
- Unclear scope
- Delivery delays
- Quality concerns
- Communication gaps
- Missing stakeholders
- Unresolved questions
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Internal resource constraints

Classify:

🔴 High Risk

🟠 Medium Risk

🟡 Low Risk

🟢 No Significant Risk

Every significant risk should have supporting evidence.

---

# 8️⃣ Assign Health Status

Use:

### 🟢 Green — Healthy

Onboarding is progressing as expected.

### 🟡 Yellow — Needs Attention

Minor issues exist but can be corrected.

### 🟠 Orange — At Risk

Meaningful issues could affect onboarding success.

### 🔴 Red — Critical

Immediate intervention is required.

Create:

| Customer | Milestones | Engagement | Delivery | Communication | Overall Health |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer] | [Status] | [Status] | [Status] | [Status] | [Health] |

Do not assign health based on a single metric.

---

# 9️⃣ Create Corrective Actions

For every meaningful issue, define:

- Problem
- Action
- Owner
- Deadline
- Expected outcome

Create:

| Problem | Action | Owner | Deadline | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Problem] | [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Outcome] |

---

# 🔟 Confirm Next Steps

Before closing the onboarding health review, confirm:

- Next customer action
- Next VBS action
- Next meeting
- Open questions
- Pending deliverables
- Expected milestones

Make sure responsibilities are clear.

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Continue Monitoring

Repeat the onboarding health check regularly until onboarding is successfully completed.

Look for:

- Health improvement
- Health decline
- New risks
- Closed risks
- Milestone completion
- Customer confidence
- Delivery stability

Once onboarding is complete, transition the customer into the regular Customer Health Review process.

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Assume onboarding is healthy because all meetings happened.
- Assume customer silence means satisfaction.
- Ignore poor first-delivery experiences.
- Invent customer concerns.
- Invent milestone completion.
- Assign health without evidence.
- Leave corrective actions without owners.
- Close onboarding before success criteria are reasonably met.

The goal is to create a strong customer foundation.

The workflow should help the CSM move from:

The goal is to identify onboarding problems early and create a smooth transition into long-term customer success management.

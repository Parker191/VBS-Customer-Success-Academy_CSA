# 📊👔 Executive Management Reporting Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for converting CSM portfolio information into a concise management-level report.

The objective is to help leadership quickly understand customer health, performance, risks, revenue-related activity, achievements, challenges, and required actions.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
CSM Data
   ↓
1. Collect Reporting Data
   ↓
2. Review Portfolio
   ↓
3. Review KPIs
   ↓
4. Review Customer Health
   ↓
5. Identify Key Wins
   ↓
6. Identify Risks
   ↓
7. Review Revenue Activity
   ↓
8. Identify Challenges
   ↓
9. Identify Required Actions
   ↓
10. Create Executive Summary
   ↓
11. CSM Review
   ↓
12. Share Report
   ↓
13. Update Records
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Reporting Data

Collect available information for the reporting period:

- Reporting period
- Total accounts
- Active customers
- Dormant customers
- At-risk customers
- Customer meetings
- Follow-ups
- Customer feedback
- Escalations
- Renewals
- Expansion opportunities
- Revenue activity
- Invoice / payment follow-ups
- Major customer wins
- Major customer concerns
- Open action items

Use verified information.

Do not invent missing data.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Portfolio

Review the overall customer portfolio.

Identify:

- Healthy customers
- Customers needing attention
- At-risk customers
- Dormant customers
- Customers showing improvement
- Customers showing declining engagement
- Strategic accounts requiring attention

Create:

| Category | Count | Key Observation |
|---|---:|---|
| Healthy | [Count] | [Observation] |
| Needs Attention | [Count] | [Observation] |
| At Risk | [Count] | [Observation] |
| Dormant | [Count] | [Observation] |

---

# 3️⃣ Review KPIs

Review relevant CSM KPIs.

Possible KPIs:

- Customer retention
- Revenue retention
- Customer health
- Customer meetings
- Proactive check-ins
- Follow-up completion
- Customer feedback
- Escalations
- Renewal progress
- Expansion activity
- Invoice follow-up
- Dormant customer reactivation

Create:

| KPI | Target | Actual | Status | Comment |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| [KPI] | [Target] | [Actual] | [Status] | [Comment] |

Only use actual available data.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Customer Health

Review:

- Green customers
- Yellow customers
- Orange customers
- Red customers
- Health movement from previous period

Identify:

### Improving

Customers whose health has improved.

### Stable

Customers whose health has remained relatively stable.

### Declining

Customers whose health has deteriorated.

Create:

| Customer | Previous Health | Current Health | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer] | [Status] | [Status] | [Change] | [Reason] |

---

# 5️⃣ Identify Key Wins

Identify meaningful achievements such as:

- Customer successfully onboarded
- At-risk customer stabilized
- Customer reactivated
- Renewal progressed
- Expansion opportunity identified
- Escalation resolved
- Positive customer feedback
- Important project completed
- Relationship strengthened
- Payment successfully resolved

Focus on outcomes rather than activity.

Example:

### Weak

> Sent 15 follow-up emails.

### Better

> Re-engaged three previously inactive customers and established follow-up meetings.

---

# 6️⃣ Identify Risks

Identify the most important risks.

Possible risks:

- Customer dissatisfaction
- Delivery problems
- Escalations
- Renewal risk
- Revenue risk
- Payment risk
- Reduced engagement
- Dormant accounts
- Stakeholder changes
- Resource issues

Create:

| Risk | Customer | Impact | Current Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Risk] | [Customer] | [Impact] | [Status] | [Action] |

Focus on the **top risks**, not every minor issue.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Revenue Activity

Where relevant, review:

- Revenue retained
- Renewal opportunities
- Expansion opportunities
- Cross-sell opportunities
- Upsell activity
- Payment follow-ups
- Revenue at risk

Do not exaggerate opportunities.

A potential opportunity should be clearly labeled as:

**Potential**

rather than confirmed revenue.

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Challenges

Identify the main challenges affecting CSM performance.

Examples:

- Customer responsiveness
- Delivery coordination
- Internal dependencies
- CRM data quality
- Resource availability
- Process gaps
- Payment delays
- Lack of customer engagement
- Unclear customer requirements

Focus on challenges that require management awareness or action.

---

# 9️⃣ Identify Required Actions

Create:

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Reason] |

Separate:

**CSM Actions**

from

**Management Support Required**

This helps leadership understand where intervention is needed.

# 1️⃣1️⃣ CSM Review

Before sharing the report:

Check:

- Are all numbers accurate?
- Are customer names correct?
- Are health statuses supported by evidence?
- Are risks clearly explained?
- Are opportunities clearly labeled?
- Are sensitive details handled appropriately?
- Is the report concise?
- Are management requests clear?

AI-generated reports must be reviewed by the CSM.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Share Report

Share through the appropriate internal channel.

Possible formats:

- Weekly CSM report
- Monthly management report
- Leadership update
- Portfolio review
- Management meeting
- Executive summary

Use the format required by VBS.

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Update Records

After reporting:

- Update CRM where necessary.
- Update customer health.
- Record important risks.
- Record action items.
- Update renewal opportunities.
- Update expansion opportunities.
- Track management actions.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent numbers.
- Hide important risks.
- Exaggerate customer success.
- Present potential revenue as confirmed revenue.
- Change customer health without evidence.
- Include unnecessary confidential information.
- Create misleading executive summaries.
- Blame customers or internal teams without evidence.

Executive reporting should be:

**Accurate + Concise + Evidence-Based + Action-Oriented**

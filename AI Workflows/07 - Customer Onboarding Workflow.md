# 🚀 Customer Onboarding Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for onboarding a new VBS customer from initial setup through successful transition into the normal Customer Success cadence.

The objective is to establish clear expectations, understand customer goals, align stakeholders, define success, and create a strong foundation for the relationship.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
New Customer
      ↓
1. Collect Account Information
      ↓
2. Map Stakeholders
      ↓
3. Understand Customer Goals
      ↓
4. Prepare Kickoff
      ↓
5. Define Success Criteria
      ↓
6. Create 30/60/90-Day Plan
      ↓
7. Coordinate Initial Delivery
      ↓
8. Collect Early Feedback
      ↓
9. Review Customer Health
      ↓
10. Confirm Onboarding Completion
      ↓
11. Transition to Normal CSM Cadence
      ↓
12. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Account Information

Collect:

- Customer name or anonymized identifier
- Account type
- Services purchased
- Engagement model
- Project scope
- Start date
- Expected delivery timeline
- Commercial arrangement
- Primary contact
- Decision maker
- Key stakeholders
- Customer goals
- Success criteria
- Known requirements
- Known risks
- Previous customer history where applicable

Do not assume missing information.

# 7️⃣ Coordinate Initial Delivery

Review:

- Project kickoff
- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Resources
- Dependencies
- Communication process
- Review process
- Approval process

Coordinate with appropriate VBS teams.

Do not promise delivery dates or resources without confirmation.

---

# 8️⃣ Collect Early Feedback

Use:

**Customer Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer**

Look for:

- Customer satisfaction
- Delivery experience
- Communication quality
- Quality concerns
- Process concerns
- Positive feedback
- Unresolved questions
- Expectations not being met

Classify early feedback:

🟢 Positive

🟡 Neutral / Mixed

🟠 Concern

🔴 Serious Risk

---

# 9️⃣ Review Customer Health

Use:

**Customer Health Risk Analyzer**

Review:

- Delivery
- Engagement
- Relationship
- Communication
- Customer sentiment
- Early outcomes
- Risk indicators

Do not automatically classify a new customer as Green simply because onboarding is progressing.

Use evidence.

# 🔟 Confirm Onboarding Completion

Onboarding can be considered complete when:

- [ ] Customer goals are documented.
- [ ] Stakeholders are identified.
- [ ] Communication cadence is established.
- [ ] Success criteria are defined.
- [ ] Delivery process is understood.
- [ ] Initial delivery has been completed or is progressing appropriately.
- [ ] Early feedback has been collected.
- [ ] Risks are documented.
- [ ] Customer health has been reviewed.
- [ ] Next-stage success plan is established.

Onboarding completion should reflect customer readiness—not simply the passage of time.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer goals.
- Assume stakeholder responsibilities.
- Promise delivery timelines without confirmation.
- Promise resources without approval.
- Mark onboarding complete simply because a project started.
- Ignore early customer concerns.
- Treat onboarding as only an administrative process.
- Send AI-generated customer communication without CSM review.

The purpose of onboarding is to establish a strong foundation for long-term customer success.

# 🎯 Desired Outcome

The workflow should help the CSM move from:

**New Customer → Alignment → Kickoff → Delivery → Feedback → Health → Success → Ongoing Relationship**

The goal is to ensure the customer understands how VBS will support them and that the CSM understands what success means from the customer's perspective.

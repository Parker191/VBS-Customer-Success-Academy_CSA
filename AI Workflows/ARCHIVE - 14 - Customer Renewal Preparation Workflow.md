# 🔄🎯 Customer Renewal Preparation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing for customer renewals.

The objective is to understand customer value, relationship health, delivery performance, risks, goals, and renewal readiness before the renewal conversation.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Renewal Date
     ↓
1. Confirm Renewal Information
     ↓
2. Review Customer Health
     ↓
3. Review Customer Value
     ↓
4. Review Delivery Performance
     ↓
5. Review Customer Relationship
     ↓
6. Review Open Issues
     ↓
7. Review Customer Goals
     ↓
8. Identify Renewal Risks
     ↓
9. Identify Renewal Opportunities
     ↓
10. Create Renewal Plan
     ↓
11. Prepare Customer Conversation
     ↓
12. Track Renewal Decision
     ↓
13. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Confirm Renewal Information

Verify:

- Customer
- Contract / subscription
- Renewal date
- Current service
- Current scope
- Current commercial arrangement
- Renewal owner
- Relevant stakeholders
- Previous renewal information

Do not assume the renewal date or terms.

Use verified information.

---

# 2️⃣ Review Customer Health

Review:

- Current health
- Previous health
- Health trend
- Customer engagement
- Customer sentiment
- Open concerns
- Escalations

Classify:

🟢 Healthy

🟡 Needs Attention

🟠 At Risk

🔴 Critical Risk

⚪ Insufficient Information

---

# 3️⃣ Review Customer Value

Understand the value delivered to the customer.

Review:

- Completed projects
- Delivered work
- Business outcomes
- Customer goals achieved
- Time saved
- Operational improvements
- Quality improvements
- Relationship improvements
- Positive customer feedback

Focus on outcomes.

Avoid simply listing activities.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Delivery Performance

Review:

- Delivery timelines
- Quality
- Project completion
- Missed deadlines
- Rework
- Customer complaints
- Open issues
- Delivery improvements

Classify:

🟢 Strong

🟡 Acceptable

🟠 Needs Improvement

🔴 Poor / At Risk

---

# 5️⃣ Review Customer Relationship

Review:

- Key stakeholders
- Decision makers
- Executive relationships
- Customer engagement
- Meeting frequency
- Communication quality
- Responsiveness
- Relationship strength

Identify:

**Strong Relationship**

**Developing Relationship**

**Weak Relationship**

**Unknown**

Do not assume relationship strength from one interaction.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Open Issues

Identify unresolved:

- Delivery issues
- Quality issues
- Customer complaints
- Escalations
- Billing concerns
- Communication problems
- Resource concerns
- Technical issues

Create:

| Issue | Impact | Owner | Status | Resolution Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Issue] | [Impact] | [Owner] | [Status] | [Plan] |

Important unresolved issues should be addressed before renewal whenever possible.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Customer Goals

Understand:

- Current priorities
- Future projects
- Upcoming requirements
- Business changes
- Resource requirements
- Service expectations
- Potential challenges

Questions to consider:

> What does the customer need next?

> What has changed since the last contract?

> What would make renewal valuable for the customer?

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Renewal Risks

Potential risks include:

- Poor customer health
- Negative feedback
- Unresolved issues
- Declining engagement
- Reduced project volume
- Budget concerns
- Stakeholder changes
- Competitor activity
- Delivery dissatisfaction
- Unclear future requirements
- Lack of demonstrated value

Classify:

🔴 High Risk

🟠 Medium Risk

🟡 Low Risk

🟢 No Significant Risk Identified

Every significant risk should have evidence.

---

# 9️⃣ Identify Renewal Opportunities

Look for:

- Increased project demand
- Additional services
- Additional resources
- New teams
- New locations
- New project types
- Cross-sell opportunities
- Upsell opportunities
- Longer-term engagement

Potential opportunities must be clearly labeled as opportunities.

Do not treat them as confirmed revenue.

---

# 🔟 Create Renewal Plan

Create:

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Outcome] |

Typical actions:

- Schedule renewal discussion.
- Resolve open customer issues.
- Demonstrate delivered value.
- Confirm future requirements.
- Engage key stakeholders.
- Prepare commercial information.
- Coordinate with KAM / Sales.
- Prepare expansion options if appropriate.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Prepare Customer Conversation

Prepare questions such as:

### Value

> How has the current engagement supported your goals?

### Satisfaction

> How would you rate your experience so far?

### Future Needs

> What priorities or projects are coming up?

### Improvement

> Is there anything you would like us to improve?

### Renewal

> What would you need from us to feel confident continuing the partnership?

The goal is to understand—not pressure—the customer.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Assume renewal is guaranteed.
- Assume a healthy customer will automatically renew.
- Invent customer goals.
- Invent customer satisfaction.
- Ignore unresolved issues.
- Treat potential expansion as confirmed revenue.
- Pressure customers unnecessarily.
- Share confidential commercial information unnecessarily.
- Change renewal status without evidence.

Renewal preparation should focus on **customer value, relationship health, and future needs**.

The workflow should help the CSM move from:

**Renewal Date → Customer Value → Health → Risks → Future Needs → Renewal Plan → Customer Conversation → Renewal**

The goal is to make renewal a planned customer-success activity rather than a last-minute event.

# 💎 Customer Value Review Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for reviewing and documenting the value a customer receives from VBS.

The objective is to move beyond customer activity and identify measurable or evidence-based customer outcomes.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Goals
      ↓
1. Understand Customer Need
      ↓
2. Review VBS Services
      ↓
3. Identify Delivered Outcomes
      ↓
4. Identify Customer Benefits
      ↓
5. Measure Available Evidence
      ↓
6. Compare Goals vs Outcomes
      ↓
7. Identify Value Gaps
      ↓
8. Identify Value Opportunities
      ↓
9. Create Value Action Plan
      ↓
10. Communicate Customer Value
      ↓
11. Review Regularly
      ↓
12. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Understand Customer Need

Identify what the customer originally needed.

Possible needs:

- Additional project capacity
- Faster project delivery
- Cost efficiency
- Specialized BIM expertise
- Reduced internal workload
- Support for project volume
- Improved modeling capability
- Scan-to-BIM support
- CAD-to-BIM conversion
- Dedicated resources
- Flexible delivery capacity

Separate:

**Customer-Stated Need**

from

**CSM Interpretation**

Do not assume the customer's priorities.

---

# 2️⃣ Review VBS Services

Identify what VBS is currently providing.

Examples:

- BIM Modeling
- 3D Modeling
- Scan to BIM
- CAD to BIM
- Dedicated Resources
- Project-based support
- Ongoing delivery support

Document:

| Service | Usage | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| [Service] | [Usage] | [Purpose] |

---

# 3️⃣ Identify Delivered Outcomes

Review what has actually been delivered.

Examples:

- Projects completed
- Models delivered
- Hours delivered
- Resources provided
- Deadlines achieved
- Additional capacity supported
- Requirements completed

Do not confuse:

**Activity**

with

**Outcome**.

Example:

> 160 hours delivered

is an activity.

While:

> Customer completed an additional project using the supported capacity

is an outcome.

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Customer Benefits

Determine the benefit created for the customer.

Possible benefits:

- Saved internal time
- Increased project capacity
- Faster delivery
- Reduced workload
- Improved scalability
- Reduced hiring pressure
- Improved project support
- Better resource flexibility
- Improved operational efficiency

Only identify benefits supported by evidence.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Value Gaps

A value gap exists when the customer's desired outcome is not being fully achieved.

Possible causes:

- Service limitations
- Delivery issues
- Communication gaps
- Resource constraints
- Unclear requirements
- Customer-side dependencies
- Changing customer priorities
- Lack of measurement

Create:

| Value Gap | Impact | Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Gap] | [Impact] | [Cause] | [Action] |

Do not assign blame without evidence.

---

# 8️⃣ Identify Value Opportunities

Look for ways to create additional customer value.

Examples:

- Additional capacity
- New service
- Better workflow
- Improved reporting
- Additional resources
- Process improvement
- New project support
- Better stakeholder engagement

Potential expansion should only be recommended when it aligns with a genuine customer need.

# 🔟 Communicate Customer Value

When discussing value with the customer:

Focus on:

**Customer Goal**

→

**What VBS Did**

→

**Result**

→

**Business Benefit**

Example:

> Your team needed additional modeling capacity for the upcoming project workload. VBS supported the additional modeling requirements, helping your team handle the increased project volume without adding immediate internal capacity.

Only use claims supported by evidence.

Avoid:

- Exaggerated ROI
- Unsupported savings claims
- Guaranteed future outcomes
- Marketing language that does not reflect reality

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer outcomes.
- Invent ROI.
- Invent savings.
- Treat activity as customer value.
- Claim customer success without evidence.
- Exaggerate VBS impact.
- Promise future value.
- Use potential value as confirmed value.
- Ignore customer-stated goals.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important question is:

> **“Did VBS help the customer achieve something that matters to them?”**

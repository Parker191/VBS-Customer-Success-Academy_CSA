# 👥 Customer Stakeholder Mapping Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for identifying, understanding, and managing important stakeholders within a customer account.

The objective is to help the CSM build relationships across the customer organization instead of relying on a single contact.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Account
      ↓
1. Collect Stakeholder Information
      ↓
2. Identify Stakeholder Roles
      ↓
3. Map Influence
      ↓
4. Understand Stakeholder Priorities
      ↓
5. Assess Relationship Strength
      ↓
6. Identify Gaps
      ↓
7. Create Engagement Plan
      ↓
8. Assign Actions
      ↓
9. Monitor Stakeholder Changes
      ↓
10. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Stakeholder Information

Identify available customer stakeholders.

Possible information:

- Name
- Job title
- Department
- Role in the relationship
- Contact information where appropriate
- Seniority
- Customer priorities
- Communication preference
- Relationship with VBS
- Last interaction
- Next planned interaction

Only use information that is appropriate and authorized for business use.

---

# 2️⃣ Identify Stakeholder Roles

Classify stakeholders where evidence supports the classification.

Possible roles:

### 👑 Decision Maker

Person who can approve major decisions, budgets, or continuation of the relationship.

### 🎯 Champion

Person who actively supports VBS and helps promote the relationship internally.

### 👤 End User

Person who directly uses or interacts with the service.

### 🧠 Influencer

Person who can influence decisions or customer perception.

### 💼 Commercial Stakeholder

Person involved in pricing, contracts, purchasing, or financial decisions.

### 🛠️ Operational Stakeholder

Person involved in day-to-day project or delivery coordination.

### 🧑‍💼 Executive Stakeholder

Senior leader who may have strategic interest in the relationship.

### ❓ Unknown

Role is not yet confirmed.

Do not guess a stakeholder's role.

---

# 3️⃣ Map Influence

Assess stakeholder influence.

### 🔴 High Influence

Can significantly affect the customer relationship or decisions.

### 🟠 Medium Influence

Can influence important parts of the relationship.

### 🟢 Low Influence

Limited direct decision-making influence.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information.

Create:

| Stakeholder | Role | Influence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Person] | [Role] | [Level] | [Evidence] |

---

# 4️⃣ Understand Stakeholder Priorities

Identify what each important stakeholder cares about.

Possible priorities:

- Delivery speed
- Quality
- Cost
- Communication
- Capacity
- Project visibility
- Technical performance
- Reliability
- Business outcomes
- Strategic growth
- Risk reduction

Create:

| Stakeholder | Priority | Importance | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Person] | [Priority] | [High/Medium/Low] | [Evidence] |

Do not invent priorities.

If unknown:

**Priority: Unknown**

---

# 5️⃣ Assess Relationship Strength

Evaluate the current VBS relationship.

### 🟢 Strong

Regular interaction, trust, positive engagement.

### 🟡 Developing

Some engagement exists but relationship can be strengthened.

### 🟠 Weak

Limited engagement or unclear relationship.

### 🔴 At Risk

Relationship concerns or negative signals exist.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information.

Create:

| Stakeholder | Relationship | Last Interaction | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Person] | [Strength] | [Date] | [Action] |

---

# 6️⃣ Identify Relationship Gaps

Look for:

- No executive relationship
- No decision-maker relationship
- No champion
- Single-threaded relationship
- No commercial contact
- No operational backup
- Important stakeholder not contacted
- Stakeholder role unknown
- Stakeholder has become inactive

A major warning sign is:

### 🚨 Single-Threaded Account

The CSM depends heavily on one customer contact.

This should trigger an effort to build additional appropriate relationships.

---

# 7️⃣ Create Engagement Plan

Create an engagement strategy for important stakeholders.

Example:

| Stakeholder | Objective | Engagement Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision Maker | Build executive relationship | Business review | Quarterly |
| Champion | Strengthen partnership | Check-in | Monthly |
| End User | Understand experience | Feedback session | Monthly |
| Commercial | Support renewal | Commercial discussion | As needed |

Engagement frequency should match stakeholder importance and customer preferences.

Do not contact stakeholders excessively.

---

# 8️⃣ Assign Actions

Create specific actions.

Examples:

- Introduce CSM.
- Schedule executive check-in.
- Invite stakeholder to QBR.
- Request feedback.
- Share customer success update.
- Understand future priorities.
- Build relationship with backup contact.

Create:

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] | [Outcome] |

---

# 9️⃣ Monitor Stakeholder Changes

Review stakeholder mapping when:

- A stakeholder leaves.
- A new decision maker joins.
- Responsibilities change.
- Customer organization changes.
- Communication patterns change.
- A new project begins.
- Renewal approaches.
- Relationship health changes.

Update the stakeholder map when meaningful changes occur.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Guess someone's influence.
- Assume someone's job responsibilities.
- Invent stakeholder priorities.
- Contact stakeholders inappropriately.
- Ignore customer communication preferences.
- Collect unnecessary personal information.
- Share sensitive information unnecessarily.
- Assume the most senior person is always the most important stakeholder.
- Depend on only one customer relationship.

Stakeholder mapping should improve relationship coverage while respecting customer preferences and privacy.

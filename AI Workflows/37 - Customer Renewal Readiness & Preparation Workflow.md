# 🔄 Customer Renewal Readiness & Preparation Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for preparing a customer for renewal.

The workflow helps the CSM evaluate:

- Customer health
- Customer value
- Delivery performance
- Customer sentiment
- Engagement
- Stakeholder relationships
- Open issues
- Future customer needs
- Renewal risks
- Expansion opportunities
- Renewal readiness

The objective is not to assume that a customer will renew.

The objective is to understand what must be true for a healthy, value-based renewal.

1️⃣1️⃣ Assess Renewal Readiness

Use the following planning assessment:

🟢 Renewal Ready

Strong value, healthy relationship, manageable risks, and clear future needs.

🟡 Renewal Developing

Renewal appears achievable, but important actions or information are still required.

🟠 Renewal At Risk

Significant risks or unresolved concerns may affect renewal.

🔴 Critical Renewal Risk

Major customer, relationship, commercial, or delivery concerns exist.

⚪ Unknown

There is insufficient information to assess renewal readiness.

This is a CSM planning assessment, not a guaranteed prediction of customer behavior.

1️⃣3️⃣ Prepare Customer Conversation

Prepare a customer-focused conversation covering:

Opening

Confirm the customer's experience and priorities.

Value

Discuss demonstrated outcomes and value delivered.

Feedback

Ask what is working and what should improve.

Future Needs

Understand upcoming priorities, projects, and requirements.

Renewal

Understand the customer's expectations for continuing the partnership.

Next Steps

Agree on actions, owners, and timelines.

Useful questions include:

How has the current engagement supported your goals?

What is working well?

What would you like us to improve?

What priorities or projects are coming up?

What would make continuing the partnership valuable for you?

Do not pressure the customer into renewal.

⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

Assume renewal is guaranteed.
Invent customer intent.
Invent customer value.
Invent renewal dates.
Invent commercial information.
Treat potential revenue as confirmed revenue.
Ignore unresolved customer concerns.
Ignore stakeholder changes.
Promise unauthorized discounts.
Create unauthorized commercial commitments.
Pressure customers unnecessarily.
Share confidential information unnecessarily.
Send AI-generated communication without CSM review.

The objective is to create a healthy, value-based renewal aligned with customer needs.

# ⚡ Daily CSM Productivity Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for helping a Customer Success Manager prioritize daily work based on customer impact, urgency, risk, deadlines, revenue considerations, and business importance.

The objective is not to simply create a longer task list.

The objective is to identify the work that matters most today.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Today's Tasks
      ↓
1. Collect Tasks
      ↓
2. Identify Customer Impact
      ↓
3. Identify Urgency
      ↓
4. Review Customer Health
      ↓
5. Review Deadlines
      ↓
6. Review Revenue / Business Impact
      ↓
7. Identify Dependencies
      ↓
8. Prioritize Tasks
      ↓
9. Create Today's Action Plan
      ↓
10. Execute
      ↓
11. Review Progress
      ↓
12. Update CRM / Task Tracker
```

---

# 1️⃣ Collect Tasks

Collect tasks from:

- CRM
- Customer emails
- Teams
- Meeting action items
- Follow-up commitments
- Internal requests
- Delivery coordination
- Invoice / payment follow-ups
- Customer escalations
- Renewal activities
- Expansion opportunities
- Onboarding activities
- Reporting deadlines
- Management requests

Create:

| Task | Customer | Due Date | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Task] | [Customer] | [Date] | [Source] | [Status] |

Do not rely only on memory.

---

# 2️⃣ Identify Customer Impact

For each task, ask:

> What happens to the customer if this task is delayed?

Classify:

🟢 Low Customer Impact

🟡 Moderate Customer Impact

🟠 High Customer Impact

🔴 Critical Customer Impact

Customer-impacting tasks should generally receive higher priority.

---

# 3️⃣ Identify Urgency

Evaluate:

- Deadline
- Customer commitment
- Internal deadline
- Escalation status
- Time sensitivity
- Potential consequences of delay

Classify:

🔴 Urgent

🟠 High

🟡 Normal

🟢 Low

Urgency should be based on evidence—not simply how stressful the task feels.

---

# 4️⃣ Review Customer Health

Check whether the task relates to:

- At-risk customers
- Critical escalations
- Dormant customers
- Renewal-risk accounts
- High-value customers
- Important stakeholder relationships

Customer health can influence task priority.

However, do not automatically prioritize a task simply because the customer is important.

Consider the actual customer impact.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Deadlines

Identify:

- Customer deadlines
- Contract deadlines
- Renewal dates
- Meeting dates
- Invoice deadlines
- Delivery deadlines
- Internal commitments

Highlight tasks where missing the deadline creates a meaningful consequence.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Revenue / Business Impact

Consider:

- Revenue protection
- Renewal
- Expansion
- Customer retention
- Payment collection
- Strategic account activity

Examples:

```text
Renewal risk
     ↓
High priority

Critical customer issue
     ↓
High priority

Routine internal report
     ↓
Lower priority
```

Revenue should be considered alongside customer impact—not treated as the only priority.

---

# 7️⃣ Identify Dependencies

Identify whether the task depends on:

- Delivery team
- KAM
- Sales / BDE
- Finance
- Management
- Customer response
- Technical team
- Another task

Create:

| Task | Dependency | Person / Team | Waiting Since | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Task] | [Dependency] | [Team] | [Date] | [Action] |

This prevents the CSM from repeatedly working on something that cannot progress.

---

# 8️⃣ Prioritize Tasks

Use the following framework:

### 🔴 P1 — Critical

Immediate customer, relationship, financial, contractual, or escalation risk.

### 🟠 P2 — High

Important customer or business impact with meaningful deadline or consequence.

### 🟡 P3 — Normal

Important but not immediately time-sensitive.

### 🟢 P4 — Low

Administrative, optional, or lower-impact work.

Create:

| Priority | Task | Customer | Reason | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | [Task] | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Date] |
| P2 | [Task] | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Date] |
| P3 | [Task] | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Date] |
| P4 | [Task] | [Customer] | [Reason] | [Date] |

---

# 9️⃣ Create Today's Action Plan

Create a realistic plan.

### 🔴 Must Do Today

Maximum priority tasks.

### 🟠 Should Do Today

Important tasks that should be completed if possible.

### 🟡 Can Do Today

Useful tasks that can be completed after higher priorities.

### ⚪ Defer

Tasks that can safely move to another day.

Avoid creating an unrealistic schedule.

---

# 🔟 Execute

For each task:

1. Start with the highest-priority task.
2. Complete or progress the task.
3. Document important outcomes.
4. Move blocked tasks to the dependency list.
5. Continue to the next priority.

Avoid constantly switching between unrelated tasks.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Review Progress

At the end of the day ask:

- What was completed?
- What remains open?
- What became urgent?
- What is blocked?
- What customer commitments were completed?
- What customer commitments remain?
- What needs to move to tomorrow?
- Did any new risks appear?

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Update CRM / Task Tracker

Document:

- Completed customer actions
- Follow-ups
- Customer commitments
- Open actions
- New risks
- Escalations
- Renewal activities
- Expansion opportunities
- Next steps

Do not leave important customer activity undocumented.

---

# 📋 Daily CSM Checklist

- [ ] Review today's tasks.
- [ ] Review customer-impacting activities.
- [ ] Check critical escalations.
- [ ] Check at-risk customers.
- [ ] Check renewal deadlines.
- [ ] Check customer commitments.
- [ ] Review important follow-ups.
- [ ] Check dependencies.
- [ ] Prioritize P1–P4.
- [ ] Create realistic daily plan.
- [ ] Execute highest-priority work.
- [ ] Document customer actions.
- [ ] Review incomplete tasks.
- [ ] Prepare tomorrow's priorities.

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Prioritize only based on revenue.
- Ignore critical customer issues.
- Treat every task as urgent.
- Create an unrealistic daily plan.
- Forget customer commitments.
- Ignore blocked tasks.
- Delay important CRM documentation.
- Allow AI to decide business priorities without CSM review.

The goal is better prioritization—not simply doing more work.

# 🎯 Desired Outcome

The workflow should help the CSM move from:

**Task List → Impact → Urgency → Priority → Action → Documentation**

The goal is to ensure the CSM spends time on the work that creates the greatest customer and business impact.

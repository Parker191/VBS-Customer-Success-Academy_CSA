# ⚙️ AI Workflow Engine

> End-to-end AI-assisted workflows for Customer Success Managers at Virtual Building Studio (VBS).

## 🎯 Purpose

The AI Workflow Engine connects individual AI prompts into practical end-to-end Customer Success workflows.

Instead of using isolated prompts, workflows guide the CSM through a complete process from customer situation to recommended action and documentation.

## 🧠 Core Principle

AI assists — CSM decides.

The CSM must review AI-generated recommendations before taking customer-facing or business-critical actions.

## 🔄 Workflow Structure

```text
Customer Situation
       ↓
Information Collection
       ↓
AI Analysis
       ↓
Risk / Opportunity Identification
       ↓
Recommended Action
       ↓
Customer Communication
       ↓
Follow-Up
       ↓
CRM Documentation

# 🎯 Customer Success Plan Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for creating and managing Customer Success Plans.

The objective is to connect customer goals with measurable success criteria, agreed actions, ownership, timelines, and regular progress reviews.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Goals
      ↓
1. Understand Customer Situation
      ↓
2. Identify Customer Goals
      ↓
3. Define Success Criteria
      ↓
4. Identify Obstacles
      ↓
5. Create Success Actions
      ↓
6. Assign Owners
      ↓
7. Set Timelines
      ↓
8. Define Measurements
      ↓
9. Create Review Cadence
      ↓
10. Track Progress
      ↓
11. Adjust Plan
      ↓
12. Confirm Success
      ↓
13. Update CRM
```

---

# 1️⃣ Understand Customer Situation

Review:

- Current services
- Current projects
- Customer priorities
- Business objectives
- Current challenges
- Previous performance
- Customer expectations
- Stakeholders
- Existing relationship

Do not assume customer goals without evidence.

---

# 2️⃣ Identify Customer Goals

Identify what the customer wants to accomplish.

Goals may include:

- Improve delivery speed
- Improve quality
- Increase project capacity
- Reduce operational workload
- Improve communication
- Complete upcoming projects
- Expand service usage
- Improve consistency
- Reduce project delays
- Build a long-term delivery relationship

Separate:

**Customer-stated goals**

from

**CSM-recommended goals**

Do not present CSM recommendations as customer commitments.

---

# 3️⃣ Define Success Criteria

For each goal, define what success looks like.

Example:

| Customer Goal | Success Criteria |
|---|---|
| Improve delivery speed | Agreed project timelines consistently achieved |
| Improve communication | Regular progress updates provided |
| Increase capacity | Additional project workload supported successfully |

Success criteria should be measurable where possible.

---

# 4️⃣ Identify Obstacles

Identify potential barriers.

Examples:

- Resource constraints
- Unclear requirements
- Communication gaps
- Delivery delays
- Customer availability
- Internal dependencies
- Technical challenges
- Budget limitations
- Process gaps

Create:

| Goal | Obstacle | Impact | Proposed Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Goal] | [Obstacle] | [Impact] | [Action] |

Do not assume an obstacle exists without evidence.

---

# 5️⃣ Create Success Actions

Create actions that directly support customer goals.

Examples:

- Schedule regular progress reviews.
- Establish communication cadence.
- Review project requirements.
- Coordinate additional resources.
- Track delivery performance.
- Conduct quarterly success reviews.
- Share performance updates.
- Identify expansion opportunities when appropriate.

Create:

| Goal | Action | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [Goal] | [Action] | [Outcome] |

---

# 6️⃣ Assign Owners

Each action should have an owner.

Possible owners:

- CSM
- Customer
- KAM
- BDE / Sales
- Delivery Team
- Finance
- Technical Team
- Management

If ownership is unclear:

**Owner: To Be Confirmed**

Never guess.

---

# 7️⃣ Set Timelines

Define:

- Start date
- Target date
- Review date
- Milestones

Example:

| Action | Start Date | Target Date | Review Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Date] | [Date] | [Date] |

Clearly distinguish confirmed dates from recommended dates.

---

# 8️⃣ Define Measurements

Choose meaningful measures.

Possible measurements:

- Customer health
- Delivery performance
- Quality
- Response time
- Project completion
- Customer satisfaction
- Engagement
- Usage
- Renewal progress
- Expansion activity

Avoid measuring activity simply for the sake of having numbers.

The measurement should help determine whether the customer is achieving the desired outcome.

---

# 9️⃣ Create Review Cadence

Decide how often the Success Plan should be reviewed.

Possible cadence:

### Weekly

For active or high-priority initiatives.

### Monthly

For ongoing customer goals.

### Quarterly

For strategic customer success planning.

### Event-Based

When major changes occur.

Review cadence should match customer needs.

---

# 🔟 Track Progress

Use:

🟢 On Track

🟡 Needs Attention

🟠 At Risk

🔴 Blocked

⚪ Not Started

Create:

| Goal | Status | Progress | Latest Update | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Goal] | [Status] | [% / Description] | [Update] | [Action] |

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Adjust the Plan

Customer goals can change.

Review the Success Plan when:

- Customer priorities change.
- New projects appear.
- Existing projects are cancelled.
- Customer stakeholders change.
- Delivery conditions change.
- Business requirements change.
- Customer feedback indicates a different priority.

Do not keep an outdated success plan simply because it was previously agreed.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Confirm Success

A goal should be considered achieved when:

- Success criteria have been met.
- Evidence supports the outcome.
- Customer agrees where appropriate.
- The outcome is documented.
- Next priorities are identified.

Avoid declaring success based only on completed activities.

---

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Invent customer goals.
- Invent customer commitments.
- Create unrealistic success criteria.
- Treat activities as outcomes.
- Assume a customer achieved success without evidence.
- Keep outdated goals in the plan.
- Assign ownership without confirmation.
- Promise outcomes that VBS cannot guarantee.
- Share confidential customer information unnecessarily.

The Customer Success Plan should reflect the customer's priorities—not simply the CSM's activity list.

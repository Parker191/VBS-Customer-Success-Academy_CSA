# 📊 QBR Preparation & Execution Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured Customer Success workflow for preparing, conducting, and following up on a Quarterly Business Review (QBR).

The objective is to help the CSM demonstrate customer value, review performance against customer goals, identify risks and improvement areas, understand future priorities, and agree on clear next steps.

A QBR should be a **customer-value conversation**, not simply a presentation of VBS activities.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
QBR Date
    ↓
1. Confirm QBR Details
    ↓
2. Review Customer Context
    ↓
3. Review Customer Goals
    ↓
4. Collect Performance Data
    ↓
5. Review Customer Value
    ↓
6. Review Customer Health
    ↓
7. Review Customer Feedback
    ↓
8. Review Risks & Issues
    ↓
9. Identify Future Priorities
    ↓
10. Build QBR Value Story
    ↓
11. Prepare QBR Questions
    ↓
12. Create QBR Agenda
    ↓
13. Conduct QBR
    ↓
14. Capture Decisions & Actions
    ↓
15. Send Follow-Up
    ↓
16. Update CRM
    ↓
17. Track Follow-Through

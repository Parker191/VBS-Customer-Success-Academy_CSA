# 💬🎯 Customer Communication Prioritization Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for prioritizing customer communication and determining the appropriate communication objective, timing, channel, and next action.

The objective is to help CSMs communicate proactively while focusing attention on customers who require the most important or time-sensitive communication.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Customer Communication Queue
          ↓
1. Collect Communication Items
          ↓
2. Identify Customer
          ↓
3. Understand Communication Purpose
          ↓
4. Review Customer Health
          ↓
5. Review Urgency
          ↓
6. Review Customer Impact
          ↓
7. Review Relationship Importance
          ↓
8. Review Renewal / Revenue Context
          ↓

          ↓
10. Select Communication Objective
          ↓
11. Select Appropriate Channel
          ↓
12. Prepare Communication
          ↓
13. Review Before Sending
          ↓

          ↓
15. Record Outcome
```

# 3️⃣ Understand Communication Purpose

Every customer communication should have a clear purpose.

Possible purposes:

- Follow-up
- Status update
- Feedback request
- Meeting request
- Issue resolution
- Escalation response
- Renewal discussion
- Expansion discovery
- Relationship building
- Check-in
- Thank-you
- Information request
- Expectation setting

If the purpose is unclear:

**Purpose: Needs Clarification**

---

# 4️⃣ Review Customer Health

Review:

- Health status
- Health trend
- Sentiment
- Delivery
- Relationship
- Engagement
- Open issues

Possible states:

🟢 Green

🟡 Yellow

🟠 Orange

🔴 Red

⚪ Unknown

Customer health should influence communication priority but should not be the only factor.

---

# 5️⃣ Review Urgency

Classify urgency.

## 🔴 Immediate

Communication should happen as soon as reasonably possible.

Examples:

- Critical escalation
- Customer-impacting issue
- Time-sensitive deadline
- Major delivery concern

## 🟠 High

Communication should happen promptly.

Examples:

- Important customer concern
- Renewal preparation
- Important follow-up
- Significant project update

## 🟡 Normal

Communication should happen within the normal cadence.

Examples:

- Regular check-in
- Feedback request
- Standard follow-up

## 🟢 Low

Communication can be planned around higher-priority work.

Examples:

- General relationship touchpoint
- Non-urgent outreach

Do not classify everything as urgent.

---

# 6️⃣ Review Customer Impact

Ask:

> What happens if this communication is delayed?

Possible impact:

🟢 Low

Little immediate impact.

🟡 Medium

Customer experience may be affected.

🟠 High

Project, relationship, or business impact is possible.

🔴 Critical

Significant customer or business impact may occur.

---

# 7️⃣ Review Relationship Importance

Consider:

- Customer relationship
- Stakeholder importance
- Strategic importance
- Current engagement
- Renewal timing
- Customer value
- Existing risks

Do not prioritize communication based only on revenue.

---

# 8️⃣ Review Renewal / Revenue Context

Consider:

- Upcoming renewal
- Revenue at risk
- Expansion opportunity
- New project opportunity
- Contract discussion
- Commercial issue

Separate:

**Confirmed**

from

**Potential**

Never present potential revenue as confirmed.

---

# 9️⃣ Determine Communication Priority

Use:

### 🔴 P1 — Critical

Immediate or highly time-sensitive communication.

### 🟠 P2 — High

Important communication that should happen promptly.

### 🟡 P3 — Normal

Regular customer communication.

### 🟢 P4 — Low

Communication that can be scheduled after higher-priority work.

# 🔟 Select Communication Objective

Before writing anything, define:

**Objective:**

[Objective]

Example:

Instead of:

> Send follow-up email.

Use:

> Obtain confirmation of the customer's expected project start date.

A clear objective produces better communication.

---

# 1️⃣1️⃣ Select Appropriate Channel

Possible channels:

- Email
- Teams
- Phone
- Video meeting
- CRM task
- Internal escalation

Choose the channel based on:

- Urgency
- Complexity
- Customer preference
- Sensitivity
- Need for documentation

For complex or sensitive matters, a conversation may be better than a long email.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Prepare Communication

Use:

**Context → Purpose → Key Message → Action → Timeline**

Example:

### Context

Following up on the project timeline.

### Purpose

Confirm expected start date.

### Key Message

We want to ensure resources are aligned.

### Customer Action

Please confirm expected project start date.

### Timeline

Please confirm by [Date], if appropriate.

Keep the communication:

- Clear
- Professional
- Customer-focused
- Concise
- Specific

---

# 1️⃣3️⃣ Review Before Sending

Check:

- [ ] Correct customer.
- [ ] Correct context.
- [ ] Clear purpose.
- [ ] Accurate information.
- [ ] Appropriate tone.
- [ ] Clear next action.
- [ ] Correct deadline.
- [ ] No unsupported claims.
- [ ] No confidential information unnecessarily included.
- [ ] No unnecessary pressure.

---

# 1️⃣4️⃣ Send Communication

Send through the appropriate approved channel.

For sensitive or important matters:

- Confirm internal alignment.
- Follow escalation procedures where required.
- Ensure the right stakeholders are included.

Do not make unauthorized commercial commitments.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Prioritize only high-revenue customers.
- Ignore critical customer issues.
- Make everything urgent.
- Send generic messages without a purpose.
- Invent customer information.
- Invent deadlines.
- Promise unauthorized commercial terms.
- Use the wrong communication channel.
- Over-communicate unnecessarily.
- Ignore customer preferences.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Every customer communication should have a clear purpose and an appropriate level of urgency.**

# 🔄💰 Customer Renewal Readiness Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured AI-assisted workflow for assessing customer renewal readiness and identifying actions required to improve renewal confidence.

The objective is to identify renewal risks early, demonstrate customer value, understand future requirements, and create a clear renewal action plan.

---

# 1️⃣2️⃣ Assess Renewal Readiness

Use:

### 🟢 Renewal Ready

Strong value, healthy relationship, limited risk, and clear future need.

### 🟡 Renewal Developing

Renewal appears achievable but some actions remain.

### 🟠 Renewal At Risk

Important risks or unresolved concerns exist.

### 🔴 Critical Renewal Risk

Major relationship, value, commercial, or delivery concerns exist.

### ⚪ Unknown

Insufficient information to assess readiness.

Do not treat this as a guaranteed renewal prediction.

It is a CSM planning assessment.

# ⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

- Treat renewal readiness as a guaranteed renewal.
- Invent customer intentions.
- Assume the customer will renew.
- Invent commercial information.
- Invent renewal dates.
- Treat potential revenue as confirmed.
- Ignore unresolved customer concerns.
- Focus only on revenue.
- Ignore customer value.
- Ignore stakeholder changes.
- Create pressure-based sales actions.
- Share confidential information unnecessarily.

The most important principle is:

> **Renewal should be the result of demonstrated value, strong relationships, and alignment with future customer needs.**

# 👋 Welcome to the VBS Customer Success Academy

Congratulations on joining the Customer Success team at Virtual Building Studio (VBS)!

This academy has been created to help you learn your role step by step.

---

# 🎯 Your Mission

As a Customer Success Manager, your goal is to:

- Build strong customer relationships
- Ensure project success
- Coordinate with internal teams
- Keep customer information updated
- Identify growth opportunities
- Deliver an outstanding customer experience

---

# 📚 How to Use This Academy

Complete the modules in the following order:

1. Daily Planning
2. Customer Onboarding
3. Client Engagement
4. CRM Management
5. Customer Health Management
6. Invoice & Payment Management
7. Business Reviews

Do not skip any module.

Each one builds on the previous one.

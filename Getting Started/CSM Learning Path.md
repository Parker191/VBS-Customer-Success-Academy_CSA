# 🏆 Graduation Checklist

Before completing your training, make sure you can:

- [ ] Conduct a customer onboarding call
- [ ] Update Zoho CRM correctly
- [ ] Send a professional follow-up email
- [ ] Update Customer Health
- [ ] Prepare a Weekly Business Review (WBR)
- [ ] Prepare a Monthly Business Review (MBR)
- [ ] Follow up on invoices professionally

---

Congratulations!

Once every box is checked, you are ready to independently manage customer accounts.

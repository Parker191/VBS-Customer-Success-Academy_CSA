# 🤔 What is a KPI?

KPI stands for **Key Performance Indicator**.

A KPI is a measurable value that helps determine whether an individual, team, or organization is achieving its objectives.

Simply put:

> **A KPI is a way of measuring success.**

Just as a school report card measures a student's performance, KPIs measure an employee's performance.

---

# 🌟 Why Are KPIs Important?

KPIs help both employees and the organization by:

- Measuring performance objectively.
- Setting clear expectations.
- Identifying strengths and improvement areas.
- Supporting business growth.
- Improving customer satisfaction.
- Tracking progress over time.

Without KPIs, it would be difficult to know whether we are moving in the right direction.

# 🏢 KPIs at Virtual Building Studio

At VBS, Customer Success Managers contribute to multiple business objectives.

Although the CSM role is not a direct sales role, Customer Success has a significant impact on:

- Customer retention
- Customer satisfaction
- Revenue growth
- Customer engagement
- Operational efficiency
- Payment follow-ups
- Business expansion

For this reason, CSM KPIs are grouped into four major categories.

---

# 📈 KPI Categories

The primary KPI categories for a Customer Success Manager at VBS are:

## 💰 Revenue

Measures business growth and financial contribution.

Examples include:

- Revenue Achievement
- Upsell Opportunities
- Cross-sell Opportunities
- Referral Revenue

---

## 😊 Customer Success

Measures customer relationships and satisfaction.

Examples include:

- Customer Health
- Customer Retention
- Customer Satisfaction
- Customer Meetings

---

## ⚙️ Operations

Measures process execution and operational discipline.

Examples include:

- CRM Updates
- Follow-up Completion
- Meeting Documentation
- Internal Tracker Updates

---

## 💳 Finance Support

Measures activities related to outstanding payments.

Examples include:

- Invoice Follow-up
- Outstanding Payments
- Payment Confirmation

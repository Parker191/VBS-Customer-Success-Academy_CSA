# Why This Module Matters

A Customer Success Manager works with multiple systems every day.

Knowing which tool to use—and when—helps improve productivity, customer communication, and data accuracy.

Remember:

**The tool is only as useful as the process behind it.**

# Tool 1 - Microsoft Outlook

## Purpose

Primary communication platform with customers.

### Used For

- Customer emails
- Meeting invitations
- Calendar management
- Payment follow-ups
- Internal communication

### Best Practices

✅ Respond within agreed timelines.

✅ Use professional email signatures.

✅ Keep subject lines clear.

✅ Schedule reminders.

---

# Tool 2 - Microsoft Teams

## Purpose

Internal collaboration and online meetings.

### Used For

- Customer meetings
- Internal meetings
- Chat with colleagues
- Screen sharing
- File sharing

### Best Practices

- Join meetings early.
- Keep meeting notes.
- Share action items after every meeting.

---

# Tool 3 - Zoho CRM

## Purpose

The central system for customer information.

### Store

- Customer details
- Contacts
- Meeting notes
- Opportunities
- Follow-ups
- Health score
- Next actions

### Golden Rule

**Every customer interaction should be recorded in CRM.**

---

# Tool 4 - One Drive

## Purpose

Document management.

### Store

- SOPs
- Customer documents
- Project files
- Meeting notes
- Internal templates

### Best Practices

Keep documents organized.

Avoid duplicate files.

Always upload the latest version.

---

# Tool 5 - Tracker

## Purpose

Reporting and tracking.

### Used For

- KPI Dashboards
- Customer Health Tracker
- Revenue Tracker
- Invoice Tracker
- Weekly Reports
- Monthly Reports

### Best Practices

Keep formulas protected.

Review data before sharing.

---

# Tool 6 - QuickBooks

## Purpose

Invoice reference and payment tracking.

### Customer Success Uses

- Check invoice status
- Verify payment
- Share invoice copies
- Coordinate with Finance

Customer Success should not modify accounting records unless authorized.

---

# Tool 7 - GitHub

## Purpose

Knowledge Management System.

### Used For

- SOP Library
- Email Templates
- CRM Guides
- Meeting Templates
- Training Videos
- Best Practices

GitHub serves as the Customer Success Academy.

# Best Practices

✅ Keep all systems updated.

✅ Avoid storing customer information in personal files.

✅ Follow naming conventions.

✅ Use the latest document versions.

✅ Protect confidential information.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Forgetting CRM updates.

❌ Saving files locally instead of SharePoint.

❌ Missing calendar invitations.

❌ Duplicate reports.

❌ Not documenting customer meetings.

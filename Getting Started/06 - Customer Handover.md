Welcome to **Module 06 – Customer Handover**.

Customer Handover is the process of transferring ownership of a customer account from the Sales team to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).

# 🌟 Business Context

The customer journey begins long before the first project is delivered.

Once a customer signs an agreement or decides to work with VBS, the Sales team transfers the account to the Customer Success team.

The Customer Success Manager becomes the customer's primary point of contact and is responsible for building the relationship, coordinating internally, and ensuring a positive customer experience.

A well-executed handover creates trust, reduces confusion, and sets the foundation for a successful long-term partnership.

# 📖 What is Customer Handover?

Customer Handover is the formal process of transferring ownership of a customer account from the Sales team to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).

This process ensures that all customer information, project details, expectations, and commitments are communicated accurately before the Customer Success team begins managing the account.

A successful handover enables the CSM to confidently engage with the customer from Day 1 without asking the customer to repeat information already shared with the Sales team.

---

## 🎯 Objectives of Customer Handover

The Customer Handover process aims to:

- Ensure a smooth transition from Sales to Customer Success.
- Provide the CSM with complete customer information.
- Minimize communication gaps.
- Build customer confidence from the very first interaction.
- Align internal teams on customer expectations and project goals.

# ⭐ Why Customer Handover Matters

A structured Customer Handover benefits both the customer and the VBS team.

### Benefits for the Customer

- They don't have to repeat the same information.
- They know who their primary point of contact is.
- They receive faster responses.
- They experience a smooth transition.

### Benefits for VBS

- Better internal collaboration.
- Accurate customer information.
- Reduced communication errors.
- Higher customer satisfaction.
- Faster project coordination.
- Improved customer retention.

A good handover builds trust from the very beginning of the customer relationship.

# 🔄 Customer Handover Workflow

The Customer Handover process at VBS generally follows these steps:

```text
Lead Generated
      │
      ▼
Sales Qualification
      │
      ▼
Proposal & Commercial Discussion
      │
      ▼
Customer Confirmation
      │
      ▼
Sales → Customer Success Handover
      │
      ▼
CRM & Internal Tracker Update
      │
      ▼
CSM Review
      │
      ▼
Customer Introduction Email
      │
      ▼
Kickoff Meeting
      │
      ▼
Ongoing Customer Success Management
```

Every step in this workflow is important because it ensures that the customer receives a consistent and professional experience.

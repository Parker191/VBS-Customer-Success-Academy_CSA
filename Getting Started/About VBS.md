

# 🏢Module 1 : About Virtual Building Studio (VBS)

# Welcome

Welcome to Virtual Building Studio (VBS).

VBS partners with Architecture, Engineering and Construction (AEC) firms by providing BIM and digital design services.

Our goal is to help clients deliver projects faster, improve coordination and reduce project risks through high-quality technical support.

---

# What We Do

Our core services include:

• BIM Modeling

• Scan to BIM

• CAD to BIM Conversion

• Construction Documentation

• BIM Coordination

• Dedicated BIM Resource Model (DRM)

• Architectural, Structural and MEP Support

Welcome to **Module 06 – Customer Handover**.

# 🌟 Business Context

The customer journey begins long before the first project is delivered.

Once a customer signs an agreement or decides to work with VBS, the Sales team transfers the account to the Customer Success team.

The Customer Success Manager becomes the customer's primary point of contact and is responsible for building the relationship, coordinating internally, and ensuring a positive customer experience.

A well-executed handover creates trust, reduces confusion, and sets the foundation for a successful long-term partnership.

# 📖 What is Customer Handover?

Customer Handover is the formal process of transferring ownership of a customer account from the Sales team to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).

This process ensures that all customer information, project details, expectations, and commitments are communicated accurately before the Customer Success team begins managing the account.

A successful handover enables the CSM to confidently engage with the customer from Day 1 without asking the customer to repeat information already shared with the Sales team.

---

## 🎯 Objectives of Customer Handover

The Customer Handover process aims to:

- Ensure a smooth transition from Sales to Customer Success.
- Provide the CSM with complete customer information.
- Minimize communication gaps.
- Build customer confidence from the very first interaction.
- Align internal teams on customer expectations and project goals.

- ---

# ⭐ Why Customer Handover Matters

A structured Customer Handover benefits both the customer and the VBS team.

### Benefits for the Customer

- They don't have to repeat the same information.
- They know who their primary point of contact is.
- They receive faster responses.
- They experience a smooth transition.

### Benefits for VBS

- Better internal collaboration.
- Accurate customer information.
- Reduced communication errors.
- Higher customer satisfaction.
- Faster project coordination.
- Improved customer retention.

A good handover builds trust from the very beginning of the customer relationship.

# 👥 Roles & Responsibilities

A successful customer handover requires collaboration between multiple teams. Each team has a specific role in ensuring a smooth transition.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| **Business Development Executive (BDE)** | Confirms the deal, shares customer expectations, transfers all customer information, proposal, and commercial details. |
| **Sales Manager** | Reviews and approves the handover process when required, ensuring all commitments made during the sales cycle are communicated clearly. |
| **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** | Reviews all customer information, prepares for onboarding, introduces themselves to the customer, coordinates internally, and becomes the primary point of contact. |
| **Delivery Manager** | Reviews the project scope, confirms resource allocation, plans project execution, and supports technical discussions during onboarding. |
| **Finance Team** | Assists with customer billing information, invoice generation, and payment-related queries when required. |

---

## 🤝 Collaboration Between Teams

A successful customer experience depends on strong collaboration.

Each team should communicate openly and ensure that no important customer information is missed during the handover process.

Remember:

**The customer should experience one VBS team—not separate departments.**

---

> 💡 **Manager's Tip**

> Never assume information has already been shared. If you're unsure about a project requirement, commercial commitment, or customer expectation, ask the BDE or Sales Manager before contacting the customer.

# 📅 Internal Customer Handover Meeting

## Purpose

The Internal Customer Handover Meeting ensures that everyone involved in the customer journey has the same understanding before customer onboarding begins.

This meeting helps eliminate communication gaps, clarify responsibilities, and align expectations across departments.

---

## 👥 Participants

The following team members should attend whenever applicable:

- Business Development Executive (BDE)
- Sales Manager
- Customer Success Manager (CSM)
- Delivery Manager
- Finance Representative (if billing discussions are required)

---

## 📝 Standard Meeting Agenda

1. Customer Introduction
2. Company Overview
3. Services Purchased
4. Proposal Review
5. Commercial Agreement Review
6. Expected Project Start Date
7. Customer Expectations
8. Risks or Special Requirements
9. Delivery Team Assignment
10. Next Steps
11. Open Questions

---

## 🎯 Expected Outcome

By the end of the meeting, the CSM should clearly understand:

- Who the customer is.
- What services have been sold.
- What commitments were made during the sales process.
- Who is responsible for project delivery.
- What the immediate next steps are.

---

> 💡 **Manager's Tip**
>
> Use the handover meeting to ask questions. It's much easier to clarify expectations internally than after speaking with the customer.
>

# 📧 Customer Introduction Email

## Objective

The Customer Introduction Email is sent after the internal handover is complete.

Its purpose is to:

- Introduce the Customer Success Manager.
- Explain the CSM's role.
- Reassure the customer that they have a dedicated point of contact.
- Invite the customer to schedule a kickoff meeting.

---

## 📄 Sample Email

**Subject:** Welcome to Virtual Building Studio – Your Customer Success Manager

Dear **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

My name is **[Your Name]**, and I am the Customer Success Manager at **Virtual Building Studio (VBS)**.

I will be your primary point of contact throughout our engagement and will work closely with you to ensure a smooth and successful experience.

I have reviewed the information shared by our Sales team and look forward to supporting your team.

I would also like to schedule a kickoff meeting to introduce the project team, discuss objectives, and align on the next steps.

Please let me know a convenient time for the meeting.

We look forward to partnering with you.

Best Regards,

**[Your Name]**

Customer Success Manager

Virtual Building Studio

---

### ✅ Before Sending the Email

- [ ] Verify the customer's name.
- [ ] Verify the company name.
- [ ] Confirm the email recipients.
- [ ] Review the proposal.
- [ ] Confirm the kickoff meeting availability.

# 🎯 Real VBS Scenario

## Situation

The Sales team informs you that the customer expects the kickoff meeting on Monday.

However, the assigned Delivery Manager is unavailable until Wednesday.

## Question

As the Customer Success Manager:

- What should you do first?
- Who should you coordinate with?
- How would you communicate with the customer?
- How would you update Zoho CRM and the Internal Tracker?

---

## Suggested Approach

1. Confirm the Delivery Manager's availability.
2. Discuss alternatives with the Sales Manager.
3. Contact the customer proactively and explain the revised timeline.
4. Update Zoho CRM and the Internal Tracker.
5. Schedule the kickoff meeting after confirming internal availability.

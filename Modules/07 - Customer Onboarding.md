# 👋 Welcome

Welcome to **Module 07 – Customer Onboarding**.

Customer onboarding is one of the most important stages of the customer journey.

A well-planned onboarding process helps customers understand how Virtual Building Studio (VBS) works, what to expect during the engagement, and who will support them throughout the project.

The Customer Success Manager (CSM) is responsible for ensuring that every customer has a smooth, professional, and positive onboarding experience.

# 🌟 Business Context

The onboarding phase begins immediately after the customer handover.

During this stage, the Customer Success Manager works closely with the customer and internal teams to prepare for project execution.

A successful onboarding process establishes clear communication, confirms expectations, aligns project goals, and creates the foundation for a long-term customer relationship.

Customers who experience a smooth onboarding process are more likely to remain engaged, satisfied, and successful throughout their partnership with VBS.

---

# 📖 What is Customer Onboarding?

Customer Onboarding is the structured process of helping a new customer transition from the sales process into active project execution.

It includes:

- Introducing the Customer Success Manager.
- Introducing the Delivery Team.
- Confirming the project scope.
- Scheduling the kickoff meeting.
- Establishing communication channels.
- Preparing the customer for project delivery.

The goal is to ensure that the customer feels informed, supported, and confident before project work begins.

# 🏢 VBS Customer Onboarding Process

Customer Onboarding begins immediately after the customer handover has been completed.

- Establish communication channels.

- Prepare the customer for project execution.

The goal is to ensure that every customer clearly understands the next steps before project work begins.

# 👥 Roles & Responsibilities During Customer Onboarding

| Team | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** | Leads the onboarding process, coordinates meetings, communicates with the customer, updates Zoho CRM, and maintains the Internal Tracker. |
| **Delivery Manager** | Reviews project requirements, introduces the delivery team, confirms technical scope, and prepares for project execution. |
| **Sales Team** | Provides clarification on customer expectations and commercial commitments if required. |
| **Finance Team** | Supports billing setup and resolves invoice-related questions when necessary. |
| **Customer** | Shares project requirements, confirms timelines, provides required documents, and participates in the kickoff meeting. |

> ## ⚠️ What Could Go Wrong?

Issue:
The customer does not respond to the kickoff meeting invitation.

Possible Causes:
- Incorrect email address.
- Customer is unavailable.
- Invitation went to spam.
- Timing conflict.

Recommended Actions:
1. Send a polite follow-up email.
2. Call the customer if appropriate.
3. Confirm contact details with the Sales team.
4. Update Zoho CRM with the communication attempt.
5. Inform the Delivery Manager if the delay impacts project timelines.

# 📅 Kickoff Meeting Preparation

The kickoff meeting is the customer's first formal interaction with the VBS project team.

Proper preparation demonstrates professionalism, builds confidence, and ensures everyone starts with the same understanding.

---

## Objectives

Before the kickoff meeting, the Customer Success Manager should:

- Review the proposal and project scope.
- Confirm the meeting date and time.
- Ensure all internal stakeholders are invited.
- Share the meeting agenda in advance.
- Review customer expectations.
- Prepare questions for the customer.
- Confirm the Delivery Manager's availability.

---

# ⚠️ What Could Go Wrong?

Even with a well-planned onboarding process, challenges can occur.

The Customer Success Manager should identify issues early and take proactive action.

| Situation | Recommended Action |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Customer does not respond to emails | Send a polite follow-up, verify contact details, and inform the Sales team if necessary. |
| Kickoff meeting needs to be rescheduled | Coordinate with the customer and internal teams, then update Zoho CRM and the Internal Tracker. |
| Required documents are missing | Contact the Sales team or Delivery Manager before the kickoff meeting. |
| Customer expectations differ from the proposal | Review the proposal, clarify expectations internally, and discuss them with the customer professionally. |
| Delivery Manager is unavailable | Coordinate an alternative plan with the Delivery team before communicating with the customer. |

---

> 💡 **Manager's Tip**
>
> Never wait for a small issue to become a major problem. Early communication builds customer trust.
>

# 🎯 Real VBS Scenario

## Situation

A new customer has been handed over to you.

The kickoff meeting is scheduled for tomorrow morning.

While preparing, you realize:

- The proposal has not been uploaded to OneDrive.
- The Delivery Manager has not confirmed attendance.
- The customer has already accepted the meeting invitation.

---

## As the Customer Success Manager:

Think about the following questions:

1. What should you do first?
2. Who should you contact internally?
3. Should you inform the customer immediately?
4. What should be updated in Zoho CRM?
5. What should be updated in the Internal Tracker?

---

## Recommended Approach

1. Contact the Delivery Manager to confirm availability.
2. Request the missing proposal from the Sales team.
3. Upload the required documents to OneDrive.
4. Update Zoho CRM with any changes.
5. Update the Internal Tracker.
6. Inform the customer only if the meeting schedule is affected.

---

## Learning Point

Preparation before the kickoff meeting helps avoid last-minute issues and demonstrates professionalism.

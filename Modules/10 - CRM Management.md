# 👋 Welcome

Welcome to Module 10 – CRM Management.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is one of the most important tools used by Customer Success Managers.

At Virtual Building Studio (VBS), Zoho CRM serves as the central repository for customer information, communication history, and account activities.

An accurately maintained CRM enables Sales, Customer Success, Delivery, Finance, and Leadership to work together efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the purpose of Zoho CRM.
- Maintain accurate customer records.
- Record customer interactions.
- Schedule and manage follow-up activities.
- Keep opportunities and account information up to date.
- Support reporting through accurate CRM data.
- Understand how Zoho CRM integrates with the Internal Tracker and OneDrive.

# 🌟 Business Context

Every customer interaction generates valuable information.

When this information is properly documented in Zoho CRM, the organization gains visibility into customer history, project progress, and future opportunities.

Without accurate CRM records, teams may miss important follow-ups, duplicate work, or make decisions based on incomplete information.

# 🖥️ What is Zoho CRM?

## Introduction

Zoho CRM is a Customer Relationship Management platform used by VBS to organize, manage, and track customer-related information.

For a Customer Success Manager, Zoho CRM acts as the central place to understand:

- Who the customer is.
- What services they use.
- Who the important contacts are.
- What conversations have happened.
- What actions are pending.
- What opportunities exist.

A CRM helps the entire organization maintain a complete history of customer relationships.

# 🚨 Common Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Using OneDrive as CRM

Example:

❌ Saving customer discussions only in Word documents.

Problem:

Other teams cannot see customer history easily.

Correct Approach:

✅ Log important discussions in Zoho CRM.

---

## Mistake 2: Using CRM for Everything

Example:

❌ Adding detailed project execution tracking inside CRM.

Problem:

CRM becomes cluttered.

Correct Approach:

✅ Keep operational details in Internal Tracker.

---

## Mistake 3: Keeping Information in Personal Files

Example:

❌ Saving customer notes on personal desktops.

Problem:

Information is lost when people change roles.

Correct Approach:

✅ Store information in approved VBS systems.

---

# ⭐ Customer Success Rule

> "CRM stores the relationship.  
> Tracker manages operations.  
> OneDrive stores documents."

Remember this simple rule:
Customer Relationship → Zoho CRM

Operational Performance → Internal Tracker

Customer Documents → OneDrive

A CSM should think:

If the answer is no, more documentation is required.

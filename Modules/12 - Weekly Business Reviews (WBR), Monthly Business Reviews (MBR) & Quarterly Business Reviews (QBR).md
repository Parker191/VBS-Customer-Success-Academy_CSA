# 👋 Welcome

Welcome to **Module 12 – Weekly Business Reviews (WBR), Monthly Business Reviews (MBR) & Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR).**

Business Reviews are one of the most valuable responsibilities of a Customer Success Manager. They provide structured opportunities to review progress, align with customer goals, identify risks, celebrate achievements, and plan the next steps together.

At Virtual Building Studio (VBS), Business Reviews help strengthen customer relationships by creating regular, meaningful conversations based on measurable outcomes rather than reactive support.

Throughout this module, you'll learn how to prepare for, conduct, and follow up on effective Business Reviews that build trust, demonstrate value, and uncover future opportunities.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Explain the purpose of WBRs, MBRs, and QBRs.
- Understand the differences between each review type.
- Prepare effectively for customer review meetings.
- Present meaningful customer insights using data.
- Track action items and business outcomes.
- Strengthen customer relationships through structured communication.
- Identify growth opportunities during Business Reviews.
- Use AI to improve meeting preparation and follow-up.
---

# 🌍 Business Context

Customer Success is not only about solving problems—it is about helping customers achieve measurable business outcomes.

As projects progress, customers need visibility into what has been completed, what is currently underway, what challenges exist, and what opportunities lie ahead. Business Reviews provide a structured forum for these discussions.

At Virtual Building Studio (VBS), Business Reviews strengthen collaboration between the customer and the Customer Success Manager by ensuring that both teams remain aligned on priorities, project progress, and future goals.

Rather than waiting for issues to arise, Business Reviews encourage proactive communication, helping teams identify risks early, celebrate successes, and make informed decisions together.

Whether reviewing weekly project activities or quarterly strategic objectives, these meetings build transparency, accountability, and long-term trust.

---

# 💼 Why Business Reviews Matter

Regular Business Reviews help Customer Success Managers:

- Build stronger customer relationships.
- Demonstrate the value delivered by VBS.
- Track progress against agreed objectives.
- Identify risks before they become critical issues.
- Ensure accountability for action items.
- Improve communication between customer and internal teams.
- Discover opportunities for future projects and long-term partnerships.

Business Reviews transform conversations from **reactive problem-solving** into **proactive business planning**.
---

# 📊 Understanding WBRs, MBRs & QBRs

Business Reviews are conducted at different intervals depending on the purpose of the discussion.

Although they all focus on customer success, each review has a different objective, audience, and level of detail.

Think of them as three levels of the same conversation:

- **Weekly Business Reviews (WBRs)** focus on short-term execution.
- **Monthly Business Reviews (MBRs)** focus on operational performance.
- **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)** focus on long-term business outcomes and strategic planning.

Together, these reviews ensure that customers remain informed, engaged, and aligned with VBS throughout the partnership.

---

# 🔄 Business Review Comparison

| Review Type | Frequency | Primary Focus | Typical Audience | Outcome |
|--------------|-----------|---------------|------------------|---------|
| **WBR** | Weekly | Project progress and immediate priorities | Project Team, Customer POC | Short-term action plan |
| **MBR** | Monthly | Performance, delivery, customer health | Customer POC, Delivery Team, CSM | Operational improvements |
| **QBR** | Quarterly | Business outcomes, strategy, future opportunities | Customer Leadership, Decision Makers, CSM | Strategic roadmap |

---

> 💡 **Did You Know?**
>
> A Quarterly Business Review should not simply repeat the last three Monthly Reviews. Instead, it should connect day-to-day delivery with the customer's broader business goals and future opportunities.

# 📅 Weekly Business Reviews (WBR)

A Weekly Business Review is a short, focused meeting that keeps projects moving smoothly.

Its purpose is to review the work completed during the previous week, discuss current priorities, identify blockers, and confirm the plan for the coming week.

These meetings are tactical and action-oriented.

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Review completed work.
- Discuss current project status.
- Identify risks and blockers.
- Confirm next week's priorities.
- Assign ownership for action items.

## 👥 Typical Participants

- Customer Success Manager
- Customer Point of Contact
- Delivery Manager
- Project Team (when required)

## ⏱️ Recommended Duration

**30–45 Minutes**

## 📋 Typical Agenda

1. Welcome and quick updates
2. Review completed work
3. Current project status
4. Risks or blockers
5. Upcoming milestones
6. Action items and owners

---

> 💡 **VBS Tip**
>
> Every WBR should end with clear action items, assigned owners, and target completion dates.
>
---

# 📆 Monthly Business Reviews (MBR)

Monthly Business Reviews provide a broader operational view of the customer relationship.

Rather than focusing only on weekly tasks, MBRs evaluate delivery performance, customer health, communication, and overall account progress.

They help ensure that operational goals remain aligned with customer expectations.

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Review monthly performance.
- Evaluate Customer Health Score.
- Discuss delivery quality.
- Review completed milestones.
- Address recurring challenges.
- Plan priorities for the next month.

## 👥 Typical Participants

- Customer Success Manager
- Customer Point of Contact
- Delivery Manager
- Project Manager (if applicable)

## ⏱️ Recommended Duration

**45–60 Minutes**

## 📋 Typical Agenda

1. Monthly performance summary
2. Customer Health Score review
3. Project updates
4. Risks and mitigation plans
5. Customer feedback
6. Next month's objectives

---

> 💡 **Best Practice**
>
> Use trends instead of isolated data. Comparing current performance with previous months helps customers understand progress over time.

---

# 📈 Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)

Quarterly Business Reviews are strategic meetings that evaluate the overall success of the customer partnership.

Unlike Weekly and Monthly Business Reviews, a QBR focuses on business outcomes rather than day-to-day project activities. It provides an opportunity to demonstrate the value VBS has delivered, review progress toward customer objectives, discuss future initiatives, and strengthen executive relationships.

A successful QBR should answer one important question:

> **"How has our partnership helped the customer move closer to their business goals?"**

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Demonstrate business value delivered.
- Review quarterly achievements.
- Evaluate Customer Health trends.
- Discuss strategic challenges.
- Identify growth opportunities.
- Plan objectives for the next quarter.
- Strengthen executive relationships.

## 👥 Typical Participants

- Customer Success Manager
- Customer Decision Makers
- Customer Executives
- Delivery Manager
- Account Manager (if applicable)

## ⏱️ Recommended Duration

**60–90 Minutes**

## 📋 Typical Agenda

1. Welcome and business updates
2. Quarterly achievements
3. Delivery performance summary
4. Customer Health trends
5. Business outcomes achieved
6. Risks and mitigation plans
7. Upcoming initiatives
8. Upsell or expansion opportunities
9. Action items and next steps

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**
>
> A QBR may include completed BIM projects, delivery performance, resource utilization, customer feedback, upcoming project pipelines, and opportunities to expand support through additional VBS services.

---

# ⚖️ WBR vs MBR vs QBR

| Area | WBR | MBR | QBR |
|------|-----|-----|------|
| Frequency | Weekly | Monthly | Quarterly |
| Focus | Daily execution | Operational performance | Strategic outcomes |
| Duration | 30–45 mins | 45–60 mins | 60–90 mins |
| Audience | Project Team | Operations Team | Business Leaders |
| Main Discussion | Tasks & Progress | Performance & Health | Business Value & Strategy |
| Data Reviewed | Weekly updates | Monthly KPIs | Quarterly trends |
| Deliverable | Action Items | Performance Review | Strategic Roadmap |

# 🎯 Which Review Should You Use?

| Situation | Recommended Review |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Discuss project progress | ✅ WBR |
| Review delivery performance | ✅ MBR |
| Present business value | ✅ QBR |
| Identify operational risks | ✅ MBR |
| Plan long-term growth | ✅ QBR |
| Review weekly priorities | ✅ WBR |

# 📝 Preparing for Business Reviews

A successful Business Review begins long before the meeting starts.

Preparation ensures that discussions are based on accurate information, meaningful insights, and clear objectives rather than last-minute updates.

As a Customer Success Manager, you should review customer information, project progress, risks, and previous commitments before every Business Review.

Remember:

> **Preparation builds confidence. Preparation builds trust.**

---

# ✅ Business Review Preparation Checklist

Before every Business Review, verify the following:

| Task | WBR | MBR | QBR |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Review CRM updates | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Review previous action items | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Check Customer Health Score |  | ✅ | ✅ |
| Review project milestones | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Identify risks or blockers | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Gather customer feedback |  | ✅ | ✅ |
| Review delivery performance |  | ✅ | ✅ |
| Prepare business insights |  |  | ✅ |
| Identify growth opportunities |  |  | ✅ |
| Prepare meeting agenda | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

---

# 📂 Information to Collect

Before meeting the customer, collect information from the following sources:

| Source | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Zoho CRM | Customer history, activities, follow-ups |
| Internal Tracker | Project status, KPIs, milestones |
| OneDrive | Previous meeting notes, proposals, contracts |
| Delivery Team | Progress updates and risks |
| Finance Team | Invoice or payment status (when applicable) |

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**
>
> Before a Monthly Business Review, a CSM checks Zoho CRM for recent customer interactions, reviews the Internal Tracker for project progress, reads the previous meeting minutes stored in OneDrive, and confirms delivery updates with the Delivery Manager.

# 📅 Creating an Effective Meeting Agenda

A clear agenda keeps Business Reviews focused and ensures that every participant understands the purpose of the meeting.

Whenever possible, share the agenda with the customer before the meeting.

## Sample Agenda

| Agenda Item | Owner | Estimated Time |
|--------------|-------|---------------:|
| Welcome & Introductions | CSM | 5 mins |
| Review Previous Actions | CSM | 10 mins |
| Project Updates | Delivery Team | 15 mins |
| Customer Feedback | Customer | 10 mins |
| Risks & Challenges | All | 10 mins |
| Next Steps | CSM | 10 mins |

---

# 💡 Best Practice

Keep meetings focused on business outcomes rather than reading reports.

Customers can read reports on their own.

Business Reviews should explain:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- What it means
- What happens next

---

> ✅ **Key Takeaways**
>
> - Preparation is the foundation of every successful Business Review.
> - Use CRM, Internal Tracker, and OneDrive together.
> - Share an agenda before the meeting.
> - Always review previous action items before creating new ones.

# 🎤 Conducting Effective Business Reviews

A successful Business Review is a conversation, not a presentation.

The Customer Success Manager should encourage discussion, actively listen to customer feedback, and ensure that every participant understands the current status, challenges, and next steps.

Rather than simply reading reports, explain what the data means and how it impacts the customer's business objectives.

## 🎯 During the Meeting

A Customer Success Manager should:

- Start the meeting on time.
- Confirm the meeting agenda.
- Review progress against previous action items.
- Present updates using accurate data.
- Encourage customer participation.
- Listen actively and ask clarifying questions.
- Document important decisions.
- Confirm ownership of new action items.
- Summarize the discussion before closing.

---

# 💬 Communication Best Practices

During Business Reviews:

✅ Focus on business outcomes.

✅ Speak clearly and confidently.

✅ Support discussions with facts and data.

✅ Celebrate achievements.

✅ Address risks honestly.

✅ Avoid technical jargon unless appropriate for the audience.

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**
>
> Instead of saying:
>
> "The project is 75% complete."
>
> Explain:
>
> "The architectural BIM model has reached 75% completion. This keeps us on schedule for the planned coordination review next week."

# ✅ Managing Action Items

Every Business Review should end with clearly documented action items.

Each action item should include:

- A clear description.
- An assigned owner.
- A target completion date.
- Current status.

Without ownership, action items are unlikely to be completed.

## Example Action Tracker

| Action Item | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|--------------|-------|----------|--------|
| Share updated BIM model | Delivery Manager | 15 Aug | In Progress |
| Review design comments | Customer | 18 Aug | Pending |
| Schedule coordination meeting | CSM | 20 Aug | Not Started |

---

> 💡 **Best Practice**
>
> Review open action items at the beginning of the next Business Review before discussing new topics.

---

---

# 📧 Post-Meeting Follow-up

The Business Review does not end when the meeting finishes.

Prompt follow-up ensures that commitments are documented and everyone remains aligned.

## Follow-up Checklist

Within 24 hours:

- Send meeting minutes.
- Share the action tracker.
- Update Zoho CRM.
- Update the Internal Tracker (if required).
- Save meeting notes in OneDrive.
- Schedule the next Business Review.
- Inform internal stakeholders of important updates.

---

# 📋 Meeting Minutes Should Include

- Meeting date
- Participants
- Topics discussed
- Decisions made
- Risks identified
- Action items
- Next meeting date

---

> ✅ **Key Takeaways**
>
> A Business Review creates value only when agreed actions are completed. Timely follow-up demonstrates professionalism and builds customer confidence.

# 🤖 AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence can help Customer Success Managers prepare for and follow up on Business Reviews more efficiently.

AI should assist with organizing information, identifying trends, and drafting documentation. Final decisions and customer communication should always be reviewed by the Customer Success Manager.

## AI Can Help You:

- Summarize meeting notes.
- Draft meeting agendas.
- Create follow-up emails.
- Generate action item lists.
- Identify recurring customer concerns.
- Prepare executive summaries.
- Suggest discussion points for upcoming reviews.

---

## Example AI Prompts

### Prompt 1

> Summarize these meeting notes into professional meeting minutes.

### Prompt 2

> Create a Quarterly Business Review agenda based on these project updates.

### Prompt 3

> Draft a professional follow-up email summarizing today's Business Review and the agreed action items.

### Prompt 4

> Analyze these customer updates and identify the top three business risks that should be discussed during the next Monthly Business Review.

Successful Customer Success Managers:

- Prepare before every meeting.
- Use accurate and up-to-date information.
- Focus on customer business outcomes.
- Encourage open discussion.
- Document decisions clearly.
- Follow up on every commitment.

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes:

- Arriving unprepared.
- Reading reports instead of discussing insights.
- Ignoring customer feedback.
- Leaving meetings without action items.
- Failing to update CRM records.
- Waiting too long to send meeting notes.
- Focusing only on problems instead of achievements.

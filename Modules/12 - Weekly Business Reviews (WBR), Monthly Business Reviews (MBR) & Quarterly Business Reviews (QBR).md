# 👋 Welcome

Welcome to **Module 12 – Weekly Business Reviews (WBR), Monthly Business Reviews (MBR) & Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR).**

Business Reviews are one of the most valuable responsibilities of a Customer Success Manager. They provide structured opportunities to review progress, align with customer goals, identify risks, celebrate achievements, and plan the next steps together.

At Virtual Building Studio (VBS), Business Reviews help strengthen customer relationships by creating regular, meaningful conversations based on measurable outcomes rather than reactive support.

Throughout this module, you'll learn how to prepare for, conduct, and follow up on effective Business Reviews that build trust, demonstrate value, and uncover future opportunities.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Explain the purpose of WBRs, MBRs, and QBRs.
- Understand the differences between each review type.
- Prepare effectively for customer review meetings.
- Present meaningful customer insights using data.
- Track action items and business outcomes.
- Strengthen customer relationships through structured communication.
- Identify growth opportunities during Business Reviews.
- Use AI to improve meeting preparation and follow-up.

# 📅 VBS Customer Success — Meeting Templates

## Purpose

This section provides standardized meeting templates to help VBS Customer Success Managers prepare, conduct, document, and follow up on important customer conversations.

The templates are designed to create a consistent customer experience while allowing the CSM to adapt each meeting to the customer's situation.

---

# 📚 Available Meeting Templates

| Template | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Kickoff Meeting | Start a new customer engagement |
| WBR | Review weekly progress, actions, and priorities |
| MBR | Review monthly performance and upcoming priorities |
| QBR | Review customer value, outcomes, risks, and future priorities |
| Customer Check-in | Maintain relationship and understand customer needs |
| Escalation Meeting | Address customer concerns and agree corrective actions |
| Renewal Meeting | Review value, future needs, and renewal requirements |
| Executive Meeting | Support strategic stakeholder conversations |
| Feedback Meeting | Collect structured customer feedback |
| Project Review | Review project progress, delivery, risks, and next steps |

**🧠 Meeting Preparation Principles
**
Before an important customer meeting, the CSM should:

Review the customer's current situation.
Review previous meeting notes.
Check open action items.
Review customer health.
Understand current projects.
Identify customer concerns.
Identify relevant opportunities.
Define the meeting objective.
Prepare customer-specific questions.
Define the desired outcome.

The objective is **preparedness—not over-preparation.**

**📝 Meeting Documentation
**
After the meeting, document:

Meeting date
Meeting type
Participants
Purpose
Key discussion points
Customer feedback
Decisions
Risks
Opportunities
Customer actions
VBS actions
Owners
Deadlines
Next meeting

All actions, owners, and deadlines should be verified before being treated as confirmed commitments.

**⚠️ Important Rules
**
AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

Invent customer feedback.
Assume customer satisfaction.
Create unconfirmed commitments.
Assign owners without evidence.
Create deadlines that were not agreed upon.
Force sales conversations.
Ignore unresolved customer concerns.

The goal is to create **purposeful, customer-focused, and actionable meetings.**

**🎯 Desired Outcome
**
The meeting templates should help the CSM move from:

**Preparation → Conversation → Understanding → Decision → Action → Follow-Through

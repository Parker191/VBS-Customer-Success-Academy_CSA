---

# 📌 Purpose

Review the overall strategic partnership between the customer and Virtual Building Studio, evaluate business outcomes, discuss long-term goals, and align executive stakeholders on future opportunities.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Demonstrate business value delivered.
- Review strategic KPIs.
- Discuss executive priorities.
- Identify partnership opportunities.
- Align on future business objectives.
- Define executive action items.

---

# ⏱ Duration

60–90 Minutes

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

An Executive Business Review should:

- Focus on business outcomes, not operational tasks.
- Present concise, data-backed insights.
- Highlight measurable value.
- Discuss strategic risks proactively.
- Leave executives with a clear roadmap.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Using overly technical language.
- Spending too much time on project details.
- Presenting data without explaining business impact.
- Ending without executive decisions or next steps.

---

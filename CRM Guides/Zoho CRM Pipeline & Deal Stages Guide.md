**Document Type:** CRM Guide

---

## Purpose

Defines the deal/opportunity stages used in Zoho CRM for tracking growth opportunities (Upsell, Cross-sell, Renewal) so every CSM logs opportunities consistently.

---

## Growth Opportunity Pipeline

| Stage | Meaning | Typical Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Identified | A potential opportunity has been noted, not yet validated | CSM |
| Validated | Confirmed as genuine (need-driven, health-appropriate) with manager | CSM |
| Introduced | Raised with the customer in conversation | CSM |
| Handed to Sales | Customer expressed interest; Sales now owns commercial next steps | CSM → Sales |
| Negotiation | Terms being discussed | Sales |
| Closed Won | Opportunity converted | Sales |
| Closed Lost | Opportunity did not convert | Sales |

---

## Logging Rules

- Create a Deal record at the **Identified** stage — don't wait until it's validated to log it; this protects visibility if you're out of office.
- Always tag the Deal type: **Upsell**, **Cross-sell**, or **Renewal** — this determines which Playbook applies.
- Move a Deal to **Handed to Sales** only after the customer has expressed real interest, not just tolerance of the mention.
- On **Closed Lost**, always fill in the loss reason field — this is the single most useful field for future retention analysis.

---

## Renewal-Specific Stage Mapping

Renewals use the same pipeline, but map to the [Renewal Management SOP](../SOPs/Renewal%20Management.md) timeline:

| Pipeline Stage | SOP Milestone |
|---|---|
| Identified | 120 days out — readiness assessment |
| Validated | 90 days out — value case built |
| Introduced | 60 days out — customer engaged |
| Closed Won/Lost | 30 days out — confirmed |

---

## Do

- Log every opportunity, even ones you're not confident will close — pipeline visibility matters more than a clean-looking list.
- Tag opportunity type correctly so reporting can distinguish Upsell from Renewal from Cross-sell.

## Don't

- Don't skip the loss reason on Closed Lost deals — an empty field here is a missed learning opportunity.
- Don't move a deal to Negotiation before Sales has actually engaged — this creates false pipeline signal for leadership.

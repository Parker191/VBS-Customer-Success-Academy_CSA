# 🎬 Video Script — Module 05: KPIs & Success Metrics

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~7–8 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/05 - KPIs & Success Metrics.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand what a KPI is and why it matters
- Know the four KPI categories used at VBS
- Understand how daily CSM activities connect to KPI performance

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, chart/metrics icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Your role goes beyond meetings and emails — every interaction contributes to how the business performs. This module covers how that performance actually gets measured: KPIs."

### Slide 2 — What is a KPI?
**Visual:** Definition callout.
**Voiceover:**
"KPI stands for Key Performance Indicator — a measurable value that shows whether you, your team, or the business are hitting your objectives. Simply put: a KPI is a way of measuring success, the same way a report card measures a student's performance."

### Slide 3 — Why KPIs Matter
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"KPIs measure performance objectively, set clear expectations, identify strengths and gaps, support business growth, improve customer satisfaction, and track progress over time. Without them, it's hard to know if we're actually moving in the right direction."

### Slide 4 — Four KPI Categories at VBS
**Visual:** 2x2 grid: Revenue, Customer Success, Operations, Finance Support.
**Voiceover:**
"Even though CSM isn't a direct sales role, Customer Success impacts retention, satisfaction, revenue growth, engagement, and operational efficiency. That's why CSM KPIs fall into four categories: Revenue, Customer Success, Operations, and Finance Support."

### Slide 5 — Revenue KPIs
**Visual:** Icons: target, upsell, cross-sell, referral.
**Voiceover:**
"Revenue KPIs measure business growth and financial contribution — things like revenue achievement, upsell opportunities, cross-sell opportunities, and referral revenue."

### Slide 6 — Customer Success KPIs
**Visual:** Icons: heart, retention, satisfaction, meetings.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Success KPIs measure the health of the relationship itself — customer health score, retention, satisfaction, and meeting cadence."

### Slide 7 — Operations KPIs
**Visual:** Icons: CRM, checklist, documentation.
**Voiceover:**
"Operations KPIs measure process discipline — CRM accuracy, internal tracker updates, follow-up completion, and meeting documentation. This is where the SOPs you're learning throughout the Academy directly show up in your numbers."

---

## Closing / Call to Action

**Visual:** Summary of all 4 categories.
**Voiceover:**
"KPIs aren't about chasing numbers for their own sake — they're a way of making sure your day-to-day work is actually connecting to real outcomes for customers and the business. Next up: Customer Handover, where your relationship with each customer actually begins."

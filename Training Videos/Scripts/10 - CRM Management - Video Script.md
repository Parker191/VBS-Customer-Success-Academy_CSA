# 🎬 Video Script — Module 10: CRM Management

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Screen Recording
**Target Runtime:** ~8–9 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/10 - CRM Management.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the purpose of Zoho CRM at VBS
- Know what information belongs in a customer record
- Understand the CRM Golden Rule and how CRM connects teams

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, database/screen icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Relationship Management — CRM — is one of the most important tools you'll use as a CSM. This module covers how Zoho CRM works at VBS, and why it matters more than it might seem."

### Slide 2 — Why This Matters
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"A CRM is more than a contact database — it's the operational memory of the company. Every update you make helps VBS deliver better customer experiences, improve internal collaboration, reduce communication gaps, track opportunities, and support informed business decisions."

### Slide 3 — The Customer Success Principle
**Visual:** Quote callout.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's the principle that defines this whole module: if it's not documented in the CRM, it didn't happen. Accurate CRM updates ensure continuity, accountability, and better customer service — even when you're out of office."

### Slide 4 — What is Zoho CRM?
**Visual:** Screen recording: Zoho CRM dashboard.
**Voiceover:**
"Zoho CRM is the platform VBS uses to organize, manage, and track customer-related information. For a CSM, it's the central place to understand who the customer is, what services they use, who the key contacts are, what's been discussed, what's pending, and what opportunities exist."

### Slide 5 — How VBS Uses Zoho CRM
**Visual:** Table: Sales, Customer Success, Delivery, Finance, Leadership.
**Voiceover:**
"CRM supports every team differently. Sales manages leads and new opportunities. Customer Success manages relationships, follow-ups, and account health. Delivery accesses project information when needed. Finance supports billing visibility. And Leadership reviews growth, retention, and performance — all from the same source."

### Slide 6 — What Belongs in a Customer Record
**Visual:** Table: Company Info, Contact Info, Account Info, Communication History, Opportunities, Customer Health.
**Voiceover:**
"A complete customer record includes company and contact information, account details, full communication history, opportunities, and customer health status. If any of these categories is missing or stale, the whole team is working with an incomplete picture."

### Slide 7 — The CRM Mindset
**Visual:** Four question categories: Understanding, Relationship, Growth, Risk.
**Voiceover:**
"Don't think of CRM as data entry — think of it as answering questions. Who is the customer, and what do they need? When did we last talk, and what was discussed? Are there upsell opportunities? Is the customer satisfied, or are there risk signals? CRM is where those answers live for the whole team, not just you."

### Slide 8 — The CRM Golden Rule
**Visual:** Quote callout + checklist.
**Voiceover:**
"Update CRM while the information is fresh, not when someone asks for it. That means logging updates right after customer meetings, important emails, project discussions, feedback, payment conversations, and new opportunities — not batching it all at the end of the week."

### Slide 9 — Real Example: ABC Architects
**Visual:** Two columns: Without CRM Update / With CRM Update.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's why this matters in practice. A customer mentions an upcoming project on a call. Without a CRM update, Sales never hears about it, you might forget to follow up, and the opportunity gets missed entirely. With a CRM update logged immediately, the opportunity is visible, trackable, and nobody has to rely on memory."

# 🎬 Video Script — Module 02: Customer Success at VBS

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~7–8 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/02 - Customer Success at VBS.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the full scope of a CSM's responsibilities at VBS
- See how onboarding, engagement, health, CRM, invoicing, reviews, and growth connect
- Understand what "success" looks like in this role

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, icon representing a CSM at the center of a wheel of responsibilities.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Success at VBS isn't one task — it's a set of connected responsibilities that all work toward the same goal: making sure customers get real value and stay with us. Let's walk through what that actually covers."

### Slide 2 — Customer Onboarding
**Visual:** Icon: handshake → welcome → kickoff.
**Voiceover:**
"It starts with onboarding — making sure a new customer's first experience with VBS is smooth, well-organized, and sets clear expectations from day one."

### Slide 3 — Customer Engagement
**Visual:** Icon: recurring calendar/communication.
**Voiceover:**
"From there, it's ongoing engagement — regular touchpoints that keep the relationship active, not just reactive check-ins when something goes wrong."

### Slide 4 — Customer Health
**Visual:** RAG (green/yellow/red) indicator graphic.
**Voiceover:**
"We track customer health continuously — using real signals, not gut feel — so we can catch risk early and act on it, not just react after a customer's already frustrated."

### Slide 5 — CRM Management
**Visual:** Simple CRM record mockup.
**Voiceover:**
"Everything we do gets logged in CRM. It's not paperwork for its own sake — it's what makes our work visible, trackable, and handoff-proof."

### Slide 6 — Invoice & Payment Coordination
**Visual:** Icon: invoice/payment.
**Voiceover:**
"CSMs also stay close to the commercial side — invoice timeliness is actually one of the clearest early health signals we have."

### Slide 7 — Business Reviews
**Visual:** Icon: meeting/review cadence (Weekly → Monthly → Quarterly).
**Voiceover:**
"We run a structured cadence of business reviews — weekly internally, monthly and quarterly with the customer — to keep everyone aligned on value delivered and what's next."

### Slide 8 — Growth Opportunities
**Visual:** Icon: upward arrow / expansion.
**Voiceover:**
"And when it's genuinely the right fit, we help identify growth opportunities — upsell, cross-sell, referrals — always grounded in real customer need, never a quota push."

---

## Closing / Call to Action

**Visual:** Summary wheel graphic from Slide 1, all sections now highlighted.
**Voiceover:**
"That's the full picture of Customer Success at VBS — onboarding, engagement, health, CRM, invoicing, reviews, and growth, all working together. Every module after this one goes deep into one of these areas."

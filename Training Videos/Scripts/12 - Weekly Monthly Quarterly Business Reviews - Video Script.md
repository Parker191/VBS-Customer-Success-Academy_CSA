# 🎬 Video Script — Module 12: Weekly, Monthly & Quarterly Business Reviews

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~8–9 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/12 - Weekly Business Reviews (WBR), Monthly Business Reviews (MBR) & Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR).md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the purpose of WBRs, MBRs, and QBRs
- Know when to use each type of review
- Be able to prepare for and run an effective business review

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, calendar/review icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Business Reviews are how VBS stays aligned with customers at every level — from weekly execution to quarterly strategy. This module covers all three: WBR, MBR, and QBR."

### Slide 2 — Why Business Reviews Matter
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"Without a structured review cadence, small issues go unnoticed until they're big ones, and customers lose visibility into the value they're getting. Business Reviews create a predictable rhythm — for the team internally, and for the customer relationship itself."

### Slide 3 — Understanding WBR, MBR & QBR
**Visual:** Three-tier pyramid: Weekly (tactical) → Monthly (operational) → Quarterly (strategic).
**Voiceover:**
"Think of these three as zooming out at each level. Weekly Business Reviews are tactical — day-to-day execution. Monthly reviews are operational — performance and health. Quarterly reviews are strategic — business value and long-term direction."

### Slide 4 — WBR vs MBR vs QBR
**Visual:** Full comparison table.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's the full comparison. WBRs run weekly, 30 to 45 minutes, with the project team, focused on tasks and progress. MBRs run monthly, 45 to 60 minutes, with the operations team, focused on performance and health. QBRs run quarterly, 60 to 90 minutes, with business leaders, focused on strategic value. Same relationship, three different altitudes."

### Slide 5 — Weekly Business Reviews (WBR)
**Visual:** Icon: checklist.
**Voiceover:**
"WBRs are short and action-oriented. Review completed work, discuss current status, identify blockers, and confirm next week's priorities — with clear ownership assigned to each action item. Participants are typically the CSM, the customer's point of contact, and the Delivery Manager."

### Slide 6 — Monthly Business Reviews (MBR)
**Visual:** Icon: bar chart.
**Voiceover:**
"MBRs step back to look at overall performance and account health across the month — not just what happened this week, but the pattern building up. This is where health scores, KPI trends, and emerging risks get real attention."

### Slide 7 — Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)
**Visual:** Icon: strategy/target.
**Voiceover:**
"QBRs are the highest-altitude conversation — business value delivered, strategic alignment, and what's next for the partnership. This is where business leaders on both sides engage directly, and where renewal and growth conversations often begin to take shape."

### Slide 8 — Preparing for Business Reviews
**Visual:** Checklist icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Good reviews are prepared, not improvised. Gather the right data ahead of time, build an agenda matched to the review type, and know what decisions or discussions you actually need from this meeting — don't just show up and talk through a status update."

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand why VBS CSMs work US hours
- Know the standard shift timing
- Know what can cause schedule changes

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, world map graphic highlighting US + India time zones.
**Voiceover:**
"This is a short one — why Customer Success at VBS works the US shift, and what that means day to day."

### Slide 2 — Why the US Shift
**Visual:** Clock/globe icon, list of benefits.
**Voiceover:**
"VBS primarily serves customers across the United States. To respond quickly, attend meetings, and collaborate during their business hours, CSMs generally work the US shift. That means faster email responses, real-time meeting attendance, quicker issue resolution, and stronger relationships built on being actually available — not catching up a day later."

### Slide 3 — Standard Working Hours
**Visual:** Large clock graphic showing 6:00 PM – 4:00 AM IST.
**Voiceover:**
"The standard Customer Success shift is 6 PM to 4 AM IST. Our simple goal: be available when our customers need us."

### Slide 4 — What Can Change the Schedule
**Visual:** List: customer location, project requirements, business needs, special meetings, Daylight Saving Time.
**Voiceover:**
"These timings can shift based on customer location, project requirements, business needs, special meetings, or Daylight Saving Time changes in the US. Always check your Outlook calendar and team announcements for updates — don't assume the schedule is static."

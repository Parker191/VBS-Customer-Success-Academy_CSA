# 🚨 At-Risk Customer Playbook

**Document Type:** Playbook
**Category:** Customer Retention
**Owner:** Customer Success Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## When to Use This Playbook

Use this playbook the moment a customer's Health Score moves to 🟡 Yellow or 🔴 Red, or when any single strong risk signal appears — even if the overall score hasn't dropped yet.

**Common trigger signals:**
- Declining meeting attendance or responsiveness
- Reduced product/service usage
- Repeated invoice delays
- Negative sentiment in emails or meetings
- Escalation raised in the last 30–90 days
- Key stakeholder (champion) has left the company
- Competitor mentioned in conversation
- No engagement with renewal or growth conversations

---

## Step 1 — Confirm the Risk (Day 0–1)

- Complete a [Health Scorecard](../Templates/Health%20Scorecard%20Template.md) to confirm this is a real, evidence-based risk and not a one-off.
- Review CRM history for the last 90 days: meetings, emails, tickets, invoices.
- Identify the likely root cause category: **Value gap**, **Relationship gap**, **Delivery issue**, **Commercial issue**, or **Stakeholder change**.

## Step 2 — Internal Alignment (Day 1–2)

- Flag the account to your manager per the [Escalation Management SOP](../SOPs/Escalation%20Management.md) if severity is high.
- Loop in Delivery/Project team if the root cause is delivery-related.
- Agree internally on what VBS can realistically offer (timeline, resources, concessions) before speaking to the customer.

## Step 3 — Customer Conversation (Day 2–5)

- Request a dedicated call — do not raise this only as part of a routine check-in.
- Lead with listening, not defending. Ask directly what's changed and what "good" looks like for them right now.
- Acknowledge specific issues without over-promising fixes you can't guarantee.
- Agree on 1–3 concrete next steps with dates, however small.

## Step 4 — Recovery Plan (Day 5–10)

- Document the recovery plan using the [Customer Success Plan Template](../Templates/Customer%20Success%20Plan%20Template.md), Section 5 (90-Day Action Plan).
- Set a shorter review cadence (weekly instead of monthly) until health improves.
- Assign clear owners for every action — internal and customer-side.

## Step 5 — Monitor & Re-Score (Ongoing)

- Re-run the Health Scorecard every 2 weeks until the account returns to 🟢 Green for two consecutive checks.
- If health continues to decline after 30 days despite the recovery plan, escalate to leadership for a joint account review — do not let the account drift silently.

---

## Do

- Act on the first real signal, not after churn is imminent.
- Bring evidence (scorecard, CRM history) to internal and customer conversations.
- Keep the customer informed even when there's no new update — silence reads as neglect.

## Don't

- Don't promise commercial terms (discounts, credits, scope changes) without approval.
- Don't rely on a single positive interaction to mark an account "recovered."
- Don't skip documenting the recovery plan in the CRM — undocumented recovery efforts don't hold up at renewal time.

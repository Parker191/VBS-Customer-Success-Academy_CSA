# 🔀 Cross-sell Customer Playbook

**Document Type:** Playbook
**Category:** Growth & Expansion
**Owner:** Customer Success Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## When to Use This Playbook

Use this playbook when a customer's needs suggest a genuine fit for a different VBS product or service line than what they currently have — not simply more of what they already use (that's Upsell).

**Only proceed if:**
- Health status is 🟢 Green
- You can point to a real, stated customer need that the other service line addresses

---

## Step 1 — Identify the Fit

- Listen for needs surfaced in meetings that fall outside your current service line's scope.
- Cross-reference against VBS's other product/service offerings — don't guess; confirm what's actually available.

## Step 2 — Validate Internally

- Check with your manager or the relevant product owner that the fit is genuine, and get current positioning/pricing guidance.

## Step 3 — Introduce the Idea

- Frame it around the specific need the customer raised — "this sounds like a fit for X" — not as a generic pitch.
- Gauge interest before going further; don't push if the customer isn't receptive.

## Step 4 — Hand Off to the Right Team

- Loop in Sales and, if relevant, the owning team for that product/service line.
- Provide full context so the customer doesn't have to re-explain their need from scratch.

## Step 5 — Track Outcome

- Follow up on result and log learnings, particularly around which customer profiles respond well to which cross-sell paths — useful for future playbook refinement.

---

## Do

- Ground every cross-sell conversation in a specific, stated customer need.
- Confirm health status before raising anything commercial.

## Don't

- Don't cross-sell speculatively without a real signal from the customer.
- Don't let this feel like a generic pitch — customers notice the difference.

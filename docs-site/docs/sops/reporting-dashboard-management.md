# 📊 SOP - Reporting & Dashboard Management

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define how CSMs keep CRM data and reporting accurate and current, so leadership has reliable visibility into portfolio health without needing to ask individual CSMs for status.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Keep CRM data accurate and current.
- Produce reliable, consistent reporting inputs.
- Support portfolio-level visibility for leadership.
- Avoid reporting gaps caused by inconsistent data entry.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all CSMs maintaining accounts in the CRM/Internal Tracker.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Maintain Data Hygiene

- Update health status, renewal date, and key contacts in CRM whenever they change — not just before a report is due.
- Follow the [CRM Management SOP](crm-management.md) for field-level standards.

## Step 2 — Weekly Update

- Confirm health scores are current for every account in your portfolio.
- Flag any account with stale data (no update in 30+ days) to your manager.

## Step 3 — Monthly/Quarterly Reporting

- Confirm your portfolio data is accurate before it feeds into management reporting.
- Provide narrative context for any Red or newly-Yellow accounts — numbers alone don't explain root cause.

## Step 4 — Escalate Data Gaps

- If required fields are missing or a report looks wrong, flag it immediately rather than letting inaccurate data reach leadership.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] CRM updated in real time, not batched before reporting
- [ ] Health scores current for all accounts
- [ ] Narrative context provided for at-risk accounts
- [ ] Data gaps flagged proactively

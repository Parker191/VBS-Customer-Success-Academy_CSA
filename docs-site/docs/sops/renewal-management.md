# 🔄 SOP - Renewal Management

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define a consistent process for preparing and running customer renewals so renewal readiness is assessed early, not discovered at the last minute.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Assess renewal readiness well before the renewal date.
- Address risk before it threatens the renewal.
- Run renewal conversations with evidence of value delivered.
- Keep leadership informed of upcoming renewal risk.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all customer accounts within 120 days of their renewal date.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Assess Readiness (120 Days Out)

- Review current Health Scorecard and 90-day history.
- Confirm the customer's goals from the Customer Success Plan have been met or are on track.
- Classify renewal confidence: Likely, At Risk, or Uncertain.

## Step 2 — Prepare (90 Days Out)

- If health is 🟡 or 🔴, activate the At-Risk Customer Playbook (in the repository Customer Playbooks folder) before proceeding.
- Compile evidence of value delivered (usage, outcomes achieved, issues resolved).

## Step 3 — Engage the Customer (60 Days Out)

- Send the Renewal Discussion email and schedule a Renewal Planning Meeting (see Meeting Templates section).
- Confirm commercial terms with your manager before discussing them with the customer.

## Step 4 — Confirm & Close (30 Days Out)

- Confirm renewal decision in writing.
- Update CRM with the renewal outcome and new renewal date.
- If not renewing, document the reason for future learning.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Readiness assessed at 120 days
- [ ] At-risk accounts triaged before renewal outreach
- [ ] Value evidence compiled
- [ ] Renewal outcome documented in CRM

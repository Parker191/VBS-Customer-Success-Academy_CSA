# 📊 SOP - Business Reviews

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define a consistent, repeatable process for running Weekly, Monthly, and Quarterly Business Reviews (WBR/MBR/QBR) so customers receive predictable visibility into value delivered, and CSMs surface risks and opportunities early.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Run reviews with consistent structure and cadence.
- Demonstrate value delivered against customer goals.
- Surface risks before they escalate.
- Identify growth opportunities in context, not as a sales pitch.
- Keep leadership informed through consistent reporting.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all active customer accounts, from onboarding completion through renewal.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Determine Cadence

| Review Type | Frequency | Typical Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly Business Review (WBR) | Weekly | CSM + internal delivery team |
| Monthly Business Review (MBR) | Monthly | CSM + customer day-to-day contact |
| Quarterly Business Review (QBR) | Quarterly | CSM + customer stakeholders/leadership |

## Step 2 — Prepare

- Pull current Health Scorecard and CRM activity history.
- Review open action items from the last review.
- Confirm progress against goals in the Customer Success Plan.

## Step 3 — Run the Review

- Use the corresponding Meeting Template (see the Meeting Templates section of this site) (MBR/QBR).
- Lead with outcomes and value delivered, not just activity completed.
- Surface risks directly rather than downplaying them.

## Step 4 — Document & Follow Up

- Log meeting notes and action items in CRM within 24 hours.
- Update the Customer Success Plan with any new goals or risks.
- Send a summary to customer stakeholders who didn't attend.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Cadence confirmed with customer
- [ ] Health Scorecard reviewed before the meeting
- [ ] Action items from last review checked
- [ ] Meeting notes logged in CRM
- [ ] Follow-up summary sent

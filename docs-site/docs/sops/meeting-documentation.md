# 📝 SOP - Meeting Documentation

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define a consistent standard for documenting customer meetings so that decisions, commitments, and action items are never lost to memory or scattered across personal notes.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Capture accurate, complete meeting notes.
- Track action items to closure.
- Keep CRM as the single source of truth for customer history.
- Hand off context cleanly if an account changes owners.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all customer-facing meetings: kickoffs, check-ins, business reviews, escalation reviews, and renewal discussions.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Before the Meeting

- Use the relevant Meeting Template (see the Meeting Templates section of this site) for the meeting type.
- Review prior notes and open action items before joining.

## Step 2 — During the Meeting

- Capture decisions and commitments in real time, not from memory afterward.
- Note who owns each action item and by when.

## Step 3 — After the Meeting

- Log notes in CRM within 24 hours of the meeting.
- Send a summary to the customer if any commitments were made.
- Update the Customer Success Plan if goals or timelines changed.

## Step 4 — Track

- Review open action items before every subsequent meeting.
- Flag any item overdue by more than one cycle to your manager.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Correct meeting template used
- [ ] Notes logged in CRM within 24 hours
- [ ] Action items assigned with owners and dates
- [ ] Customer sent a summary where commitments were made

# 🧩 SOP - Project Coordination

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define how CSMs coordinate with the Delivery team so customers experience a single, consistent voice from VBS rather than conflicting updates from Sales, CSM, and Delivery.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Stay accurately informed on project/delivery status.
- Represent delivery timelines to customers accurately.
- Flag delivery risk before the customer feels it.
- Keep internal teams aligned on customer-facing commitments.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all active customer accounts with an ongoing delivery/project component.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Sync Regularly

- Attend or review the Internal Delivery Sync at the agreed cadence (see Internal Delivery Sync Meeting Template (see Meeting Templates section)).
- Confirm current status, blockers, and any timeline changes before speaking to the customer.

## Step 2 — Translate for the Customer

- Convert internal/technical delivery status into customer-facing language.
- Never share an internal timeline with the customer that Delivery hasn't confirmed.

## Step 3 — Flag Risk Early

- If a delivery delay or scope issue is likely to affect the customer, raise it internally as soon as it's known — don't wait for the next scheduled sync.
- Decide jointly with Delivery how and when to communicate the change to the customer.

## Step 4 — Close the Loop

- Confirm with Delivery once a milestone is customer-visible before communicating it as complete.
- Log any customer-facing commitment made during coordination in CRM.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Delivery sync attended or reviewed on schedule
- [ ] Customer-facing status reflects confirmed Delivery status only
- [ ] Risks flagged internally as soon as identified
- [ ] Customer commitments logged in CRM

# 📈 SOP - Growth & Expansion

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define how CSMs identify and pursue legitimate upsell, cross-sell, and referral opportunities — grounded in genuine customer need, not sales pressure.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Identify growth opportunities that are genuinely aligned to customer goals.
- Time growth conversations appropriately (never during a Red health status).
- Hand off qualified opportunities to Sales cleanly.
- Track outcomes for future planning.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to all active, healthy customer accounts (🟢 Green or stable 🟡 Yellow only).

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Identify

- Review the customer's stated goals in their Customer Success Plan.
- Look for genuine gaps between current scope and what would help them succeed further.
- Confirm health status is 🟢 Green before raising any growth conversation — never upsell into a Red account.

## Step 2 — Validate

- Confirm the opportunity is need-driven, not just contract-value-driven.
- Check with your manager if the opportunity involves a new service line.

## Step 3 — Introduce

- Raise the opportunity naturally in context (e.g., during a Business Review), not as a cold pitch.
- Use the Upsell & Cross-sell Opportunity Email template if following up in writing.

## Step 4 — Hand Off

- Once the customer expresses interest, loop in Sales with full context (what was discussed, what the customer needs, timeline).
- Log the opportunity in CRM as a tracked growth opportunity.

## Step 5 — Track

- Follow up on outcome (won/lost/deferred) and record learnings for future planning.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Health status confirmed 🟢 Green before raising opportunity
- [ ] Opportunity is need-driven, not quota-driven
- [ ] Opportunity logged in CRM
- [ ] Sales handoff includes full context
- [ ] Outcome tracked

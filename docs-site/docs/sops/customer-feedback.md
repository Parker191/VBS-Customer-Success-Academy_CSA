# 💬 SOP - Customer Feedback

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define how CSMs collect, document, and act on customer feedback so it consistently improves the customer relationship instead of being lost in individual conversations.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Capture feedback consistently across every customer touchpoint.
- Distinguish between one-off complaints and recurring themes.
- Route feedback to the right internal owner.
- Close the loop with the customer.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to feedback received through meetings, emails, surveys, or informal conversation with any active customer.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Capture

- Log feedback in CRM immediately after receiving it, in the customer's own words where possible.
- Tag it by category (Product, Delivery, Communication, Commercial, Other).

## Step 2 — Assess

- Determine if this is a one-off comment or a recurring pattern (check CRM history).
- Classify severity: informational, needs follow-up, or urgent/escalation-worthy.

## Step 3 — Route

- Route to the relevant internal owner (Delivery, Product, Finance) if outside CSM control.
- If severity is urgent, follow the [Escalation Management SOP](escalation-management.md) instead.

## Step 4 — Close the Loop

- Always respond to the customer, even if the answer is "we're looking into it."
- Confirm any resulting action once it's complete — silence after feedback erodes trust faster than the original issue.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Feedback logged in CRM with category and severity
- [ ] Internal owner identified if needed
- [ ] Customer received acknowledgement
- [ ] Loop closed with a follow-up once resolved

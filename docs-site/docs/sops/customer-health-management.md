# 💚 SOP - Customer Health Management

> **SOP Owner:** Customer Success Team
> **Department:** Customer Success
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 📌 Purpose

Define how CSMs measure, monitor, and act on customer health so risk is identified early and consistently across the portfolio, rather than depending on individual CSM intuition.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Score customer health objectively using the VBS RAG framework.
- Monitor health on a consistent cadence.
- Trigger the right response when health declines.
- Keep leadership informed of portfolio-level risk.

---

# 👥 Scope

Applies to every active customer account from the end of onboarding through renewal.

---

# 📝 Process

## Step 1 — Score

- Complete a Health Scorecard Template (in the repository Templates folder) monthly, or immediately after any risk signal.
- Base scores on evidence (CRM activity, meeting attendance, payment history), not general impression.

## Step 2 — Classify

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | Healthy — standard cadence continues |
| 🟡 Yellow | At Risk — increase monitoring, engage proactively |
| 🔴 Red | Critical — activate the At-Risk Customer Playbook |

## Step 3 — Respond

- 🟢 Green: continue standard Business Review cadence.
- 🟡 Yellow: notify your manager, increase check-in frequency, review the Customer Success Plan.
- 🔴 Red: immediately follow the At-Risk Customer Playbook (in the repository Customer Playbooks folder).

## Step 4 — Report

- Update CRM with the current health status after every scoring cycle.
- Include health status in every Business Review and portfolio reporting cycle.

---

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Health Scorecard completed on schedule
- [ ] Status classified using RAG framework
- [ ] Appropriate response triggered for the status
- [ ] CRM updated with current status

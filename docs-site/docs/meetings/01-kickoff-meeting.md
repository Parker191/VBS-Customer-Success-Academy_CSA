# 📌 Purpose

The Kickoff Meeting officially launches the customer engagement by aligning expectations, introducing key stakeholders, confirming project scope, and establishing communication guidelines.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of this meeting, both teams should have:

- A shared understanding of project goals.
- Confirmed scope and deliverables.
- Defined communication channels.
- Agreed meeting cadence.
- Identified project risks.
- Confirmed immediate next steps.

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A successful kickoff meeting should:

- Focus on business outcomes, not just deliverables.
- Encourage customer participation.
- Clarify assumptions early.
- Confirm communication expectations.
- End with clearly assigned action items.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Starting work before scope confirmation.
- Leaving roles and responsibilities unclear.
- Not documenting action items.
- Skipping risk discussions.
- Failing to send meeting notes within 24 hours.

# 📌 Purpose

Conduct a structured weekly review of project progress, discuss completed work, identify risks or blockers, and align on priorities for the upcoming week.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of this meeting:

- Review completed work.
- Discuss current project status.
- Identify risks and blockers.
- Confirm upcoming priorities.
- Assign action items.
- Ensure customer alignment.

---

# ⏱ Duration

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A Weekly Project Meeting should:

- Focus on progress and outcomes.
- Review action items from the previous meeting.
- Surface risks early.
- Keep discussions solution-oriented.
- End with clear ownership and due dates.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Reviewing completed work without discussing upcoming priorities.
- Allowing unresolved action items to carry over repeatedly.
- Failing to document customer decisions.
- Not escalating risks in time.
- Ending the meeting without confirming next steps.

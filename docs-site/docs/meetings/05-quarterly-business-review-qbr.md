---

# 📌 Purpose

Review quarterly business outcomes, measure the value delivered by Virtual Building Studio, discuss strategic priorities, and identify opportunities for continued partnership.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Review quarterly achievements.
- Demonstrate business value delivered.
- Discuss customer goals.
- Review Customer Health Score.
- Identify improvement opportunities.
- Plan the next quarter.

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A successful QBR should:

- Focus on business outcomes, not task updates.
- Use data to demonstrate value.
- Celebrate achievements.
- Discuss strategic priorities.
- Identify future opportunities.
- Finish with a clear action plan.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Turning the QBR into a weekly status meeting.
- Spending too much time on operational details.
- Ignoring executive priorities.
- Presenting data without explaining business impact.

## 🎯 Customer Success Insight

A QBR is the customer's opportunity to see the value of the partnership—not just the work completed.

Every discussion should answer:

- What value did VBS deliver?
- How did it help the customer's business?
- What should we achieve together next quarter?

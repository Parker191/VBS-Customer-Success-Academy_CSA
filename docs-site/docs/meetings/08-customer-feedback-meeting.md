# 📌 Purpose

Collect structured customer feedback, understand satisfaction levels, identify improvement opportunities, and strengthen the customer relationship.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Understand customer satisfaction.
- Discuss what's working well.
- Identify areas for improvement.
- Capture enhancement requests.
- Agree on follow-up actions.

---

# ⏱ Duration

30–45 Minutes

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A Customer Feedback Meeting should:

- Encourage honest and open discussion.
- Listen more than you speak.
- Ask follow-up questions.
- Thank the customer for every suggestion.
- Turn feedback into actionable improvements.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Becoming defensive.
- Interrupting the customer.
- Ignoring constructive criticism.
- Making promises that cannot be delivered.

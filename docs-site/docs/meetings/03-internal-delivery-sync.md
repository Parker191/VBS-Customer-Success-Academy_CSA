# 📌 Purpose

Align the Customer Success and Delivery teams on project progress, risks, priorities, resource requirements, and customer updates before external communication.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of this meeting:

- Review current project status.
- Identify delivery risks.
- Discuss customer concerns.
- Confirm upcoming deliverables.
- Prepare for customer communication.
- Assign internal action items.

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

An Internal Delivery Sync should:

- Be completed before customer-facing meetings.
- Focus on facts and data.
- Surface issues early.
- Confirm ownership of every action item.
- Ensure all customer updates are accurate and consistent.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Discussing issues with the customer before internal alignment.
- Assuming tasks are complete without verification.
- Leaving risks undocumented.
- Not assigning owners to action items.
- Failing to prepare for upcoming customer meetings.

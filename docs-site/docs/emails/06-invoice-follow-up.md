# 💳 Invoice Follow-up Email

> **Category:** Finance Support
>
> **Owner:** Customer Success Team
>
> **Version:** 1.0
>
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

Follow up on outstanding invoices professionally while maintaining a positive customer relationship.

---

## When to Use

- Invoice due date has passed.
- Payment confirmation has not been received.
- Customer requests invoice details.
- Finance team requests CSM support.

## Email Template

**Subject:** Friendly Reminder – Invoice #[Invoice Number]

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

I wanted to follow up regarding **Invoice #[Invoice Number]**, which was due on **[Due Date]**.

For your convenience, here are the invoice details:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Invoice Number | [Invoice Number] |
| Invoice Date | [Invoice Date] |
| Due Date | [Due Date] |
| Amount | [Invoice Amount] |

If the payment has already been processed, please disregard this email and accept our thanks.

If not, we would appreciate it if you could let us know the expected payment timeline. This will help us keep our records updated.

Should you need another copy of the invoice or have any questions, please let me know and I'll be happy to assist.

Thank you for your continued partnership.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

When following up on invoices:

- Be polite and professional.
- Assume positive intent.
- Never pressure the customer.
- Offer assistance if needed.
- Keep Finance informed.
- Record all communication in the CRM.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Invoice number verified
- [ ] Due date confirmed
- [ ] Amount verified
- [ ] Payment status checked
- [ ] Finance team informed (if required)

# 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Don't assume the customer is intentionally delaying payment.
- Avoid aggressive or demanding language.
- Never copy unnecessary recipients.
- Don't forget to verify invoice details before sending.
- Always acknowledge if the customer has already paid.

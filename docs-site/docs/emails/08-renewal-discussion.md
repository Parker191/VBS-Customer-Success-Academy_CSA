# 🔄 Renewal Discussion Email

> **Category:** Customer Retention
>
> **Owner:** Customer Success Team
>
> **Version:** 1.0
>
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

Initiate a renewal discussion by reviewing the partnership, highlighting delivered value, and planning for the next engagement period.

---

## When to Use

- 90–120 days before contract renewal
- Before DRM renewal discussions
- Prior to annual contract reviews
- After a successful project completion

## Email Template

**Subject:** Planning Ahead – Renewal Discussion for [Customer Name]

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

As we approach the next phase of our partnership, I'd like to schedule some time to review the progress we've made together and discuss your plans moving forward.

Our goal is to ensure that Virtual Building Studio continues to support your business objectives and upcoming project requirements.

### Discussion Topics

- Review of completed work
- Current project status
- Business goals for the coming months
- Upcoming resource requirements
- Opportunities to improve collaboration
- Renewal planning

Please let me know a convenient time for a 30-minute discussion, or feel free to suggest an alternative that works for your team.

I look forward to continuing our partnership.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio'

---

## Renewal Preparation Checklist

Before sending this email:

- [ ] Customer Health Score reviewed
- [ ] Recent project performance reviewed
- [ ] Outstanding issues resolved
- [ ] Invoice/payment status checked
- [ ] Business Review completed
- [ ] CRM renewal date verified
- [ ] Renewal opportunity updated in CRM

---

## 📈 Value Summary (Internal Preparation)

Before speaking with the customer, prepare a short summary covering:

- Projects completed
- Key milestones achieved
- Turnaround improvements
- Customer feedback received
- Business outcomes delivered
- Opportunities for future collaboration

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A renewal conversation should never feel like a last-minute request.

Start early and focus on:

- Business outcomes
- Partnership value
- Future goals
- Continuous improvement

Avoid making the conversation only about pricing.

---

## 💬 Communication Tip

Customers renew when they clearly understand the value they have received.

Lead the conversation with outcomes and achievements before discussing commercial terms.

## 🎯 Customer Success Insight

Renewals are the result of consistent customer success throughout the relationship.

The renewal conversation should confirm value that has already been delivered—not introduce it for the first time.

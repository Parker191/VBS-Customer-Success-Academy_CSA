# 📧 Welcome Email Template

## Purpose

Introduce the Customer Success Manager, welcome the customer, and prepare them for onboarding.

---

## When to Use

Send within 24 hours of the customer handover from Sales/KAM.

---

## Email Template

**Subject:** Welcome to Virtual Building Studio – Your Customer Success Manager

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

Welcome to **Virtual Building Studio (VBS)**!

My name is **[Your Name]**, and I will be your dedicated Customer Success Manager.

I'm excited to work with you and support your team throughout our partnership.

My role is to ensure:

- Smooth onboarding
- Clear communication
- Timely project coordination
- Regular business reviews
- Long-term success

As a next step, I'd like to schedule a kickoff meeting to:

- Introduce the project team
- Review project goals
- Confirm timelines
- Discuss communication preferences
- Answer any questions

Please let me know a convenient time, or feel free to choose a suitable slot from my calendar.

Looking forward to working together!

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

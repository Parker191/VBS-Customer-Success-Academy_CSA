# 🚨 Escalation Acknowledgement Email

> **Category:** Customer Communication
>
> **Owner:** Customer Success Team
>
> **Version:** 1.0
>
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

Acknowledge a customer concern promptly, confirm ownership of the issue, and communicate the next steps.

---

## When to Use

- Customer reports a critical issue.
- A delivery delay impacts the customer.
- A quality concern is raised.
- A project is at risk.
- The issue has been classified as an escalation.

## Email Template

**Subject:** We Have Received Your Concern – Next Steps

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

Thank you for bringing this to our attention.

I understand the importance of this issue and appreciate you taking the time to let us know.

Please be assured that I have taken ownership of this escalation and have already engaged the relevant internal teams to investigate the situation.

### Current Status

- Escalation logged
- Internal review initiated
- Responsible teams notified

### What Happens Next

1. We will investigate the issue.
2. We will identify the root cause.
3. We will share an update with you by **[Date/Time]**.
4. We will work with you until the issue is fully resolved.

Thank you for your patience and continued partnership.

If you have any additional information that may help our investigation, please feel free to reply to this email.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

When acknowledging an escalation:

- Respond as quickly as possible.
- Acknowledge the customer's concern.
- Take ownership.
- Avoid assigning blame.
- Share a realistic update timeline.
- Keep communication consistent until resolution.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Delaying the first response.
- Promising timelines that cannot be met.
- Blaming another team.
- Closing the issue before confirming customer satisfaction.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Escalation severity confirmed.
- [ ] Customer acknowledged.
- [ ] Internal team notified.
- [ ] CRM updated.
- [ ] Next customer update scheduled.

## 💬 Communication Tip

Customers remember **how** you respond during difficult situations more than the issue itself.

A quick acknowledgement, clear ownership, and regular updates can significantly improve trust, even before the problem is fully resolved.

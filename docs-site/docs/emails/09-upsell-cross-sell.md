## Purpose

Introduce additional VBS services that align with the customer's business goals and ongoing projects, focusing on delivering more value rather than making a sales pitch.

---

## When to Use

- Customer workload increases.
- New project types are discussed.
- Existing projects are expanding.
- During Quarterly Business Reviews.
- After successful project delivery.

## Email Template

**Subject:** Exploring Additional Ways We Can Support Your Team

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

Over the past few months, it has been a pleasure supporting your team, and I'm glad we've been able to contribute to your projects.

Based on our recent conversations and your upcoming work, I thought it might be helpful to discuss a few additional ways Virtual Building Studio could support your team.

Some areas that may be valuable include:

- Additional BIM modeling capacity
- Dedicated Resource Model (DRM)
- Scan to BIM services
- CAD to BIM conversion
- Construction documentation support

There is absolutely no urgency—I simply wanted to make you aware of these options in case they align with your future plans.

If you're interested, I'd be happy to schedule a short discussion to better understand your upcoming requirements.

Thank you for your continued partnership.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

## Opportunity Qualification Checklist

Before sending:

- [ ] Customer Health Score is Green or stable.
- [ ] Recent projects delivered successfully.
- [ ] Customer satisfaction is positive.
- [ ] Business need identified.
- [ ] Delivery team consulted (if required).
- [ ] Sales/KAM informed of the opportunity.
- [ ] CRM opportunity updated.

---

# 🔍 Customer Growth Signals

Consider discussing additional services when the customer:

- Mentions hiring challenges.
- Has increasing project volume.
- Requests faster turnaround times.
- Expands into new markets.
- Starts larger or more complex projects.
- Asks about additional VBS capabilities.

---

## Services to Consider

| Customer Need | VBS Service |
|---------------|-------------|
| More delivery capacity | Dedicated Resource Model (DRM) |
| Existing drawings | CAD to BIM |
| Site scan data | Scan to BIM |
| Larger BIM projects | BIM Modeling (LOD 300–500) |
| Ongoing support | Dedicated offshore team |

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

Growth conversations should always begin with the customer's goals—not VBS services.

Ask questions such as:

- What projects are coming up?
- What challenges is your team facing?
- Where do you need additional capacity?
- How can we make your team's work easier?

Recommend services only when they genuinely address a customer need.

---

## 🎯 Customer Success Insight

The strongest upsell opportunities come from trust.

When customers consistently achieve positive outcomes, they are more likely to explore additional ways of working with VBS.

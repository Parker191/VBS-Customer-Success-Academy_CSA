## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Know the core tools every CSM uses daily
- Understand what each tool is for, at a glance
- Know where to go for deeper tool-specific guidance

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Grid of tool logos/icons (CRM, email, calendar, OneDrive, etc.)
**Voiceover:**
"Every CSM works across a handful of core tools every day. This module is a quick tour so you know what each one is for before you dive into the detailed guides."

### Slide 2 — Zoho CRM
**Visual:** Screen recording: Zoho CRM dashboard.
**Voiceover:**
"Zoho CRM is where customer records, health scores, and interaction history live. If it's not in CRM, it effectively didn't happen — this is your single source of truth."

### Slide 3 — Email
**Visual:** Screen recording: inbox with templated email open.
**Voiceover:**
"Email is still where most customer communication happens. You'll be using the Academy's Email Templates library regularly to keep messaging consistent and professional."

### Slide 4 — Calendar
**Visual:** Screen recording: calendar with recurring customer meetings.
**Voiceover:**
"Your calendar reflects your review cadence — weekly internal syncs, monthly and quarterly customer reviews. Keeping this current is part of keeping the whole team aligned."

### Slide 5 — Microsoft OneDrive
**Visual:** Screen recording: OneDrive folder structure for a customer account.
**Voiceover:**
"OneDrive is where account documentation lives — meeting notes, success plans, shared files with the customer. Following a consistent folder structure here saves everyone time during handovers."

### Slide 6 — Putting It Together
**Visual:** Simple workflow diagram: CRM ↔ Email ↔ Calendar ↔ OneDrive.
**Voiceover:**
"These tools aren't separate silos — they work together. A meeting on your calendar leads to notes in OneDrive, which gets summarized in CRM, which might trigger a follow-up email. Once this rhythm feels natural, the day-to-day gets a lot easier."

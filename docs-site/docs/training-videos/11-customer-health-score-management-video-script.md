# 🎬 Video Script — Module 11: Customer Health Score Management

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~9–10 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/11 – Customer Health Score Management.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand what a Customer Health Score is and why it matters
- Know the RAG (Green/Yellow/Red) framework
- Understand the 100-point scoring model and its six components

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, heartbeat/pulse icon.
**Voiceover:**
"This is one of the most important skills you'll build in this Academy — how to measure customer health, objectively, before problems become visible on their own."

### Slide 2 — Why Customer Health Matters
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"Health tracking lets you catch risk early, prioritize your time across a portfolio, have informed conversations with your manager, and act before a customer ever has to raise a complaint. Waiting for the customer to tell you something's wrong means you're already behind."

### Slide 3 — Health vs Happiness
**Visual:** Two-column comparison.
**Voiceover:**
"These aren't the same thing. A customer can be happy in a single meeting but still be at long-term risk — maybe they're not using the service fully, or a champion just left the company. Health looks at the whole pattern, not just the most recent interaction."

### Slide 4 — The RAG Framework
**Visual:** Three colored blocks: Green, Yellow, Red.
**Voiceover:**
"VBS uses a simple, visual framework: Green, Yellow, and Red. Green means healthy — maintain the relationship. Yellow means attention needed — monitor closely. Red means at risk — immediate action required. The power of RAG is that anyone on the team can glance at an account and instantly understand where it stands."

### Slide 5 — RAG Comparison Table
**Visual:** Table: Communication, Sentiment, Progress, Payment, Opportunities across Green/Yellow/Red.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's what each status actually looks like in practice. Communication regular versus slower versus no response. Sentiment positive versus mixed versus negative. Payment on time versus occasional delays versus overdue. This table is your quick reference for classifying any account."

### Slide 6 — The 100-Point Health Score Model
**Visual:** Bar breakdown: Communication 20, Engagement 20, Delivery 20, Payment 15, Growth 15, Relationship 10.
**Voiceover:**
"For a more precise score, VBS uses a 100-point model across six components: Communication and Customer Engagement each worth 20 points, Project Delivery worth 20, Payment Behaviour and Growth Opportunity each worth 15, and Relationship Strength worth 10. Together, these give you a number, not just a color — useful for tracking trends over time."

### Slide 7 — Why Multiple Components?
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"No single factor tells the whole story. A customer might pay on time but be disengaged. Or be highly engaged but facing delivery delays. Scoring across six components forces a fuller, more honest picture than any one signal alone."

### Slide 8 — Best Practices
**Visual:** Checklist.
**Voiceover:**
"Score based on evidence, not gut feel. Review on a consistent cadence, not just when something feels off. Document the reasoning behind a score, not just the number. And remember — health status can change. An account that was Green last month isn't automatically Green today."

### Slide 9 — Common Mistakes
**Visual:** Warning icon list.
**Voiceover:**
"The most common mistakes: scoring from memory instead of CRM data, only updating scores when asked, treating one bad meeting as a permanent Red status, and — just as risky — assuming a quiet account is automatically a healthy one."

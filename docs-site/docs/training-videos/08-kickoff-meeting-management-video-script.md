## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the purpose of a kickoff meeting
- Know how to prepare for and run one professionally
- Understand roles, communication planning, and how to document outcomes

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, meeting/podium icon.
**Voiceover:**
"The kickoff meeting is the customer's first formal meeting with the VBS project team — it sets the tone for everything that follows. Let's talk about how to run it well."

### Slide 2 — What is a Kickoff Meeting?
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"A kickoff meeting is the first official project meeting between customer and VBS team. Its job: introduce all stakeholders, review scope, confirm timelines, explain communication methods, clarify roles, address questions, and agree on next steps. Done well, everyone leaves with the same understanding of what happens next."

### Slide 3 — Preparation
**Visual:** Checklist icon.
**Voiceover:**
"A good kickoff starts long before the meeting itself. As CSM, you confirm attendees, prepare materials, and make sure everyone internally knows their role. Good preparation is what makes you look professional and builds customer confidence from minute one."

### Slide 4 — Standard Meeting Agenda
**Visual:** Agenda list with rough timing.
**Voiceover:**
"There's a standard structure: welcome and introductions, project scope review, timeline discussion, communication plan, Q&A, and next steps. Having a consistent agenda means nothing important gets missed, even under time pressure."

### Slide 5 — Running the Meeting
**Visual:** Speech bubble icon.
**Voiceover:**
"As the CSM, you're typically the one opening the meeting, guiding transitions between topics, and closing with clear next steps. Confidence here matters — this is the customer's first real impression of how VBS operates."

### Slide 6 — Communication Plan
**Visual:** Phone/email icons.
**Voiceover:**
"Before the meeting ends, agree on how you'll communicate going forward — primary contacts, preferred channels, meeting cadence, and response time expectations. Setting this explicitly avoids friction later."

### Slide 7 — Documenting the Outcome
**Visual:** Meeting minutes document icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Immediately after, capture Meeting Minutes — attendees, discussion summary, decisions, and action items — and share them with the customer. This isn't just documentation for its own sake; it's what makes the next 90 days go smoothly."

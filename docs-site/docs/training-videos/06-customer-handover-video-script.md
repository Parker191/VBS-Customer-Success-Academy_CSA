# 🎬 Video Script — Module 06: Customer Handover

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~6–7 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/06 - Customer Handover.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the Customer Handover process
- Identify the responsibilities of Sales and Customer Success
- Know how to prepare for a successful customer transition

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, handoff/relay icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Handover is where your relationship with a customer actually starts — the process of transferring ownership of an account from Sales to you, the CSM. Let's walk through how to do it well."

### Slide 2 — What is Customer Handover?
**Visual:** Sales → CSM transfer diagram.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Handover is the formal process of transferring account ownership from Sales to Customer Success. It ensures all customer information, project details, expectations, and commitments are communicated accurately before you start managing the account — so you never have to ask the customer to repeat what they already told Sales."

### Slide 3 — Why It Matters
**Visual:** Two columns: Benefits for Customer / Benefits for VBS.
**Voiceover:**
"For the customer, a good handover means they don't repeat themselves, they know their point of contact immediately, and they get faster responses from day one. For VBS, it means fewer communication gaps, stronger internal alignment, and a customer who trusts us from the very first interaction."

### Slide 4 — Objectives of the Handover Process
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"The goal is simple: a smooth transition, complete customer information in your hands, minimal communication gaps, customer confidence from interaction one, and full internal alignment on expectations and project goals."

### Slide 5 — Roles & Responsibilities
**Visual:** Sales column vs CSM column.
**Voiceover:**
"Sales owns the relationship up to the handover point — they bring the context, commitments, and history. Once handover happens, you own it — verifying that information, updating internal systems, and becoming the customer's primary point of contact."

### Slide 6 — The Internal Handover Meeting
**Visual:** Meeting agenda icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Before you ever speak with the customer, there's an internal handover meeting between Sales and CSM — confirming scope, commitments made during the sales process, key stakeholders, and any known risks. This is your one chance to ask questions before the customer is watching."

### Slide 7 — Customer Introduction
**Visual:** Email icon.
**Voiceover:**
"After the internal meeting, you send the Customer Introduction email — your first direct touchpoint, setting a professional, confident tone for everything that follows."

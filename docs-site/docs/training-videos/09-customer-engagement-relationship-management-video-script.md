# 🎬 Video Script — Module 09: Customer Engagement & Relationship Management

**Video Type:** Voiceover + Slides
**Target Runtime:** ~9–10 minutes
**Source Module:** Modules/09 - Customer Engagement & Relationship Management.md
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand why ongoing customer engagement matters
- Know the customer lifecycle and recommended communication cadence
- Understand how to build trust and manage expectations

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, handshake/heart icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Winning a customer is only the beginning. This module is about what keeps them — engaged, supported, and successful for the long term."

### Slide 2 — Why This Matters
**Visual:** List of disengagement causes.
**Voiceover:**
"Customers rarely leave because of one big issue. More often, it's poor communication, slow responses, lack of proactive follow-up, unclear expectations, or just feeling ignored. Your job as CSM is to prevent exactly these situations."

### Slide 3 — The Customer Success Principle
**Visual:** Quote callout.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's the principle that guides this whole module: customers don't remember every email you send — they remember how easy you made it to work with VBS. Your goal isn't just managing projects. It's building trust and reducing customer effort."

### Slide 4 — What is Customer Engagement?
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Engagement is the continuous process of building and maintaining a strong relationship throughout the customer's journey. It's not limited to project discussions — it's regular communication, understanding goals, delivering value, and making sure customers feel supported at every stage."

### Slide 5 — The Customer Lifecycle
**Visual:** Vertical flow: Lead → Sales → Handover → Onboarding → Kickoff → Delivery → Engagement → Success → Renewal/Upsell/Referral.
**Voiceover:**
"Every customer moves through a lifecycle — from lead, through sales, handover, onboarding, kickoff, and delivery, into ongoing engagement and success, eventually reaching renewal, upsell, or referral. Your role as CSM spans from handover all the way through renewal."

### Slide 6 — Communication Cadence
**Visual:** Table: activity, frequency, owner.
**Voiceover:**
"Consistency beats intensity. The recommended cadence: weekly project status updates, bi-weekly check-in calls, quarterly executive reviews and satisfaction checks, and renewal discussions starting 60 to 90 days before contract end. This isn't busywork — a consistent cadence reduces uncertainty and helps you catch issues early, before the customer has to raise them."

### Slide 7 — Customer Touchpoints
**Visual:** Icon grid: email, meeting, call, review, feedback.
**Voiceover:**
"Every touchpoint — from the welcome email to the QBR to a renewal discussion — shapes how the customer experiences VBS. The mindset behind every single one: did we make it easier for the customer to work with us today?"

### Slide 8 — Relationship Building
**Visual:** Trust equation: Reliable, Responsive, Knowledgeable, Honest, Consistent.
**Voiceover:**
"Strong relationships come from consistency, trust, and real value — not just frequent contact. Customers trust CSMs who are reliable, responsive, knowledgeable, honest, and consistent. Respond promptly, keep your commitments, and be proactive about sharing updates, even when there's nothing urgent to report."

### Slide 9 — Managing Expectations
**Visual:** Table: Project Scope, Deliverables, Timeline, Communication.
**Voiceover:**
"One of your most important jobs is setting and managing expectations — scope, deliverables, timelines, and how you'll communicate. Clear expectations up front prevent misunderstandings and protect trust later."

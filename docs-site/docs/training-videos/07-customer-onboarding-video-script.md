## Learning Objectives (Recap for Video Open)

- Understand the Customer Onboarding process
- Know how to prepare for a successful kickoff
- Understand roles and responsibilities across teams during onboarding

---

## Slide-by-Slide Script

### Slide 1 — Title
**Visual:** Module title, rocket/launch icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Onboarding is one of the most important stages in the customer journey. Get it right, and you set the tone for the entire relationship. Let's walk through how VBS does it."

### Slide 2 — What is Customer Onboarding?
**Visual:** Bullet list.
**Voiceover:**
"Customer Onboarding is the structured process of helping a new customer move from the sales process into active project execution — introducing the CSM and Delivery team, confirming scope, scheduling kickoff, establishing communication channels, and preparing the customer for delivery. The goal is simple: the customer should feel informed, supported, and confident before work begins."

### Slide 3 — The Onboarding Workflow
**Visual:** Vertical flow diagram: Handover → CSM Reviews Info → Introduction Email → Kickoff Scheduled → Internal Prep → Kickoff Conducted → Minutes Shared → CRM Updated → Tracker Updated → Execution Begins.
**Voiceover:**
"Here's the full flow: once handover is complete, you review the customer information, send the introduction email, schedule and prepare for kickoff, conduct the meeting, share minutes, update CRM and the internal tracker — and then project execution begins. Every step matters because it keeps the customer and VBS aligned before real work starts."

### Slide 4 — Roles & Responsibilities
**Visual:** Table: CSM, Delivery Manager, Sales, Finance, Customer.
**Voiceover:**
"The CSM leads the process — coordinating meetings, communicating with the customer, and keeping CRM and the tracker current. Delivery reviews requirements and prepares the team. Sales clarifies any commercial commitments. Finance supports billing setup. And the customer's role matters too — sharing requirements, confirming timelines, and showing up to the kickoff."

### Slide 5 — Kickoff Meeting Preparation
**Visual:** Checklist icon.
**Voiceover:**
"Before the kickoff itself, there's real preparation — confirming attendees, reviewing scope, and making sure the customer folder and CRM record are ready. Good prep is what makes the meeting feel effortless to the customer."

### Slide 6 — OneDrive & CRM Setup
**Visual:** Folder + CRM icons.
**Voiceover:**
"Two things need to happen in parallel: setting up the customer's OneDrive folder using the standard structure, and updating Zoho CRM with the account details. Both matter for the same reason — anyone on the team should be able to pick up this account and understand where things stand."

### Slide 7 — First 30-Day Success Plan
**Visual:** Calendar icon, 30-day arc.
**Voiceover:**
"Onboarding doesn't end at kickoff — the first 30 days set the trajectory for the whole relationship. That's why we build a First 30-Day Success Plan, tracking early wins and catching any risk signals before they become real problems."

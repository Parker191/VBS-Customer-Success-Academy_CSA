# 🌱 New Customer Playbook

**Document Type:** Playbook
**Category:** Customer Onboarding
**Owner:** Customer Success Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## When to Use This Playbook

Use this playbook from the moment a deal closes through the first 90 days of the customer relationship — the period that most strongly predicts long-term retention.

---

## Step 1 — Handover from Sales (Day 0–2)

- Complete the internal handover using the [Customer Handover SOP](../sops/customer-handover.md).
- Confirm: contract scope, commercial terms, key stakeholders, any commitments made during the sales process, and known risks.
- Create the customer record in CRM per the [CRM Management SOP](../sops/crm-management.md).

## Step 2 — Welcome & Kickoff Scheduling (Day 1–5)

- Send the Welcome Email (Email Templates / 01 - Welcome Email.md).
- Send the Kickoff Meeting Invitation (Email Templates / 02 - Kickoff Meeting Invitation.md).
- Confirm internal attendees (Delivery, CSM, any relevant SMEs) before scheduling.

## Step 3 — Kickoff Meeting (Week 1)

- Run the meeting using the [Kickoff Meeting Template](../meetings/01-kickoff-meeting.md).
- Confirm the customer's definition of success — capture this directly into a new [Customer Success Plan](../template-library/customer-success-plan-template.md).
- Set expectations on communication cadence, escalation path, and points of contact.

## Step 4 — First 30 Days

- Send Follow-up After Kickoff (Email Templates / 03 - Follow-up After Kickoff.md) within 48 hours of the kickoff.
- Establish weekly check-ins per the [Daily Planning SOP](../sops/daily-planning.md) cadence guidance.
- Confirm the customer has completed any required setup steps on their side; don't assume — verify.
- Run a baseline Health Scorecard at the end of Week 4, even if everything looks fine — this becomes your reference point.

## Step 5 — Days 30–60

- Confirm early value has been delivered and is visible to the customer, not just internally tracked.
- Identify the customer's champion and confirm they're engaged, not just the original buyer.
- Flag any early risk signals immediately using the [At-Risk Customer Playbook](at-risk-customer-playbook.md) — don't wait for Day 90.

## Step 6 — Days 60–90 (Onboarding Close-Out)

- Conduct a light onboarding review — confirm goals from Step 3 are on track.
- Transition cadence from onboarding-intensity check-ins to standard Business Review cadence per the [Business Reviews SOP](../sops/business-reviews.md).
- Update the Customer Success Plan to reflect steady-state goals for the next 90 days.

---

## Do

- Confirm sales commitments in writing during handover — don't inherit surprises.
- Get the customer talking about their goals in their own words during kickoff, not just VBS's delivery plan.
- Document the baseline health score even when things look healthy — you'll need it later for comparison.

## Don't

- Don't skip the handover step even for "simple" accounts — most early churn traces back to a handover gap.
- Don't let the first 30 days pass without a documented Success Plan.
- Don't wait until Day 90 to flag a risk that appeared on Day 20.

# 🔄 Renewal Customer Playbook

**Document Type:** Playbook
**Category:** Retention & Renewals
**Owner:** Customer Success Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## When to Use This Playbook

Use this playbook starting 120 days before every customer's renewal date, regardless of current health status. This playbook operationalizes the [Renewal Management SOP](../sops/renewal-management.md) into a step-by-step action guide.

---

## Step 1 — Assess Readiness (120 Days Out)

- Pull the current Health Scorecard and 90-day CRM history.
- Review the Customer Success Plan: were the customer's original goals met?
- Classify renewal confidence:

| Confidence | Signal |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Likely | Green health, goals met, strong stakeholder relationships |
| 🟡 At Risk | Yellow/Red health, unmet goals, or stakeholder turnover |
| 🔴 Uncertain | Active escalation, no clear champion, budget/priority concerns raised |

## Step 2 — Triage Before Proceeding (110–100 Days Out)

- If confidence is 🟡 or 🔴, activate the [At-Risk Customer Playbook](at-risk-customer-playbook.md) first. Do not open commercial renewal conversations with an unresolved risk in play.
- If 🟢, proceed directly to Step 3.

## Step 3 — Build the Value Case (90 Days Out)

- Compile concrete evidence of value delivered: outcomes achieved, usage trends, issues resolved, time saved.
- Avoid generic value statements — tie every point back to the customer's own stated goals from their Customer Success Plan.

## Step 4 — Confirm Commercial Terms Internally (75 Days Out)

- Align with your manager on renewal pricing, term length, and any flexibility before discussing terms with the customer.
- Never improvise commercial terms live in a customer conversation.

## Step 5 — Engage the Customer (60 Days Out)

- Send the Renewal Discussion email and schedule the [Renewal Planning Meeting](../meetings/07-renewal-planning-meeting.md).
- Present the value case before discussing commercial terms — lead with outcomes, not price.
- Listen for hesitation signals (budget concerns, competitor mentions, stakeholder change) and address them directly rather than pushing past them.

## Step 6 — Confirm & Close (30 Days Out)

- Get the renewal decision confirmed in writing.
- Update CRM with the new renewal date, terms, and any notes for the next cycle.
- If the customer is not renewing, document the reason honestly — this feeds directly into future retention improvements.

---

## Do

- Start the readiness assessment at 120 days, not 30 — early triage prevents last-minute surprises.
- Lead every renewal conversation with value delivered, in the customer's own language.
- Document non-renewals honestly; a clear reason is more useful than a vague one.

## Don't

- Don't open commercial discussions with an account that hasn't been triaged for risk first.
- Don't negotiate terms you haven't cleared with your manager.
- Don't treat renewal as guaranteed just because the account looks healthy — confirm it directly with the customer.

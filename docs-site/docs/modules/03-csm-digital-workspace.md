**The tool is only as useful as the process behind it.**

# Tool 1 - Microsoft Outlook

## Purpose

Primary communication platform with customers.

### Used For

- Customer emails
- Meeting invitations
- Calendar management
- Payment follow-ups
- Internal communication

### Best Practices

✅ Respond within agreed timelines.

✅ Use professional email signatures.

✅ Keep subject lines clear.

✅ Schedule reminders.

---

# Tool 2 - Microsoft Teams

## Purpose

Internal collaboration and online meetings.

### Used For

- Customer meetings
- Internal meetings
- Chat with colleagues
- Screen sharing
- File sharing

### Best Practices

- Join meetings early.
- Keep meeting notes.
- Share action items after every meeting.

---

# Tool 3 - Zoho CRM

## Purpose

The central system for customer information.

### Store

- Customer details
- Contacts
- Meeting notes
- Opportunities
- Follow-ups
- Health score
- Next actions

### Golden Rule

**Every customer interaction should be recorded in CRM.**

---

## Tool 4 - Microsoft OneDrive

### Purpose

OneDrive is VBS's centralized document storage platform used for storing, sharing, and collaborating on project and internal documents.

### Used For

- Project folders
- Customer documents
- SOPs
- Meeting Minutes
- Templates
- Training material
- Internal documentation

### Best Practices

✅ Keep folders organized.

✅ Use standard naming conventions.

✅ Share links instead of sending multiple file copies.

✅ Ensure documents are stored in the correct project folder.

✅ Keep the latest version of every document.

### Common Mistakes

❌ Saving files only on your local computer.

❌ Creating duplicate versions.

❌ Uploading documents to the wrong folder.

❌ Sharing incorrect permissions.

---

# Tool 5 - Internal Tracker

## URL

https://tracker.virtualbuildingstudio.com/dashboard

## Purpose

The Internal Tracker is VBS's operational dashboard used by Customer Success Managers to monitor customer activities, projects, invoices, meetings, and business performance.

### Used For

- Customer Dashboard
- Active Projects
- Revenue Tracking
- Invoice Monitoring
- Customer Health
- Follow-up Activities
- Meeting Tracking
- KPI Monitoring

### Best Practices

✅ Review the dashboard every morning.

✅ Monitor overdue customer actions.

✅ Keep customer activities updated.

✅ Coordinate with Delivery and Finance when required.

### Common Mistakes

❌ Ignoring overdue actions.

❌ Working from outdated information.

❌ Not reviewing dashboard alerts.

---

# Tool 6 - QuickBooks

## Purpose

Invoice reference and payment tracking.

### Customer Success Uses

- Check invoice status
- Verify payment
- Share invoice copies
- Coordinate with Finance

Customer Success should not modify accounting records unless authorized.

---

# Tool 7 - GitHub

## Purpose

Knowledge Management System.

### Used For

- SOP Library
- Email Templates
- CRM Guides
- Meeting Templates
- Training Videos
- Best Practices

GitHub serves as the Customer Success Academy.

# Best Practices

✅ Keep all systems updated.

✅ Avoid storing customer information in personal files.

✅ Follow naming conventions.

✅ Use the latest document versions.

✅ Protect confidential information.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Forgetting CRM updates.

❌ Saving files locally instead of SharePoint.

❌ Missing calendar invitations.

❌ Duplicate reports.

❌ Not documenting customer meetings.

# 💰 Invoice & Payment Management

Welcome to the Invoice & Payment Management module.

This lesson explains how to monitor invoices and follow up professionally with customers.

---

# 🎯 Objective

By the end of this lesson you will know how to:

✅ Review pending invoices

✅ Send payment reminders

✅ Coordinate with Accounts

✅ Confirm payments

✅ Update CRM

---

# 📋 Step 1 - Review Pending Invoices

Collect information from the Accounts team.

Review:

• Invoice Number

• Invoice Date

• Due Date

• Outstanding Amount

---

# 📋 Step 2 - Verify Customer Details

Confirm:

• Correct Contact Person

• Email Address

• Purchase Order (if applicable)

---

# 📋 Step 3 - Send Friendly Reminder

Include:

• Invoice Number

• Outstanding Amount

• Due Date

• Payment Instructions

Maintain a polite and professional tone.

---

# 📋 Step 4 - Follow Up

If there is no response after a few business days:

• Send another reminder

OR

• Schedule a phone call

---

# 📋 Step 5 - Coordinate with Accounts

Confirm:

• Payment Received

• Partial Payment

• Any Disputes

Resolve customer questions promptly.

---

# 📋 Step 6 - Update CRM

Record:

• Payment Status

• Payment Date

• Follow-up Notes

• Next Action

---

# 📋 Step 7 - Send Thank You Email

After payment is received:

Thank the customer.

Confirm the payment has been recorded.

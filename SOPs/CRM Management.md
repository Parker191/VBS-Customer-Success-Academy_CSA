# 📊 CRM Management

Welcome to the CRM Management module.

This lesson explains how to maintain accurate customer information in Zoho CRM.

---

# 🎯 Objective

By the end of this lesson you will know how to:

✅ Update customer records

✅ Log meetings

✅ Create follow-ups

✅ Update customer health

✅ Maintain opportunity details

---

# 📋 Step 1 - Log in to Zoho CRM

Open:

Zoho CRM

Navigate to:

Customers

---

# 📋 Step 2 - Search for the Customer

Search using:

• Customer Name

• Company Name

• CRM ID

Open the customer record.

---

# 📋 Step 3 - Update Customer Information

Verify:

• Contact Details

• Active Projects

• Delivery Manager

• Account Owner

---

# 📋 Step 4 - Log Customer Interaction

Record:

• Calls

• Emails

• Meetings

• Discussions

• Customer Feedback

---

# 📋 Step 5 - Update Customer Health

Choose:

🟢 Green

🟡 Amber

🔴 Red

Add comments explaining the health status.

---

# 📋 Step 6 - Create Next Follow-up

Add:

• Follow-up Date

• Task Owner

• Reminder

---

# 📋 Step 7 - Save Changes

Verify all information.

Click Save.

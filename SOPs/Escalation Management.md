# 📌 Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to define a structured approach for identifying, managing, resolving, and preventing customer escalations.

This process ensures:

- Customer concerns are handled quickly.
- Internal teams have clear ownership.
- Communication remains professional.
- Root causes are identified.
- Future issues are prevented.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Identify escalation risks early.
- Classify issue severity.
- Coordinate with internal teams.
- Maintain customer trust.
- Track resolution progress.
- Document learnings.

---

# 👥 Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Customer Success Managers.
- Account Managers.
- Delivery Teams.
- Project Managers.
- Finance Teams.
- Leadership Team (when required).

---

# 🏢 When To Use This SOP

Use this SOP when:

- Customer expresses dissatisfaction.
- Delivery issues impact customer experience.
- Customer threatens cancellation.
- Payment issues create business risk.
- Communication breakdown occurs.
- Quality concerns are raised.
- Project timelines are missed.

# 🚦 Escalation Severity Levels

## 🟢 Level 1 — Low Severity

### Definition

Minor issue with limited customer impact.

### Examples:

- Small clarification request.
- Minor communication gap.
- General feedback.

### Response Timeline:

Within 2 business days.

### Owner:

Customer Success Manager

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Medium Severity

### Definition

Issue impacting customer satisfaction or delivery experience.

### Examples:

- Delivery delay.
- Repeated follow-up required.
- Quality concerns.

### Response Timeline:

Within 1 business day.

### Owner:

CSM + Relevant Internal Team

---

## 🔴 Level 3 — High Severity

### Definition

Critical issue impacting customer relationship or revenue.

### Examples:

- Customer considering cancellation.
- Major delivery failure.
- Significant quality issue.
- Contract risk.

### Response Timeline:

Immediate action required.

### Owner:

CSM + Leadership + Relevant Teams

# 🔄 Escalation Management Process

## Step 1 — Identify The Issue

CSM should capture:

- Customer concern.
- Date identified.
- Customer impact.
- Business impact.
- Related project/account.

---

## Step 2 — Acknowledge Customer

The CSM should:

- Respond quickly.
- Acknowledge the concern.
- Show ownership.
- Avoid blaming internal teams.

Example:

> "Thank you for sharing your concern. We understand the impact this has created and are reviewing this internally. We will share an update by [date]."

---

## Step 3 — Investigate Internally

Collect information from:

- Delivery Team.
- Project Manager.
- Finance Team.
- Sales/KAM Team.

Identify:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- Who owns resolution?
- Expected timeline?

---

## Step 4 — Create Recovery Plan

Recovery plan should include:

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Identify root cause | CSM + Team | 24 hours |
| Define solution | Responsible Team | 48 hours |
| Customer update | CSM | Agreed timeline |
| Closure confirmation | CSM | After resolution |

---

## Step 5 — Customer Follow-up

After resolution:

- Confirm customer satisfaction.
- Document feedback.
- Update CRM.
- Close escalation.

# 🔍 Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

After every major escalation, perform a Root Cause Analysis to identify why the issue occurred and how to prevent it in the future.

---

## RCA Questions

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- Who was impacted?
- Why did it happen?
- Could it have been prevented?
- What changes are required?

# 🛠️ Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA)

After identifying the root cause, define actions to resolve the issue and prevent it from happening again.

| Action Type | Purpose | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Corrective Action | Fix the current issue | Rework BIM model |
| Preventive Action | Prevent recurrence | Add an additional QA review before delivery |

---

## CAPA Checklist

- [ ] Root cause confirmed.
- [ ] Corrective action completed.
- [ ] Preventive action implemented.
- [ ] Customer informed.
- [ ] Internal documentation updated.

# 🏢 VBS Example Scenario

## Situation

A customer reports that a BIM model delivery was delayed by three days, impacting their internal review schedule.

### CSM Actions

1. Acknowledge the concern within the agreed response timeline.
2. Review the delivery status with the Delivery Manager.
3. Identify the reason for the delay.
4. Share a revised delivery timeline with the customer.
5. Coordinate corrective actions with the project team.
6. Complete the RCA and CAPA.
7. Confirm customer satisfaction after delivery.
8. Update the CRM and close the escalation.

---

## Expected Outcome

- Customer receives timely communication.
- Delivery issue is resolved.
- Future delays are reduced through preventive actions.
- Customer confidence is maintained.

---

# 📌 Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to define a structured approach for identifying, managing, resolving, and preventing customer escalations.

This process ensures:

- Customer concerns are handled quickly.
- Internal teams have clear ownership.
- Communication remains professional.
- Root causes are identified.
- Future issues are prevented.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP helps Customer Success Managers:

- Identify escalation risks early.
- Classify issue severity.
- Coordinate with internal teams.
- Maintain customer trust.
- Track resolution progress.
- Document learnings.

---

# 👥 Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Customer Success Managers.
- Account Managers.
- Delivery Teams.
- Project Managers.
- Finance Teams.
- Leadership Team (when required).

---

# 🏢 When To Use This SOP

Use this SOP when:

- Customer expresses dissatisfaction.
- Delivery issues impact customer experience.
- Customer threatens cancellation.
- Payment issues create business risk.
- Communication breakdown occurs.
- Quality concerns are raised.
- Project timelines are missed.

# 📂 Escalation Categories

Understanding the type of escalation helps the CSM assign the right owner and resolution approach.

---

## 🏗️ Delivery Escalation

### Examples:

- Project delivery delays.
- Missed milestones.
- Resource availability issues.
- Incorrect deliverables.

### Primary Owner:

Delivery Manager

### CSM Responsibility:

- Customer communication.
- Tracking resolution.
- Providing updates.

### Examples:

- BIM model quality concerns.
- Multiple correction cycles.
- Deliverables not meeting expectations.
- Customer rejection.

### Primary Owner:

QA Team / Delivery Lead

### CSM Responsibility:

- Capture customer feedback.
- Track improvement actions.

---

## 💬 Communication Escalation

### Examples:

- Delayed responses.
- Lack of project updates.
- Customer feels ignored.
- Misalignment in expectations.

### Primary Owner:

Customer Success Manager

---

## 💰 Financial Escalation

### Examples:

- Invoice disputes.
- Payment delays.
- Billing concerns.

### Primary Owner:

Finance Team + CSM

---

## 🤝 Relationship Escalation

### Examples:

- Customer dissatisfaction.
- Churn risk.
- Cancellation discussion.
- Loss of customer confidence.

### Primary Owner:

CSM + Leadership Team

# 🚦 Escalation Severity Levels

## 🟢 Level 1 — Low Severity

### Definition

Minor issue with limited customer impact.

### Examples:

- Small clarification request.
- Minor communication gap.
- General feedback.

### Response Timeline:

Within 2 business days.

### Owner:

Customer Success Manager

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Medium Severity

### Definition

Issue impacting customer satisfaction or delivery experience.

### Examples:

- Delivery delay.
- Repeated follow-up required.
- Quality concerns.

### Response Timeline:

Within 1 business day.

### Owner:

CSM + Relevant Internal Team

---

## 🔴 Level 3 — High Severity

### Definition

Critical issue impacting customer relationship or revenue.

### Examples:

- Customer considering cancellation.
- Major delivery failure.
- Significant quality issue.
- Contract risk.

### Response Timeline:

Immediate action required.

### Owner:

CSM + Leadership + Relevant Teams

# 🔄 Escalation Management Process

## Step 1 — Identify The Issue

CSM should capture:

- Customer concern.
- Date identified.
- Customer impact.
- Business impact.
- Related project/account.

---

## Step 2 — Acknowledge Customer

The CSM should:

- Respond quickly.
- Acknowledge the concern.
- Show ownership.
- Avoid blaming internal teams.

Example:

> "Thank you for sharing your concern. We understand the impact this has created and are reviewing this internally. We will share an update by [date]."

---

## Step 3 — Investigate Internally

Collect information from:

- Delivery Team.
- Project Manager.
- Finance Team.
- Sales/KAM Team.

Identify:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- Who owns resolution?
- Expected timeline?

---

## Step 4 — Create Recovery Plan

Recovery plan should include:

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Identify root cause | CSM + Team | 24 hours |
| Define solution | Responsible Team | 48 hours |
| Customer update | CSM | Agreed timeline |
| Closure confirmation | CSM | After resolution |

---

## Step 5 — Customer Follow-up

After resolution:

- Confirm customer satisfaction.
- Document feedback.
- Update CRM.
- Close escalation.

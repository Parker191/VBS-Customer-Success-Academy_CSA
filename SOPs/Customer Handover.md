# 📌 Purpose

This SOP defines the standardized process for transferring a customer from the Sales or Key Account Management (KAM) team to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).

A structured handover ensures:

- Complete customer information is transferred.
- Customer expectations are aligned.
- Internal teams understand project requirements.
- Customer onboarding begins smoothly.
- Risks are identified early.

---

# 🎯 Objective

This SOP enables the Customer Success team to:

- Receive complete customer information.
- Build customer confidence from Day 1.
- Reduce onboarding delays.
- Improve collaboration between Sales, KAM, Delivery, and Customer Success.
- Maintain a seamless customer experience.

---

# 👥 Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Sales Team
- Key Account Managers (KAM)
- Customer Success Managers
- Delivery Managers
- Project Managers

---

# 🏢 When To Use This SOP

Use this SOP whenever:

- A new customer signs a contract.
- A new project begins.
- An existing customer is assigned to a new CSM.
- Ownership of an account changes.

---

# 🔄 Customer Handover Process

## Step 1 — Contract Confirmation

Sales/KAM confirms:

- Signed agreement received.
- Scope finalized.
- Commercial terms approved.
- Customer contact details verified.

---

## Step 2 — Internal Handover Meeting

Participants:

- Sales/KAM
- Customer Success Manager
- Delivery Manager (if applicable)

Agenda:

- Customer background
- Services purchased
- Business goals
- Key stakeholders
- Risks or commitments
- Special requirements

---

## Step 3 — Documentation Review

The CSM should receive:

- Proposal/Quotation
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- Contract (if applicable)
- Customer requirements
- Project timeline
- Commercial information
- Key contacts

---

## Step 4 — CRM Verification

Ensure the CRM includes:

- Customer name
- Company details
- Primary contacts
- Services purchased
- Project status
- Start date
- Renewal date
- Notes from Sales

---

## Step 5 — Customer Introduction

The CSM should:

- Send a welcome email.
- Introduce themselves.
- Share communication channels.
- Schedule the kickoff meeting.

# 🚀 Customer Kickoff Readiness Checklist

Before the kickoff meeting, ensure:

- [ ] Welcome email sent
- [ ] Kickoff meeting scheduled
- [ ] Agenda shared
- [ ] Customer goals documented
- [ ] Project team introduced
- [ ] Communication plan agreed
- [ ] Escalation contacts shared
- [ ] Next milestones defined

---

## Kickoff Deliverables

At the end of the kickoff meeting, the customer should know:

- Project scope
- Timeline
- Team members
- Communication process
- Meeting cadence
- Escalation path
- Next immediate actions

---

# ⚠️ Early Risk Indicators

Watch for these during handover:

- Missing customer information
- Incomplete scope
- Unrealistic timelines
- Unclear customer expectations
- No executive sponsor identified
- Missing internal owner
- Contract ambiguity
- Poor Sales-to-CSM knowledge transfer

---

## Recommended Action

If any critical information is missing:

1. Pause onboarding if necessary.
2. Clarify with Sales/KAM.
3. Update CRM.
4. Inform Delivery before project execution.

---

# 🏢 VBS Example

## Scenario

A new architecture firm signs a Dedicated Resource Model (DRM) agreement for BIM modeling services.

### Handover Process

1. Sales closes the opportunity.
2. KAM shares the commercial agreement.
3. CSM reviews the scope and customer goals.
4. Delivery Manager confirms resource availability.
5. CSM schedules the kickoff meeting.
6. Customer receives the welcome email.
7. CRM is updated.
8. Onboarding officially begins.

---

## Expected Outcome

- Smooth transition.
- No loss of information.
- Clear customer expectations.
- Faster onboarding.

# ❤️ Client Engagement

Welcome to the Client Engagement training module.

This lesson teaches a Customer Success Manager how to maintain strong relationships with customers.

---

# 🎯 Objective

After completing this lesson you will be able to:

- Conduct regular client check-ins
- Build customer trust
- Capture customer feedback
- Update CRM
- Identify new opportunities

---

# 📋 Step 1 - Prepare Before the Meeting

## Why?

Preparation helps you have a meaningful conversation.

## What to do?

Review:

- Active Projects
- Previous Meeting Notes
- Pending Action Items
- Customer Health
- Outstanding Invoices

✅ Once completed, continue to Step 2.

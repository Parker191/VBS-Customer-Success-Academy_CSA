# ❤️ Client Engagement

Welcome to the Client Engagement module.

This lesson explains how a Customer Success Manager should maintain strong relationships with customers throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# 🎯 Objective

By the end of this lesson you will know how to:

✅ Conduct regular client check-ins

✅ Share project updates

✅ Build customer trust

✅ Identify new opportunities

✅ Record customer feedback

---

# 📋 Step 1 - Review Customer Information

## Why?

Before speaking with the customer, understand the current project status.

## What to do?

Review:

• Active Projects

• Previous Meeting Notes

• Pending Action Items

• Customer Health

• Outstanding Invoices (if any)

✅ Done?

Go to Step 2.

---

# 📋 Step 2 - Schedule a Check-in Meeting

## Why?

Regular communication builds trust.

## What to do?

Schedule a Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly meeting.

Include:

• Project Updates

• Risks

• Upcoming Deliverables

• Customer Questions

---

# 📋 Step 3 - Conduct the Meeting

## Discuss

• Progress since the last meeting

• Completed work

• Upcoming milestones

• Risks or blockers

• Customer feedback

---

# 📋 Step 4 - Capture Action Items

Record:

• Task

• Owner

• Due Date

• Priority

---

# 📋 Step 5 - Update Zoho CRM

Update:

• Meeting Notes

• Next Follow-up Date

• Customer Sentiment

• Health Score

• Opportunities

---

# 📋 Step 6 - Send Meeting Minutes

Within 24 hours send:

• Meeting Summary

• Action Items

• Timeline

• Next Meeting Date

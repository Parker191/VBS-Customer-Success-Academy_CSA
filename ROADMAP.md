# 🗺️ VBS Customer Success Academy — Roadmap

> A practical roadmap for building, improving, and scaling the VBS Customer Success Academy.

---

# 🎯 Roadmap Purpose

The VBS Customer Success Academy is designed to continuously evolve from a learning repository into a structured **Customer Success enablement and operating system**.

The roadmap focuses on:

- Customer Success knowledge
- Standardized processes
- Practical CSM tools
- AI-assisted workflows
- Reporting and governance
- Continuous improvement
- CSM learning and certification

**📊 Academy Maturity Model
**
The Academy can progressively mature through five levels:

| Level | Stage        | Description                                                        |
| ----- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | Foundation   | Core knowledge and structure                                       |
| 2     | Standardized | SOPs, templates, and playbooks                                     |
| 3     | Enabled      | CSM tools and practical workflows                                  |
| 4     | AI-Assisted  | AI-supported analysis and productivity                             |
| 5     | Scaled       | Measurement, certification, governance, and continuous improvement |

**Current Direction
**

**The Academy is progressing from Level 3 toward Level 4.
**
The focus is now on connecting the existing knowledge and tools into a more integrated Customer Success operating system.

**🔐 Governance Principles
**
The Academy should follow these principles:

**Accuracy**

Do not publish unverified customer or business information.

**Ownership**

Important processes should have a clearly identified owner.

**Version Control
**
Major process changes should be documented.

**Security**

Confidential customer information must not be stored inappropriately.

**AI Governance
**
AI output must be reviewed by a CSM before being used for customer decisions or communication.

**Continuous Review
**
Processes should be reviewed and updated when business requirements change.

**🤖 AI Governance Principle
**
**AI assists — the CSM decides.
**

AI should help with:

Preparation
Analysis
Summarization
Drafting
Organization
Pattern identification

AI should not independently:

Make customer commitments
Approve commercial terms
Invent customer information
Determine customer sentiment without evidence
Replace CSM judgment
Make unauthorized decisions

# 📧 Follow-up After Kickoff Meeting

## Purpose

Summarize the kickoff meeting, confirm agreed actions, and establish the next steps.

---

## When to Use

Send within 24 hours after the kickoff meeting.

---

## Email Template

# 💡 VBS Best Practice

Always send the kickoff follow-up within **24 hours**.

Benefits:

- Prevents misunderstandings.
- Documents agreed actions.
- Builds customer confidence.
- Creates accountability.
- Provides a written record for future reference.

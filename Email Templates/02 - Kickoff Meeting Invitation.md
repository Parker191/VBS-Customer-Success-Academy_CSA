# 📅 Kickoff Meeting Invitation

## Purpose

Invite the customer to the official project kickoff meeting and set clear expectations for the partnership.

---

## When to Use

Send after the customer has acknowledged the welcome email or within 1–2 business days of the customer handover.

---

## Email Template

**Subject:** Kickoff Meeting Invitation – [Project Name]

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

As the next step in our partnership, I'd like to invite you to our **Project Kickoff Meeting**.

The purpose of this meeting is to ensure everyone is aligned before project execution begins.

### Meeting Details

**Date:** [Date]

**Time:** [Time]

**Duration:** 30–45 Minutes

**Platform:** Microsoft Teams / Zoom

**Meeting Link:** [Insert Link]

---

### Agenda

- Team introductions
- Project overview
- Scope confirmation
- Timeline & milestones
- Communication plan
- Roles & responsibilities
- Questions & discussion
- Next steps

---

If the proposed time doesn't work for you, please let me know a suitable alternative, and we'll be happy to accommodate.

Looking forward to meeting you and your team.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

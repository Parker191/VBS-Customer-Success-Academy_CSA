> **Category:** Customer Communication
>
> **Owner:** Customer Success Team
>
> **Version:** 1.0
>
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

Proactively understand customer satisfaction, identify risks, gather feedback, and ensure the customer is achieving their desired outcomes.

---

## When to Use

- Monthly customer health review
- Before a Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
- After major project milestones
- When customer engagement decreases
- Before renewal discussions

  ## Email Template

**Subject:** Checking In – How Are Things Going?

Hi **[Customer Name]**,

I hope you're doing well.

I wanted to take a moment to check in and understand how things are going from your perspective.

Your feedback is important to us, and we want to ensure we're supporting your team effectively.

### We'd Love Your Feedback

- Are our deliverables meeting your expectations?
- Is communication with our team working well?
- Are there any challenges or concerns we should be aware of?
- Is there anything we can improve to better support your goals?

If you'd prefer, I'd be happy to schedule a short call to discuss your experience in more detail.

Thank you for your continued partnership. We appreciate your trust in Virtual Building Studio and look forward to supporting your ongoing success.

Kind regards,

**[Your Name]**  
Customer Success Manager  
Virtual Building Studio

## Customer Health Review Checklist

- [ ] Customer Health Score reviewed
- [ ] Recent project status checked
- [ ] Outstanding action items reviewed
- [ ] Invoice/payment status verified
- [ ] Previous feedback reviewed
- [ ] CRM updated after sending

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practice

A Customer Health Check is not a satisfaction survey.

It is a proactive conversation to:

- Strengthen relationships.
- Identify risks early.
- Discover growth opportunities.
- Demonstrate partnership.
- Build long-term customer trust.

Whenever possible:

- Personalize the email.
- Reference recent projects or milestones.
- End with a clear invitation to connect.

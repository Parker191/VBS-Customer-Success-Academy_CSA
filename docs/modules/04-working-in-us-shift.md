# 🎓 VBS Customer Success Academy

# 🌎 Module 04 -  Why Customer Success Works in the US Shift

Virtual Building Studio (VBS) primarily serves customers across the United States.

To provide fast responses, attend customer meetings, and collaborate during customer business hours, Customer Success Managers generally work the US shift.

This schedule allows CSMs to:

- Respond to customer emails promptly.
- Attend customer meetings during their business hours.
- Coordinate project updates with customers.
- Resolve issues faster.
- Build stronger customer relationships through real-time communication.

Our goal is simple:

> **Be available when our customers need us.**
>
# 🕒 Standard Working Hours

The standard Customer Success shift at VBS is:

**🕕 6:00 PM IST to 🕓 4:00 AM IST**

These timings may change based on:

- Customer location
- Project requirements
- Business needs
- Special meetings
- Daylight Saving Time (DST) in the United States

Always check your Outlook calendar and team announcements for any schedule changes.

💡 Did You Know?
Most VBS customers are based in the United States. By working during their business hours, Customer Success Managers can respond faster, join meetings without delays, and provide a better overall customer experience.

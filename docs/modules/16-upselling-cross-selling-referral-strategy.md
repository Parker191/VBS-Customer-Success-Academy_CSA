# 👋 Welcome

Welcome to **Module 16 – Upselling, Cross-selling & Referral Strategy**.

Customer Success Managers play an important role in helping customers grow.

As customers achieve success with VBS, new opportunities often emerge. These opportunities may involve expanding existing services, introducing complementary solutions, or receiving referrals to new customers.

The goal is never to sell aggressively.

Instead, Customer Success Managers focus on understanding customer needs, identifying opportunities, and connecting customers with the right VBS solutions at the right time.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Explain upselling, cross-selling, and referrals.
- Identify customer growth opportunities.
- Recognize signals that indicate expansion potential.
- Conduct value-focused customer conversations.
- Collaborate effectively with Sales and Account Management.
- Document growth opportunities in CRM.
- Use AI to prepare opportunity summaries and customer insights.

# 🌍 Business Context

Customer Success is not only about helping customers solve problems—it is also about helping them achieve greater business outcomes.

As customers experience success with VBS, they often discover new challenges, larger projects, or opportunities where additional services can provide value.

A Customer Success Manager should recognize these opportunities and collaborate with Sales or Account Management to recommend the right solutions.

Growth should always be based on customer success, business value, and genuine customer needs.

---

# 💼 Why Growth Matters

Helping existing customers grow benefits everyone.

For Customers:

- Receive additional value.
- Solve new business challenges.
- Work with a trusted partner.
- Reduce onboarding time with new vendors.

For VBS:

- Increase customer lifetime value.
- Strengthen long-term partnerships.
- Generate sustainable revenue.
- Increase customer loyalty.
- Create referral opportunities.

---

# 🌟 Customer Success Principle

> **"Recommend solutions because they create value—not because they create revenue."**

Long-term trust always comes before short-term sales.

---

---

# ⬆️ What is Upselling?

Upselling means helping an existing customer expand or enhance the services they already use because it supports their business goals.

The recommendation should always be based on customer needs rather than sales targets.

## VBS Examples

| Current Service | Upsell Opportunity |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| BIM Modeling | Higher Level of Development (LOD) |
| Dedicated Resource Model | Additional dedicated resources |
| Ongoing project support | Extended engagement period |
| BIM Coordination | Advanced coordination support |

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**

A customer currently uses one dedicated BIM modeler.

As project volume increases, the Customer Success Manager identifies the need for an additional dedicated resource and coordinates with the Account Manager to discuss the opportunity.

---

---

# 🔄 What is Cross-selling?

Cross-selling means introducing complementary VBS services that help the customer achieve better project outcomes.

The focus should always remain on solving customer challenges.

## VBS Examples

| Existing Service | Cross-sell Opportunity |
|------------------|------------------------|
| CAD Drafting | CAD-to-BIM Conversion |
| BIM Modeling | Scan-to-BIM |
| Scan-to-BIM | BIM Coordination |
| Project-Based Support | Dedicated Resource Model (DRM) |

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**

A customer working with VBS for CAD drafting begins renovating an existing building.

The Customer Success Manager identifies that Scan-to-BIM could support the project and introduces the opportunity to the Sales or Account Management team.

# 🤝 Referral Strategy

Satisfied customers are often willing to recommend trusted partners to colleagues, consultants, or other organizations.

Referrals should never be requested too early.

Instead, they naturally result from consistently delivering excellent customer experiences.

## Good Opportunities to Request a Referral

- Successful project completion.
- Positive customer feedback.
- Long-term customer relationships.
- Successful renewal.
- Customer expresses high satisfaction.

---

## Referral Best Practices

- Thank the customer first.
- Ask politely and professionally.
- Make it easy for the customer to refer others.
- Never pressure the customer.
- Record referral opportunities in CRM.

---

> 💡 **Remember**

Every satisfied customer has the potential to become a brand ambassador for VBS.

# 🎯 Opportunity Identification

Growth opportunities are discovered by understanding the customer's business, not by pitching services.

Customer Success Managers should actively listen during meetings, ask thoughtful questions, and identify where VBS can provide additional value.

## Where Opportunities Come From

- New customer projects
- Increased workload or project volume
- Business expansion
- New office locations
- Additional stakeholders joining the account
- Requests outside the current scope
- Positive feedback about existing services

---

## Opportunity Discovery Questions

Consider asking:

- What projects are coming up next?
- Are there any new business goals this quarter?
- What challenges are you expecting?
- Are there areas where additional support would help?
- Has your team or workload changed recently?

---

> 💡 **Best Practice**

Focus on understanding first. Recommendations should come naturally after you've identified a genuine customer need.

# 📈 Growth Signals

Certain customer behaviors can indicate that an account is ready for expansion.

Recognizing these signals helps Customer Success Managers start the right conversations at the right time.

## Positive Growth Signals

| Signal | What It May Indicate |
|--------|-----------------------|
| More project requests | Increased business activity |
| Positive feedback | High customer satisfaction |
| Additional stakeholders involved | Larger organizational engagement |
| Questions about other services | Interest in expanding |
| Increased meeting frequency | Greater collaboration |
| Successful project delivery | Trust in VBS capabilities |
| Long-term engagement | Strong partnership potential |

---

## When to Involve Sales or Account Management

Involve the appropriate team when:

- A commercial discussion is required.
- Pricing or contracts need to be discussed.
- The customer requests a proposal.
- The opportunity involves a new service agreement.
- A significant expansion opportunity is identified.

---

> 🏢 **VBS in Practice**

A customer mentions they have three upcoming commercial projects that require BIM support.

The CSM documents the opportunity in CRM and coordinates with the Account Manager to prepare a proposal.

# 💬 Customer Conversation Framework

Growth conversations should feel like business discussions, not sales presentations.

A simple framework helps keep conversations customer-focused.

## The L.I.S.T.E.N. Framework

| Step | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **L** | Listen to the customer's goals and challenges |
| **I** | Identify opportunities where VBS can help |
| **S** | Suggest relevant solutions based on customer needs |
| **T** | Transition the discussion to the appropriate internal team when needed |
| **E** | Ensure the customer understands the potential value |
| **N** | Note the opportunity in Zoho CRM and follow up |

---

## Example Conversation

**Customer:**

> "We're planning to expand into healthcare projects next quarter."

**Customer Success Manager:**

> "That's exciting. Could you tell me more about the type of BIM support you'll need? Once I understand your requirements, I'll coordinate with our Account Management team to explore the best solution."

---

> 💡 **Remember**

Great Customer Success conversations are based on curiosity, not persuasion.

# ⭐ Best Practices

Successful Customer Success Managers:

- Focus on customer outcomes before discussing services.
- Listen more than you speak.
- Ask thoughtful discovery questions.
- Recommend only relevant solutions.
- Collaborate closely with Sales and Account Management.
- Record opportunities in Zoho CRM.
- Follow up consistently after discussions.
- Celebrate customer successes.

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes:

- Recommending services without understanding customer needs.
- Acting like a salesperson.
- Discussing pricing before understanding requirements.
- Ignoring smaller growth opportunities.
- Forgetting to document opportunities.
- Overpromising results.
- Asking for referrals before delivering value.

---

> ✅ **Key Takeaway**

Growth is the result of trust, value, and strong customer relationships—not aggressive selling.

# 🏢 Real VBS Scenario

A customer has successfully completed two BIM modeling projects with VBS.

During a Quarterly Business Review, the customer mentions that they are planning several renovation projects involving existing buildings.

The Customer Success Manager:

1. Asks discovery questions to understand the upcoming projects.
2. Identifies that **Scan-to-BIM** services may support the customer's goals.
3. Documents the opportunity in Zoho CRM.
4. Coordinates with the Account Manager.
5. Ensures the customer receives a tailored proposal.

The customer expands their engagement with VBS, strengthening the long-term partnership.

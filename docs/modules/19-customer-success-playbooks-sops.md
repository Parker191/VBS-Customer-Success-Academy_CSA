As Customer Success teams grow, relying only on individual experience becomes difficult.

# 📚 What are Customer Success Playbooks?

A Customer Success Playbook is a structured guide that explains how a CSM should handle specific customer situations throughout the customer lifecycle.

Playbooks provide:

- Recommended actions.
- Best practices.
- Communication guidelines.
- Decision-making frameworks.
- Templates and resources.

---

# Why Playbooks Matter

Playbooks help Customer Success teams:

- Deliver consistent customer experiences.
- Reduce dependency on individual knowledge.
- Train new CSMs faster.
- Improve team collaboration.
- Scale customer operations.

---

# Examples of Customer Success Playbooks

| Playbook | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Onboarding Playbook | Guide new customer implementation |
| Engagement Playbook | Maintain customer relationships |
| Renewal Playbook | Prepare for renewals |
| Escalation Playbook | Handle customer issues |
| Expansion Playbook | Identify growth opportunities |

---

> 💡 **Customer Success Principle**

A playbook tells you **what approach to follow and why it matters.**

---

# 📄 What are SOPs?

SOP stands for **Standard Operating Procedure**.

An SOP is a detailed step-by-step instruction document that explains how to complete a specific task.

SOPs remove confusion by creating a standard way of working.

---

# Why SOPs Matter

SOPs help teams:

- Reduce mistakes.
- Improve consistency.
- Speed up execution.
- Simplify training.
- Maintain quality standards.

---

# Examples of Customer Success SOPs

| SOP | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| Customer Onboarding SOP | Steps after customer kickoff |
| CRM Update SOP | Maintain accurate records |
| Invoice Follow-up SOP | Manage payment coordination |
| Escalation SOP | Resolve customer issues |
| Health Score SOP | Review account health |

---


---

> 💡 **Remember**

# 🔄 SOP vs Playbook

Although SOPs and Playbooks are related, they serve different purposes.

| Area | Playbook | SOP |
|------|----------|-----|
| Purpose | Guide strategy and approach | Explain exact steps |
| Focus | What and why | How |
| Usage | Situations and decisions | Repeatable tasks |
| Flexibility | More adaptable | More structured |
| Example | Renewal Strategy | Renewal Process Steps |

---

# Example

## Playbook:

"How to manage a customer renewal."

Includes:

- Renewal strategy.
- Customer conversations.
- Risk management.
- Stakeholder alignment.

---

## SOP:

"How to complete renewal preparation."

Includes:

1. Review contract.
2. Check customer health.
3. Prepare renewal summary.
4. Update CRM.
5. Schedule customer discussion.

---

> 🌟 Best Practice

Great Customer Success teams use both:

**Playbooks provide direction.  
SOPs provide execution.**

| 🟡 Medium | Customer impact | Delivery delay |

---

# Escalation Management Process

## Step 1 — Understand the Issue

Collect:

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- Who is impacted?
- What is the business impact?

---

## Step 2 — Align Internally

Coordinate with:

- Delivery Team.
- Project Manager.
- Sales/KAM Team.
- Leadership (if required).

---

## Step 3 — Communicate With Customer

Communication should include:

- Acknowledgment.
- Current understanding.
- Action plan.
- Expected timeline.
- Next update date.

---

# Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

After resolution, identify:

## What happened?

Describe the issue.

## Why did it happen?

Identify the root cause.

## How will we prevent it?

Define corrective actions.

---

# CAPA Framework

**Corrective Action:**

Fix the current problem.

Example:
- Rework delivered files.

**Preventive Action:**

Prevent future occurrence.

Example:
- Improve quality review checklist.

---

# 🏢 VBS Example

Customer reports delayed BIM model delivery.

CSM action:

1. Acknowledge customer concern.
2. Understand delivery impact.
3. Coordinate with Delivery Manager.
4. Share recovery plan.
5. Track completion.
6. Document RCA and preventive actions.

---

> 💡 **Key Principle**

A successful escalation is not only about solving today's problem—it is about improving tomorrow's experience.

# 📄 SOP Framework

A well-written SOP should allow any trained team member to understand and complete a process correctly.

---

# SOP Template

## 1. SOP Name

Clearly define the process.

Example:

Customer Invoice Follow-up SOP

---

## 2. Purpose

Explain why this SOP exists.

Example:

Ensure timely payment follow-ups and accurate finance coordination.

---

## 3. Scope

Define when this SOP applies.

Example:

Applicable to all active customer accounts.

---

## 4. Ownership

Define responsibility.

Example:

Owner: Customer Success Manager

Supporting Teams:
- Finance
- Sales
- Delivery

---

## 5. Process Steps

Document every step.

Example:

1. Review outstanding invoices.
2. Confirm invoice details.
3. Contact customer.
4. Update CRM.
5. Follow up based on timeline.

---

## 6. Tools Required

Examples:

- CRM
- Email
- Customer Tracker
- Reporting Dashboard

---

## 7. Success Criteria

Define completion.

Example:

Invoice status updated and payment confirmation received.

---

# SOP Quality Checklist

- [ ] Clear objective.
- [ ] Defined owner.
- [ ] Step-by-step process.
- [ ] Required tools listed.
- [ ] Success measurement included.
- [ ] Reviewed regularly.

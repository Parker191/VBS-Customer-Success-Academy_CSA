# 📌 Purpose

Review a customer escalation, understand the root cause, align on corrective actions, and agree on a recovery plan to restore customer confidence.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Review the escalation details.
- Understand the customer impact.
- Identify the root cause.
- Agree on corrective and preventive actions.
- Define communication commitments.
- Assign ownership and timelines.

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

An Escalation Review Meeting should:

- Focus on facts rather than blame.
- Acknowledge the customer's experience.
- Present a clear recovery plan.
- Assign ownership for every action.
- Commit only to achievable timelines.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Becoming defensive.
- Assigning blame during the meeting.
- Promising unrealistic timelines.
- Leaving actions without owners.
- Ending the meeting without confirming next updates.

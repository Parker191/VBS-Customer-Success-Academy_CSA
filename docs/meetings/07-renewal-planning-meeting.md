# 📌 Purpose

Review the value delivered throughout the partnership, discuss future business needs, and prepare for a successful renewal.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Review partnership achievements.
- Discuss customer satisfaction.
- Understand future business plans.
- Identify renewal opportunities.
- Agree on next steps.
- Assign follow-up actions.

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A Renewal Planning Meeting should:

- Focus on customer outcomes before commercial discussions.
- Address concerns early.
- Highlight measurable value delivered.
- Explore future business goals.
- End with a mutually agreed plan.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Waiting until the contract is about to expire.
- Focusing only on pricing.
- Ignoring unresolved issues.
- Not involving the right decision makers.

---

# 📌 Purpose

Review the previous month's performance, project progress, customer satisfaction, risks, and priorities for the upcoming month.

---

# 🎯 Meeting Objectives

By the end of the meeting:

- Review project performance.
- Discuss completed milestones.
- Review KPIs and Customer Health.
- Identify risks and mitigation plans.
- Confirm priorities for the next month.
- Agree on action items.

---

# ⏱ Duration

45–60 Minutes

---

# 💡 VBS Best Practices

A successful MBR should:

- Be data-driven.
- Celebrate achievements.
- Discuss challenges openly.
- Focus on solutions.
- End with measurable action items.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Spending the entire meeting reviewing tasks.
- Ignoring customer feedback.
- Discussing only problems.
- Leaving action items without owners.
- Failing to follow up after the meeting.

## 💬 Customer Success Insight

An effective Monthly Business Review focuses on operational excellence.

It answers three key questions:

1. What did we achieve this month?
2. What challenges do we need to address?
3. What are our priorities for next month?

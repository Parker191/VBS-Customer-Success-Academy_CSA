# 📂 Naming Convention

## Files

Use Title Case.

Examples:

Customer Health SOP.md

Quarterly Business Review.md

Invoice Management.md

---

## Numbered Files

01 - Module Name.md

02 - Module Name.md

03 - Module Name.md

---

## Folders

Use spaces instead of underscores.

Example:

Meeting Templates

Customer Playbooks

Email Templates

---

## Images

Use lowercase with hyphens.

Example:

customer-journey.png

health-score-dashboard.png

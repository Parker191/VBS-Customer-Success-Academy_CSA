# 📅 Weekly Check-in Email

## Purpose

Maintain regular communication with the customer by sharing project updates, identifying blockers, and confirming upcoming priorities.

---

## When to Use

Send once a week for active projects or as per the agreed communication cadence.

# 💡 VBS Best Practice

A weekly check-in should provide value, not just ask for updates.

Whenever possible:

- Highlight completed work.
- Share upcoming milestones.
- Call out risks early.
- Offer proactive support.
- End with a clear next step or question.

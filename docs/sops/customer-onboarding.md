# 🤝 Customer Onboarding

## 🎯 Objective

Learn how to welcome a new customer to VBS and ensure they have a smooth start.

---

## 📋 What You'll Learn

- Send a welcome email
- Schedule a kickoff meeting
- Introduce the project team
- Explain communication channels
- Define next steps

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1
Send the welcome email.

### Step 2
Schedule the kickoff meeting.

### Step 3
Introduce the Delivery Manager.

### Step 4
Discuss project scope and timelines.

### Step 5
Answer customer questions.

**Q:** What if the customer doesn't respond?

**A:** Send a follow-up email after 2–3 business days and try reaching out by phone if appropriate.

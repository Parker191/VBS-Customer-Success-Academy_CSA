# ⚡ CSM Productivity Assistant

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager organize daily responsibilities, prioritize customer-facing work, and create a practical action plan.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as an experienced Customer Success Manager helping me prioritize my work.

Date:
[Date]

Working Hours:
[Available working hours]

Customer Meetings Today:
[List meetings]

Customer Follow-Ups:
[List follow-ups]

Customer Issues:
[List active issues]

Escalations:
[List escalations]

Pending Internal Actions:
[List internal tasks]

Pending Customer Actions:
[List customer-dependent tasks]

CRM Tasks:
[List CRM updates]

Reporting Requirements:
[List reports or dashboards due]

Revenue / Renewal Activities:
[List relevant activities]

Other Tasks:
[List additional tasks]

Analyze my workload and create a prioritized action plan.

Use the following priority levels:

P0 - Critical
P1 - High
P2 - Medium
P3 - Low

Prioritize based on:

- Customer impact
- Urgency
- Business impact
- Escalation level
- Deadlines
- Dependencies
- Revenue / renewal risk

Provide:

1. Top 3 priorities
2. Full prioritized task list
3. Tasks that can be delegated
4. Tasks that can be postponed
5. Customer-facing actions
6. Internal actions
7. Quick wins
8. Suggested time blocks
9. End-of-day checklist

Do not invent deadlines.

If information is missing, identify it.

Prioritize customer impact over simply completing the largest number of tasks.

Keep the plan realistic for the available working hours.
```

---

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Situation

You have a busy CSM day with:

- A customer meeting
- Two overdue follow-ups
- One customer escalation
- CRM updates
- An invoice-related internal follow-up
- A potential expansion opportunity
- Weekly reporting

### Example Input

```text
Date:
August 10

Working Hours:
8 hours

Customer Meetings Today:
Customer A - MBR at 8 PM

Customer Follow-Ups:
Customer B - waiting for feedback
Customer C - follow-up overdue

Customer Issues:
Customer D has a delivery concern.

Escalations:
Customer D escalation is active.

Pending Internal Actions:
Follow up with delivery team regarding Customer D.

CRM Tasks:
Update Customer A meeting notes.

Reporting:
Weekly CSM report due today.

Revenue:
Customer E mentioned upcoming project requirements.

Other Tasks:
Clean up CRM notes.
```

---

# 🎯 Expected Output

The AI should recognize that the active escalation is more important than routine CRM cleanup.

For example:

### 🔴 P0 / Critical

**Customer D escalation**

- Confirm delivery status.
- Coordinate with internal team.
- Prepare customer update.
- Monitor resolution.

### 🟠 P1 / High

**Customer A MBR**

- Review customer history.
- Prepare agenda.
- Review health and open actions.

### 🟡 P2 / Medium

**Customer C follow-up**

- Send overdue follow-up.

### 🟢 P3 / Low

**CRM cleanup**

- Complete after customer-critical work.

---

# ⏱️ Suggested Time Blocking

The AI should help create realistic blocks such as:

```text
First Block
↓
Critical customer / escalation work

Second Block
↓
Meeting preparation

Third Block
↓
Customer follow-ups

Fourth Block
↓
CRM + reporting

Final Block
↓
Review + tomorrow's priorities
```

---

# 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before following the AI-generated plan:

- [ ] Critical customer issues identified.
- [ ] Escalations reviewed.
- [ ] Customer deadlines verified.
- [ ] Meeting preparation included.
- [ ] Revenue / renewal activities considered.
- [ ] Internal dependencies identified.
- [ ] Available working hours considered.
- [ ] Plan is realistic.
- [ ] CRM updates included.
- [ ] End-of-day review scheduled.

---

# ⚠️ Important

Productivity should not mean simply completing more tasks.

The goal is to spend CSM time on the activities that create the greatest **customer and business impact**.

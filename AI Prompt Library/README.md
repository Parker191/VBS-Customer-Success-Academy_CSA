# 🤖 AI Prompt Library

> A practical AI toolkit for Customer Success Managers at Virtual Building Studio (VBS).

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The AI Prompt Library provides structured, reusable prompts that help Customer Success Managers work faster, communicate better, analyze customer information, and make better decisions.

The goal is **not to replace CSM judgment**.

AI should support the CSM by reducing repetitive work and improving the quality of preparation.

# ⚠️ Important Rule

Never provide AI with confidential customer information, passwords, financial credentials, personal information, or other sensitive company data unless the company's approved AI policy explicitly permits it.

When necessary, anonymize customer information before using a prompt.

Example:

❌ `John Smith from ABC Architects has an overdue invoice #4280.`

✅ `A customer has an overdue invoice of $3,200.`

---

# 🧠 Prompt Structure

Most prompts in this library follow this structure:

**Role → Context → Task → Constraints → Output Format**

Example:

```text
Role:
Act as a Customer Success Manager.

Context:
A customer has raised concerns about project delays.

Task:
Analyze the situation and suggest a response strategy.

Constraints:
Keep the response professional and customer-focused.

Output:
Provide:
1. Risk level
2. Recommended action
3. Customer communication
4. Internal follow-up
```

# 🏆 AI Principles for CSMs

### 1. AI assists — CSM decides

AI recommendations should be reviewed before being used.

### 2. Context improves output

Better context generally produces better results.

### 3. Protect customer information

Never expose confidential information unnecessarily.

### 4. Verify important information

AI-generated content can contain mistakes.

### 5. Maintain the human relationship

Customer Success is ultimately about people and relationships.

# 🔄 Renewal Readiness & Risk Analyzer

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager assess customer renewal readiness, identify renewal risks, evaluate customer value, and create a practical action plan before a renewal discussion.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager responsible for customer retention and renewal readiness.

Analyze the following customer information.

Customer:
[Customer name or anonymized description]

Renewal Date:
[Date]

Current Services:
[List services currently being used]

Current Relationship:
[Brief relationship summary]

Customer Health:
[Green / Yellow / Red / Unknown]

Customer Goals:
[Known customer goals]

Value Delivered:
[Key outcomes, completed projects, improvements, or benefits]

Customer Feedback:
[Recent feedback]

Engagement:
[Meeting frequency, responsiveness, stakeholder engagement]

Delivery Performance:
[Quality, timeliness, issues, improvements]

Open Issues:
[List unresolved issues]

Escalations:
[List active or recent escalations]

Financial Status:
[Payment / invoice status if relevant]

Stakeholder Relationships:
[Key stakeholders and relationship strength]

Usage / Adoption:
[Service usage or engagement level]

Recent Wins:
[List positive developments]

Known Concerns:
[List customer concerns]

Competitive / Alternative Risk:
[Known information, if any]

Expansion Opportunities:
[Known potential future requirements]

Analyze the account and provide:

1. Renewal Readiness
   Ready / Partially Ready / Not Ready / Unknown

2. Renewal Confidence
   High / Medium / Low

3. Overall Renewal Risk
   Low / Medium / High / Critical

4. Customer Value Delivered
   Summarize the value demonstrated so far.

5. Positive Renewal Signals
   Identify evidence supporting renewal.

6. Renewal Risk Signals
   Identify evidence that could threaten renewal.

7. Unresolved Issues
   Identify anything that should be addressed before renewal.

8. Stakeholder Risk
   Identify whether there are relationship or stakeholder gaps.

9. Value Gaps
   Identify areas where customer value has not been clearly demonstrated.

10. Recommended Actions
    Provide specific actions the CSM should take.

11. Renewal Preparation Questions
    Provide questions the CSM should ask the customer.

12. Executive / Internal Support Required
    Identify where leadership or other internal teams may need to help.

13. Recommended Timeline
    Suggest when the CSM should complete key renewal activities based on the renewal date.

14. Renewal Readiness Checklist
    Provide a final checklist of items that should be completed before the renewal discussion.

Rules:

- Do not assume the customer will renew.
- Do not invent customer sentiment.
- Do not treat an absence of information as a positive signal.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed facts, risks, assumptions, and missing information.
- Prioritize customer value and relationship health.
- Do not recommend discounting as the default solution.
- Do not create commercial commitments.
```

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Situation

A customer has a renewal coming up.

```text
Renewal Date:
October 1

Customer Health:
Yellow

Value Delivered:
Several BIM projects completed successfully.

Customer Feedback:
Positive about quality but concerned about delivery visibility.

Engagement:
Customer attends monthly meetings but responds slowly to some follow-ups.

Delivery:
Quality is strong. Two recent timeline issues occurred.

Open Issues:
Delivery communication needs improvement.

Recent Wins:
Customer approved another project.

Expansion:
Customer mentioned possible additional projects later in the year.
```

### Expected Analysis

The AI should **not** simply say:

> "Customer will renew."

Instead, it should identify:

### Positive Signals

- Customer continues using VBS.
- Quality is viewed positively.
- Another project was approved.
- Potential future requirements exist.

### Renewal Risks

- Customer health is Yellow.
- Delivery visibility remains a concern.
- Some communication is slower than expected.

### Recommended Actions

1. Address delivery visibility.
2. Demonstrate value delivered.
3. Confirm upcoming customer priorities.
4. Strengthen stakeholder engagement.
5. Discuss future requirements.
6. Monitor health before renewal.

Use:

```text
Customer Value
      +

      +
Stakeholder Engagement
      +

      ↓
Renewal Readiness
```

---

# 📅 Renewal Preparation Timeline

The AI should help the CSM work backward from the renewal date.

Example:

```text
90+ Days Before
      ↓
Understand goals + health + value

60–90 Days Before
      ↓
Address risks + strengthen relationships

30–60 Days Before
      ↓
Demonstrate value + confirm future needs

0–30 Days Before
      ↓
Confirm renewal requirements + remove blockers

Renewal
      ↓
Finalize agreement
```

The exact timing should be adjusted to VBS's actual renewal process.

---

# 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before considering an account renewal-ready:

- [ ] Customer value is documented.
- [ ] Customer goals are understood.
- [ ] Customer health is current.
- [ ] Major risks are identified.
- [ ] Open issues have owners.
- [ ] Stakeholder relationships are strong.
- [ ] Recent customer feedback has been reviewed.
- [ ] Delivery performance is understood.
- [ ] Renewal requirements are known.
- [ ] Future customer needs are understood.
- [ ] Internal stakeholders are aligned.
- [ ] CRM is updated.

---

# ⚠️ Important

Renewal readiness is **not the same as renewal certainty**.

A customer may look healthy today but still have:

- Budget changes
- Leadership changes
- New projects ending
- Competitive alternatives
- Changing business priorities

The CSM should continuously validate renewal assumptions with the customer.

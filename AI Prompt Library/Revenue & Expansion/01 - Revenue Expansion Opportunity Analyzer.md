# 💰 Revenue & Expansion Opportunity Analyzer

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager identify potential renewal, upsell, cross-sell, and expansion opportunities from customer information and engagement signals.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager focused on customer value, retention, and sustainable account growth.

Analyze the following customer information.

Customer Context:
[Describe the customer relationship]

Current Services:
[List services currently being used]

Current Engagement:
[Meetings, communication, project activity, etc.]

Current Projects:
[List active projects]

Customer Feedback:
[Positive, neutral, negative, or mixed feedback]

Upcoming Requirements:
[List known or suspected future requirements]

Unused / Underused Services:
[List if known]

Customer Growth Signals:
[New projects, increased workload, hiring, new locations, new stakeholders, etc.]

Renewal Information:
[Renewal date or contract status if relevant]

Revenue Information:
[Current revenue or subscription information if appropriate]

Customer Goals:
[Known business or project goals]

Analyze the information and provide:

1. Customer Value Summary
2. Retention / Renewal Risk
3. Expansion Signals
4. Potential Upsell Opportunities
5. Potential Cross-Sell Opportunities
6. Potential Additional Services
7. Customer Need Behind Each Opportunity
8. Recommended CSM Action
9. Recommended Timing
10. Suggested Customer Conversation
11. Opportunity Confidence:
   Low / Medium / High

Do not create an opportunity simply because one is possible.

Only identify opportunities supported by the information provided.

Clearly distinguish between:
- Confirmed customer needs
- Strong signals
- Possible opportunities
- Missing information

Prioritize customer value before revenue.

Do not use aggressive sales language.
```

---

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Situation

A customer currently uses VBS for BIM modeling.

They mention that:

- Their project pipeline is increasing.
- They expect additional projects next month.
- Their existing team may not have enough capacity.
- They are satisfied with the current service.

### Input

```text
Customer Context:
Existing VBS customer.

Current Services:
BIM modeling support.

Current Engagement:
Regular communication with the CSM.

Current Projects:
Two active projects.

Customer Feedback:
Positive.

Upcoming Requirements:
Customer mentioned three potential projects next month.

Unused / Underused Services:
No known unused services.

Customer Growth Signals:
Customer expects a higher project workload.

Renewal Information:
Customer relationship is ongoing.

Customer Goals:
Increase project capacity without significantly increasing internal headcount.
```

---

# 🎯 Expected Analysis

### Customer Need

The customer may need additional delivery capacity.

### Expansion Signal

The customer has indicated an expected increase in project workload.

### Potential Opportunity

Additional BIM modeling capacity or a dedicated resource arrangement may be relevant.

### Confidence

**High**, because the customer directly indicated upcoming requirements.

### Recommended CSM Action

Do not immediately sell.

Instead:

1. Confirm expected project volume.
2. Understand timelines.
3. Identify required skill sets.
4. Understand expected duration.
5. Discuss suitable delivery options if there is a confirmed need.

---

# 💬 Suggested Customer Conversation

The AI might suggest:

> "You mentioned that you may have several projects coming up next month. I'd love to understand the expected volume and timelines so we can make sure we have the right capacity available to support you."

The CSM can then explore the requirement naturally.

---

# 🚦 Opportunity Qualification

Use this framework:

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Confirmed Need | Customer explicitly stated a requirement |
| 🟢 Strong Signal | Multiple indicators suggest a likely need |
| 🟡 Possible Opportunity | Potential need but insufficient evidence |
| ⚪ Unknown | More information required |
| 🔴 Poor Fit | Opportunity does not align with customer need |

---

# 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before pursuing an opportunity:

- [ ] Customer need is understood.
- [ ] Opportunity is supported by evidence.
- [ ] Customer is receiving expected value.
- [ ] Current issues are addressed.
- [ ] Timing is appropriate.
- [ ] Relevant internal team is informed.
- [ ] Commercial discussion is appropriate.
- [ ] CRM opportunity is updated if required.

---

# ⚠️ Important

Customer Success should not manufacture opportunities simply to meet a revenue target.

The correct sequence is:

```text
Customer Need
      ↓
Customer Value
      ↓
Solution Fit
      ↓
Customer Conversation
      ↓
Opportunity
      ↓
Commercial Discussion
```

**Customer value comes first.**

# ✉️ Customer Communication Quality Reviewer

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager review customer-facing communication before sending it, ensuring that the message is clear, professional, empathetic, customer-focused, and action-oriented.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager and Customer Communication Reviewer.

Review the following customer-facing communication.

Customer Context:
[Briefly describe the customer situation]

Communication Purpose:
[Follow-up / Update / Escalation / Invoice / Meeting / Renewal / Feedback / Other]

Customer Relationship:
[Brief relationship context]

Draft Communication:
[Paste the email or message here]

Review the communication across the following areas:

1. Clarity
2. Professionalism
3. Customer Focus
4. Empathy
5. Tone
6. Conciseness
7. Accountability
8. Call to Action
9. Potential Customer Risk
10. Overall Effectiveness

Provide:

### 1. Overall Score
Rate the communication from 0–100.

### 2. What Works
Identify the strongest parts.

### 3. What Could Be Improved
Identify unclear, unnecessary, weak, or potentially problematic wording.

### 4. Tone Assessment
Describe the tone:
Professional / Friendly / Empathetic / Defensive / Aggressive / Passive / Unclear

### 5. Customer Risk
Identify any wording that could:
- Create confusion
- Sound defensive
- Shift blame
- Create an unintended commitment
- Damage trust
- Escalate the situation unnecessarily

### 6. Recommended Improvements
Provide specific improvements.

### 7. Improved Version
Rewrite the communication while preserving its original purpose.

### 8. Recommended Subject Line
If the communication is an email, suggest an appropriate subject.

### 9. Final Pre-Send Checklist
Provide a short checklist of things the CSM should verify.

Rules:

- Do not change the intended purpose.
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not create commitments that were not approved.
- Do not blame internal teams or individuals.
- Do not use overly sales-focused language.
- Keep the customer relationship and business context in mind.
- Preserve important factual information.
- Prefer clear and natural language over corporate jargon.
```

---

# 🧪 Example

### Draft

```text
Hi John,

The delivery was delayed because our team was busy with other projects.

We will try to deliver it as soon as possible.

Please let us know if this is a problem.

Thanks.
```

### The reviewer should identify:

❌ "Our team was busy with other projects" may sound like the customer was not a priority.

❌ "We will try" creates uncertainty.

❌ "Let us know if this is a problem" puts the burden back on the customer.

### Better direction

The communication should:

- Acknowledge the delay.
- Take ownership of the communication gap.
- Provide a confirmed timeline if available.
- Explain the next action.
- Offer a clear path for discussion.

# 🎯 Communication Quality Framework

Use:

```text
FACTS
  ↓
CUSTOMER IMPACT
  ↓
OWNERSHIP
  ↓
ACTION
  ↓
TIMELINE
  ↓
CALL TO ACTION
```

For example:

> **What happened → How it affects the customer → What we're doing → When → What we need from the customer**

---

# 📋 CSM Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending an important customer message:

- [ ] Facts verified.
- [ ] Customer impact understood.
- [ ] Tone is appropriate.
- [ ] No blame assigned.
- [ ] No unsupported commitments.
- [ ] Timeline is accurate.
- [ ] Call to action is clear.
- [ ] Customer knows what happens next.
- [ ] Message is concise.
- [ ] Sensitive information has been reviewed.

---

# ⚠️ Important

AI review does **not** replace CSM judgment.

For sensitive situations such as:

- Major escalations
- Commercial disputes
- Contract issues
- Legal matters
- Executive complaints

the CSM should follow the appropriate VBS escalation and approval process before sending communication.

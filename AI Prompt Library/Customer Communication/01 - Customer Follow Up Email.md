# 📧 Customer Follow-Up Email Generator

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager create a professional, concise, and customer-focused follow-up email after a customer interaction.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as an experienced Customer Success Manager.

I need to write a follow-up email to a customer after a recent interaction.

Customer Context:
[Briefly describe the customer situation]

Previous Discussion:
[Summarize what was discussed]

Customer Concerns:
[List any concerns or issues]

Agreed Actions:
[List the agreed actions]

Next Steps:
[List the next steps]

Deadline or Important Date:
[Add date if applicable]

Tone:
Professional, warm, concise, proactive, and customer-focused.

Please create:

1. A clear email subject line.
2. A concise opening.
3. A summary of the discussion.
4. The agreed action items.
5. Next steps and ownership.
6. A clear closing.

Do not invent information that was not provided.

Keep the email natural and avoid overly formal or robotic language.
```

---

## Example Use Case

### Situation

A customer discussed project delays during a meeting.

### Input

```text
Customer Context:
The customer is concerned about delays in the current BIM project.

Previous Discussion:
We discussed the delayed deliverables and agreed that the delivery team would provide an updated timeline.

Customer Concerns:
The customer needs better visibility into delivery dates.

Agreed Actions:
Delivery team will share the revised timeline.

Next Steps:
CSM will follow up with the customer after receiving the timeline.

Deadline:
Friday.
```

### Expected Output

The AI should produce a professional follow-up email that:

- Acknowledges the customer's concern.
- Summarizes the discussion.
- Clearly identifies the agreed action.
- Sets expectations for the next update.
- Maintains a positive customer relationship.

---

## 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before sending an AI-generated email:

- [ ] Facts are accurate.
- [ ] Customer name is correct.
- [ ] Dates are correct.
- [ ] Commitments are realistic.
- [ ] Tone sounds natural.
- [ ] No confidential information was unnecessarily included.
- [ ] CSM reviewed the final email.

---

## 💡 Best Practice

Never send an AI-generated customer email without reviewing it first.

AI can help you **write faster**, but the CSM remains responsible for the final communication.

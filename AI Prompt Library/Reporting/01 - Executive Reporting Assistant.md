# 📊 Executive Reporting Assistant

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager convert customer activity, KPI data, risks, and achievements into a concise leadership-ready report.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager preparing a weekly executive update.

Reporting Period:
[Week / Month]

Portfolio:
[Number of accounts managed]

Customer Activity:
[Meetings, check-ins, follow-ups, emails, etc.]

Customer Health:
[Number of Green / Yellow / Red accounts]

At-Risk Customers:
[List relevant customers using anonymized names where appropriate]

Key Customer Issues:
[List important issues]

Escalations:
[List active or recently resolved escalations]

Delivery Updates:
[Important project or delivery updates]

Financial Updates:
[Invoice/payment information if relevant]

Revenue Opportunities:
[Upsell, cross-sell, renewal, or expansion opportunities]

Customer Wins:
[List positive outcomes]

Completed Actions:
[List important actions completed]

Pending Actions:
[List outstanding actions]

KPI Performance:
[Provide relevant KPI results]

Prepare an executive summary using this structure:

1. Executive Summary
2. Key Wins
3. Customer Health
4. Major Risks
5. Escalations
6. Delivery Updates
7. Revenue Opportunities
8. KPI Performance
9. Pending Actions
10. Priorities for Next Period
11. Leadership Support Required

Keep the report concise and focused on business impact.

Do not simply repeat the information provided.

Identify trends and patterns where supported by the data.

Do not invent information.

Clearly distinguish:
- Confirmed information
- Observed trends
- Potential risks
- Recommended actions

Use professional executive language.
```

---

## Example Use Case

### Input

```text
Reporting Period:
August 3–7

Portfolio:
24 accounts

Customer Activity:
5 customer meetings
15 follow-up emails
8 proactive check-ins

Customer Health:
17 Green
5 Yellow
2 Red

At-Risk Customers:
Customer A
Customer B

Escalations:
One delivery escalation currently in progress.

Customer Wins:
Customer C approved the next project phase.

Revenue Opportunities:
Customer D indicated potential additional project requirements.

KPI Performance:
Client meetings exceeded target.
Proactive check-ins below target.
```

---

## Expected Output

The AI should turn the raw information into something like:

### Executive Summary

Customer engagement remained positive during the week, with meeting activity exceeding target. Two customers require closer monitoring due to health risks, while one delivery escalation remains active.

### Key Wins

- Customer C progressed to the next project phase.
- Meeting activity exceeded the weekly target.
- Potential expansion opportunity identified with Customer D.

### Major Risks

- Two customers are currently Red/At Risk.
- Proactive check-in activity remains below target.
- One delivery escalation requires monitoring.

### Next Priorities

1. Engage Red accounts.
2. Drive proactive customer check-ins.
3. Monitor the active escalation.
4. Follow up on the expansion opportunity.

---

# 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before sharing the report:

- [ ] Verify KPI numbers.
- [ ] Verify customer health counts.
- [ ] Confirm escalation status.
- [ ] Validate revenue opportunities.
- [ ] Remove confidential information where unnecessary.
- [ ] Confirm trends are supported by actual data.
- [ ] Review recommendations.
- [ ] Ensure leadership asks are clearly stated.

---

# ⚠️ Important

AI should help summarize information, but the CSM remains responsible for the accuracy of the final report.

Never allow AI to create or assume KPI results that were not provided.

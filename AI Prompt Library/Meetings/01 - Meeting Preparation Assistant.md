# 📅 Meeting Preparation Assistant

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager prepare for an upcoming customer meeting by organizing available information into a practical meeting plan.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as an experienced Customer Success Manager preparing for a customer meeting.

Meeting Type:
[Kickoff / WBR / MBR / QBR / EBR / Check-in / Renewal / Escalation]

Customer:
[Customer name or anonymized description]

Meeting Objective:
[What do we need to accomplish?]

Customer Background:
[Brief customer context]

Recent Communication:
[Summarize recent emails, calls, or discussions]

Current Project Status:
[Project / delivery status]

Customer Health:
[Green / Yellow / Red / Unknown]

Recent Customer Concerns:
[List concerns]

Open Action Items:
[List outstanding actions]

Recent Wins:
[List positive developments]

Business / Revenue Context:
[Renewal, expansion, invoice, or other relevant context]

Stakeholders Attending:
[List customer and internal stakeholders]

Meeting Date:
[Date]

Prepare the meeting using the following structure:

1. Meeting Objective
2. Customer Situation Summary
3. Key Topics to Discuss
4. Customer Concerns
5. Recent Wins
6. Open Action Items
7. Questions the CSM Should Ask
8. Risks to Monitor
9. Potential Opportunities
10. Recommended Meeting Agenda
11. Desired Outcomes
12. Post-Meeting Actions

For every recommendation:

- Use only the information provided.
- Do not invent customer facts.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Prioritize customer value.
- Keep the recommendations practical.

Also provide a final section:

"CSM Pre-Meeting Checklist"

Include the most important things the CSM should verify before entering the meeting.
```

---

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Situation

You have an upcoming MBR with a customer.

The customer:

- Has active BIM projects.
- Had two recent delivery delays.
- Is generally satisfied with the relationship.
- Has one outstanding action.
- Has an upcoming project that may require additional resources.

### Input

```text
Meeting Type:
MBR

Meeting Objective:
Review the previous month's performance and agree on priorities for the next month.

Customer Background:
Existing VBS customer using BIM modeling services.

Recent Communication:
Customer appreciated the recent improvement in communication.

Current Project Status:
Two active projects. One recently experienced a delivery delay.

Customer Health:
Yellow

Recent Customer Concerns:
Customer wants better visibility into delivery timelines.

Open Action Items:
Delivery team needs to provide an updated timeline.

Recent Wins:
Communication has improved.

Business / Revenue Context:
Customer may have additional project requirements next month.

Stakeholders Attending:
Customer project manager and VBS CSM.
```

---

# 🎯 Expected Output

The AI should help the CSM prepare:

### Meeting Objective

Review performance, address delivery visibility, and align on upcoming requirements.

### Key Discussion Topics

1. Current project status
2. Delivery timeline
3. Communication improvements
4. Open action item
5. Upcoming project requirements

### Questions

- Is the current delivery visibility meeting your expectations?
- Are there any upcoming project deadlines we should plan for?
- Are there additional resources you expect to need next month?

### Risk

Delivery visibility remains a potential customer-health risk.

### Opportunity

Upcoming project requirements may represent a potential expansion opportunity.

---

# 📋 CSM Pre-Meeting Checklist

Before the meeting:

- [ ] Review CRM history.
- [ ] Review previous meeting notes.
- [ ] Check open action items.
- [ ] Confirm current project status.
- [ ] Review customer health.
- [ ] Check recent customer communication.
- [ ] Review outstanding invoices if relevant.
- [ ] Identify potential risks.
- [ ] Identify potential opportunities.
- [ ] Confirm meeting objective.
- [ ] Prepare customer-specific questions.

---

# ⚠️ Important

The AI should **prepare the CSM, not conduct the relationship on the CSM's behalf**.

The CSM should validate the information before using it in a customer meeting.

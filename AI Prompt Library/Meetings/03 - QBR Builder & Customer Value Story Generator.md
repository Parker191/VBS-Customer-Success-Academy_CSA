# 📊 QBR Builder & Customer Value Story Generator

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager prepare a structured Quarterly Business Review (QBR) by turning customer performance, outcomes, feedback, risks, and future priorities into a clear customer-value story.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager preparing a Quarterly Business Review for a customer.

Customer:
[Customer name or anonymized description]

Quarter:
[Quarter / Reporting Period]

Customer Goals:
[List customer goals]

Success Criteria:
[List agreed success criteria]

Services Used:
[List VBS services]

Projects Completed:
[List completed projects]

Projects In Progress:
[List active projects]

Key Performance Data:
[Provide relevant metrics]

Delivery Performance:
[Quality, timeliness, productivity, or other relevant information]

Customer Feedback:
[Recent customer feedback]

Customer Health:
[Green / Yellow / Red / Unknown]

Major Issues:
[List significant issues]

Escalations:
[List escalations]

Value Delivered:
[List measurable or observable customer outcomes]

Customer Wins:
[List important successes]

Areas for Improvement:
[List areas that need improvement]

Upcoming Priorities:
[List next-quarter priorities]

Future Requirements:
[List known upcoming needs]

Expansion Opportunities:
[List potential opportunities if relevant]

Create a QBR structure containing:

1. Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview of the quarter.

2. Customer Goals

Summarize the goals the customer wanted to achieve.

3. Progress Against Goals

Show:

| Customer Goal | Progress | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

4. Value Delivered

Identify measurable and observable value delivered during the quarter.

5. Key Performance Highlights

Summarize the most important performance indicators.

6. Customer Feedback

Summarize positive feedback, concerns, and expectations.

7. Challenges & Risks

Identify important challenges and their potential impact.

8. Actions Taken

Explain what was done to address issues or improve outcomes.

9. Lessons Learned

Identify useful lessons from the quarter.

10. Next-Quarter Priorities

Recommend priorities based on the information provided.

11. Future Opportunities

Identify potential opportunities supported by customer needs.

12. Customer Discussion Questions

Provide strategic questions to ask during the QBR.

13. Recommended QBR Agenda

Create a practical meeting agenda.

14. Closing Message

Create a concise customer-focused closing statement.

Rules:

- Do not invent metrics or outcomes.
- Do not claim value that cannot be supported by evidence.
- Clearly distinguish facts from recommendations.
- Customer value should be the central theme.
- Avoid making the QBR purely an internal performance report.
- Do not use aggressive sales language.
- Identify missing information that should be collected before the QBR.
```

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Customer Goal

Increase project delivery capacity while maintaining quality.

### Quarterly Data

```text
Projects Completed:
8

Customer Feedback:
Quality remains strong.

Delivery:
7 of 8 projects delivered on agreed timelines.

Issue:
One project experienced a delay.

Customer Health:
Green

Upcoming Priorities:
Customer expects increased project volume next quarter.
```

### Expected QBR Story

The AI should help the CSM tell a story like:

```text
Customer Goal
      ↓
Increase delivery capacity
      ↓
8 projects completed
      ↓
Strong quality maintained
      ↓
7/8 delivered on agreed timelines
      ↓
One delivery issue identified
      ↓
Improvement opportunity
      ↓
Higher expected volume next quarter
      ↓
Plan capacity + communication
```

The important part is that **the numbers come from the CSM's data**.

The AI should not invent them.

# 🎯 QBR Value Story Framework

Use this structure:

### 1. Where We Started

What were the customer's goals?

### 2. What We Delivered

What work was completed?

### 3. What Changed

What measurable or observable improvement occurred?

### 4. What We Learned

What worked and what needs improvement?

### 5. Where We're Going

What should the customer and VBS focus on next?

---

# 💬 Strategic QBR Questions

The AI can generate questions such as:

- Are we supporting your highest-priority projects?
- Where are you seeing the most value from the current engagement?
- What would you like us to improve next quarter?
- Are there upcoming workload changes we should plan for?
- What challenges do you anticipate in the next quarter?
- Are there capabilities or services you expect to need?

---

# 📋 CSM QBR Checklist

Before the QBR:

- [ ] Customer goals reviewed.
- [ ] Success metrics collected.
- [ ] Project performance verified.
- [ ] Customer feedback reviewed.
- [ ] Customer health updated.
- [ ] Risks identified.
- [ ] Escalations reviewed.
- [ ] Value delivered documented.
- [ ] Next-quarter priorities identified.
- [ ] Future requirements reviewed.
- [ ] QBR questions prepared.
- [ ] Data verified.
- [ ] CRM updated.

---

# ⚠️ Important

A QBR should not become a presentation of everything the CSM did.

The focus should be:

> **Customer goals → outcomes → value → learning → future priorities**

Customer activity is supporting evidence, not the main story.

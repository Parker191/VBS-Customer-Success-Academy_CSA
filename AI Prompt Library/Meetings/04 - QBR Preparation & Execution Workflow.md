# 📊 QBR Preparation & Execution Workflow

## Purpose

Provide a structured Customer Success workflow for preparing, conducting, and following up on a Quarterly Business Review (QBR).

The objective is to help the CSM demonstrate customer value, review performance against customer goals, identify risks and improvement areas, understand future priorities, and agree on clear next steps.

A QBR should be a **customer-value conversation**, not simply a presentation of VBS activities.

1️⃣ Confirm QBR Details

Before preparing the QBR, confirm:

Customer name
QBR date
Reporting period
Meeting participants
Customer stakeholders
Internal VBS participants
Meeting objective
Previous QBR date
Current renewal status, if relevant

Possible participants may include:

Customer project manager
Customer leadership
Customer decision makers
CSM
KAM / Sales
Delivery representative
Management

Do not assume the meeting objective or participants if they have not been confirmed.

2️⃣ Review Customer Context

Review the customer's current situation.

Check:

Services being used
Active projects
Completed projects
Current engagement model
Customer goals
Success criteria
Stakeholders
Recent communications
Recent feedback
Customer health
Open issues
Escalations
Commercial context, when relevant
Renewal status
Future requirements

Focus only on information relevant to the QBR.

Do not include unnecessary customer information.

3️⃣ Review Customer Goals

Identify what the customer wanted to accomplish during the reporting period.

Examples:

Increase project capacity
Improve delivery speed
Maintain quality
Reduce workload
Support additional projects
Improve communication
Improve operational efficiency
Increase flexibility
Support upcoming workload

Create:

Customer Goal	Success Criteria	Current Progress	Evidence	Status
[Goal]	[Criteria]	[Progress]	[Evidence]	[Status]

Do not claim a goal was achieved without supporting evidence.

4️⃣ Collect Performance Data

Collect relevant performance information.

Possible data points:

Projects completed
Projects in progress
Delivery timelines
Quality performance
Rework
Productivity
Response time
Turnaround time
Milestone completion
Customer feedback
SLA performance, if applicable
Resource utilization, if relevant

Use verified data wherever possible.

Create:

Metric	Target	Actual	Status	Source
[Metric]	[Target]	[Actual]	[Status]	[Source]

If data is unavailable:

Data Not Available

Do not estimate or invent metrics.

5️⃣ Review Customer Value

Translate activity into customer value.

Review:

What work was completed?
What customer problem did the work address?
What improvement occurred?
What outcome was achieved?
What evidence supports the outcome?
What positive customer feedback was received?

Use:

Customer Need / Goal	VBS Activity	Value Delivered	Evidence	Status
[Need]	[Activity]	[Value]	[Evidence]	[Status]

Remember:

Activity ≠ Value

For example:

"8 BIM projects completed"

is an activity.

A stronger value statement would be:

"8 BIM projects were completed, supporting the customer's project workload during the quarter."

Only make stronger outcome claims when evidence supports them.

6️⃣ Review Customer Health

Review the customer's current health.

Consider:

Delivery
Quality
Engagement
Relationship
Sentiment
Commercial status
Open issues
Customer feedback
Renewal risk

Possible classifications:

🟢 Healthy

🟡 Needs Attention

🟠 At Risk

🔴 Critical

⚪ Unknown

Also review the health trend:

Improving
Stable
Declining
Unknown

If health has changed, document the evidence.

Do not classify the account based on a single metric.

7️⃣ Review Customer Feedback

Review recent customer feedback.

Identify:

Positive Feedback
What is working well?
What does the customer appreciate?
What outcomes are they satisfied with?
Concerns
What is not working?
What needs improvement?
What expectations are not being met?
Requests
What does the customer want from VBS?
What changes have they requested?

Create:

Feedback Type	Customer Feedback	Evidence	Action Required
Positive	[Feedback]	[Evidence]	[Action]
Concern	[Concern]	[Evidence]	[Action]
Request	[Request]	[Evidence]	[Action]

Do not invent customer sentiment.

8️⃣ Review Risks & Issues

Identify important risks before the QBR.

Possible risks:

Delivery concerns
Quality concerns
Communication issues
Low engagement
Customer dissatisfaction
Stakeholder changes
Renewal risk
Payment concerns
Resource concerns
Unresolved escalations

Create:

Risk / Issue	Evidence	Customer Impact	Mitigation	Owner	Status
[Risk]	[Evidence]	[Impact]	[Action]	[Owner]	[Status]

If the owner is unknown:

To be confirmed

Do not create unsupported risks.

9️⃣ Identify Future Priorities

Understand what matters to the customer next.

Review:

Upcoming projects
Expected workload
New requirements
Resource needs
Timeline changes
Business priorities
Process improvements
Potential challenges

Questions to consider:

What is changing for the customer?
What projects are coming next?
Does the customer expect higher or lower workload?
What support may be required?
What risks should VBS prepare for?

Separate:

Confirmed Future Requirements

from

Potential Requirements

Do not treat potential requirements as confirmed business.

1️⃣1️⃣ Prepare QBR Questions

Prepare strategic questions.

Customer Experience
How has the overall experience been this quarter?
What has worked particularly well?
What would you like us to improve?
Value
Where are you seeing the most value from the current engagement?
Are we supporting the areas that matter most to your team?
Is there any value we are not currently demonstrating clearly?
Delivery
Are current delivery timelines meeting your expectations?
Are there any delivery concerns we should address?
Future
What are your priorities for the next quarter?
Are there upcoming projects we should prepare for?
Do you anticipate changes in workload or resource requirements?
Relationship
Is there anything we can do to make working with VBS easier?
Are there additional stakeholders we should be engaging with?

Questions should encourage a two-way conversation.

Do not use the QBR only as a presentation.

1️⃣2️⃣ Create QBR Agenda

Create a practical customer-focused agenda.

QBR Agenda
1. Welcome & Objectives
Welcome participants
Confirm meeting objective
Confirm agenda
2. Customer Priorities

Discuss current customer priorities and changes.

3. Quarter Review

Review relevant performance and completed work.

4. Progress Against Goals

Review customer goals and supporting evidence.

5. Value Delivered

Discuss customer outcomes and demonstrated value.

6. Customer Feedback

Discuss what is working and what needs improvement.

7. Risks & Challenges

Discuss important concerns and corrective actions.

8. Future Priorities

Discuss upcoming projects and customer requirements.

9. Next Steps

Confirm actions, owners, and timelines.

1️⃣3️⃣ Conduct QBR

During the QBR:

Listen actively.
Keep the discussion customer-focused.
Ask open questions.
Present verified information.
Explain value clearly.
Acknowledge issues honestly.
Avoid defensive responses.
Capture customer feedback.
Clarify unclear points.
Confirm decisions.
Confirm next steps.

Do not turn the QBR into a sales presentation.

Potential expansion opportunities may be discussed only when relevant to customer needs.

1️⃣4️⃣ Capture Decisions & Actions

Capture all agreed actions.

Create:

Action	Owner	Deadline	Expected Outcome	Status
[Action]	[Owner]	[Deadline]	[Outcome]	Open

Separate:

Customer Actions

Actions owned by the customer.

VBS Actions

Actions owned by VBS.

Internal Actions

Actions requiring coordination between VBS teams.

Only record an owner or deadline when it has been confirmed.

If missing:

Not specified

1️⃣5️⃣ Send Follow-Up

Send a concise customer follow-up after the QBR.

The follow-up should include:

Thank you
Key discussion points
Customer feedback
Decisions
Agreed actions
Owners
Deadlines
Next steps

Example:

Subject: Thank You – QBR Discussion & Next Steps

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with us for our quarterly business review.

It was helpful to review the progress made during the quarter, discuss your feedback, and understand your priorities for the coming period.

As discussed, the key next steps are:

• [Action] – [Owner] – [Deadline]
• [Action] – [Owner] – [Deadline]

We will continue to monitor the agreed actions and keep you updated on progress.

Thank you again for your feedback and partnership.

Best regards,
[CSM Name]
VBS

The CSM must review the email before sending it.

Do not include unconfirmed commitments.

1️⃣7️⃣ Track Follow-Through

A QBR is not complete when the meeting ends.

Track agreed actions until completion.

Create:

In Progress
Blocked
Completed
Cancelled

Awaiting Internal Team

Follow up on overdue or blocked actions.

Update the customer when appropriate.

⚠️ Important Rules

AI assists — CSM decides.

Do not:

Invent customer goals.
Invent performance metrics.
Invent customer feedback.
Claim unsupported customer outcomes.
Present assumptions as facts.
Hide important risks.
Ignore unresolved issues.
Promise unapproved actions.
Create unauthorized commercial commitments.
Pressure customers into expansion.
Treat potential opportunities as confirmed revenue.
Share unnecessary confidential information.

The QBR should remain evidence-based and customer-focused.

# 🎯 Customer Success Plan Generator

## Purpose

Help a Customer Success Manager create a structured Customer Success Plan based on customer goals, desired outcomes, current services, milestones, risks, and responsibilities.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a Senior Customer Success Manager responsible for developing a measurable Customer Success Plan.

Analyze the following customer information.

Customer:
[Customer name or anonymized description]

Customer Business / Project Goals:
[List the customer's goals]

Current Challenges:
[List current challenges]

Services Used:
[List current VBS services]

Expected Outcomes:
[What does the customer want to achieve?]

Success Metrics:
[List known success metrics]

Current Performance:
[Current results or status]

Key Stakeholders:
[List customer and internal stakeholders]

Important Milestones:
[List known milestones or deadlines]

Customer Responsibilities:
[List customer responsibilities]

VBS Responsibilities:
[List VBS responsibilities]

Known Risks:
[List risks]

Dependencies:
[List dependencies]

Timeline:
[Overall timeline]

Create a structured Customer Success Plan containing:

1. Customer Objective
2. Business / Project Goals
3. Definition of Success
4. Success Metrics
5. Current State
6. Desired Future State
7. Key Milestones
8. Customer Responsibilities
9. VBS Responsibilities
10. Risks
11. Mitigation Actions
12. Dependencies
13. Communication Cadence
14. Success Review Points
15. Next 30-Day Priorities
16. Next 60-Day Priorities
17. Next 90-Day Priorities

For each success objective, create a table:

| Objective | Success Metric | Current State | Target State | Owner | Deadline | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- Do not invent customer goals or metrics.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Do not create unrealistic targets.
- Separate confirmed information from recommendations.
- Focus on measurable customer outcomes.
- Avoid making the plan purely activity-based.
- Prioritize customer value.
```

---

# 🧪 Example Use Case

### Customer Goal

A customer wants to increase BIM project capacity without significantly increasing internal headcount.

### Current State

```text
Customer currently manages two active projects.

VBS provides BIM modeling support.

Customer is satisfied with quality but wants more predictable delivery visibility.
```

### Desired Outcome

```text
Increase project delivery capacity while maintaining expected quality and timeline visibility.
```

### Example Success Metrics

| Objective | Success Metric |
|---|---|
| Increase capacity | Additional projects supported |
| Improve delivery visibility | Regular status updates |
| Maintain quality | Customer-approved deliverables |
| Improve communication | Agreed communication cadence |

# 📅 30 / 60 / 90-Day Framework

### First 30 Days

Focus on:

- Relationship alignment
- Requirements
- Initial delivery
- Success criteria
- Communication cadence

### Days 31–60

Focus on:

- Performance
- Customer feedback
- Process improvements
- Risk identification
- Adoption / utilization

### Days 61–90

Focus on:

- Outcome measurement
- Value demonstration
- Long-term planning
- Renewal readiness
- Potential expansion opportunities

---

# 🧠 CSM Review Checklist

Before sharing the Success Plan:

- [ ] Customer goals are confirmed.
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable where possible.
- [ ] Current state is accurate.
- [ ] Target state is realistic.
- [ ] Owners are correct.
- [ ] Deadlines are confirmed.
- [ ] Risks are documented.
- [ ] Customer responsibilities are included.
- [ ] VBS responsibilities are included.
- [ ] Customer agrees with the plan.

---

# ⚠️ Important

A Customer Success Plan should be **customer-centered**, not simply a list of CSM tasks.

Bad:

> CSM will send weekly emails.

Better:

> Customer will have predictable visibility into project progress through an agreed weekly reporting cadence.

The activity supports the outcome—the outcome is what matters.

# 🎓 VBS Customer Success Academy

> **A centralized Customer Success learning, process, enablement, and AI knowledge system for Virtual Building Studio (VBS).**

---

## 🏢 About the Academy

The **VBS Customer Success Academy (CSA)** is a structured internal learning and enablement platform designed to help Customer Success Managers develop the knowledge, processes, tools, and practical skills required to manage customers consistently and effectively.

The Academy brings together:

- 📚 Customer Success Training
- 📄 Standard Operating Procedures
- 📘 Customer Playbooks
- 📧 Email Templates
- 📅 Meeting Templates
- 📊 Reporting & Dashboard Frameworks
- 🤖 AI Prompt Library
- 🔄 AI-Assisted Customer Success Workflows
- 🧠 Knowledge Resources
- 🎯 Assessments & Certification
- 🗺️ Continuous Improvement Roadmap

The goal is to create a **repeatable Customer Success operating system** rather than relying only on individual experience or tribal knowledge.

---

# 🎯 Academy Objectives

The VBS Customer Success Academy is designed to help CSMs:

- Build stronger customer relationships.
- Standardize Customer Success processes.
- Improve customer onboarding.
- Monitor customer health and engagement.
- Identify customer risks earlier.
- Improve customer communication.
- Establish consistent meeting practices.
- Demonstrate customer value.
- Improve retention and renewal readiness.
- Identify relevant growth opportunities.
- Use AI responsibly to improve productivity.
- Maintain consistent documentation.
- Accelerate onboarding of new CSMs.

# 🎯 Academy Goals

By the end of this academy, every Customer Success Manager will be able to:

- Successfully onboard new customers.
- Communicate professionally across customer touchpoints.
- Manage customer information using Zoho CRM and the Internal Tracker.
- Measure and improve Customer Health Scores.
- Conduct Weekly, Monthly, and Quarterly Business Reviews.
- Manage invoices, escalations, and renewals.
- Identify growth opportunities through Upselling, Cross-selling, and Referrals.
- Use AI responsibly to improve Customer Success workflows.
- Follow standardized VBS SOPs and Playbooks.
- Manage customer relationships confidently from onboarding to long-term partnership.
